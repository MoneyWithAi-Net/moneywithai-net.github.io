Grok Bots That Use eBay

5 Grok Bot setups that connect to eBay, for resale listings and store operations. Each links to its creator's public source.

Updated 8 November 2026. Built from our listings; every bot links to its public source.

eBay is a large marketplace for resale. These bots check sold prices, draft listings and help resellers keep stock in sync.

What They Do With eBay

  • Resale listings: 4 bots
  • Store operations: 1 bot

All 5 Bots

  • Computer Shop Bot

    Takes the paperwork off a small computer repair and parts shop by pricing eBay buys and sales for profit after fees, tracking orders and quoting network installs.

    By jimaggie · Store operations · eBay, Grok · One-click Add

  • eBay Lister

    Send a photo of an item to sell: it identifies it, checks eBay sold prices, suggests a price and drafts the listing for your approval.

    By Christian Morón · Resale listings · Grok, eBay · One-click Add

  • eBay Lister

    Turns a photo of a thrift find, card or toy into an eBay draft with sold comps, a title and a price.

    By Gabi_Valerio3 · Resale listings · eBay · One-click Add

  • Snap & Flip

    Prices resale finds from real sold listings: snap a photo and get eBay sold comps, shipping picks and a ready 80-character title. It never lists for you.

    By LisaMAUSA · Resale listings · Grok, eBay · One-click Add

  • The Chief Minister Lord Of Frocks

    Prices clothing lots from sold comps, cross-lists them to eBay, Poshmark, Depop and Etsy and removes items everywhere once they sell.

    By Ignota · Resale listings · Grok, eBay · One-click Add
