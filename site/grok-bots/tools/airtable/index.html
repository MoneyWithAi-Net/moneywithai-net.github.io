Grok Bots That Use Airtable

5 Grok Bot setups that connect to Airtable, for hiring, phone answering and quotes. Each links to its creator's public source.

Airtable is a database that feels like a spreadsheet. These bots use it to store leads, quotes and job records you can filter.

All 5 Bots

  • Job to Shortlist Recruiter

    Turns a job description into a candidate shortlist from Apollo, scored and stored in Airtable.

    By Fabian Herhold · Hiring · n8n, Airtable, Claude, OpenAI

  • Listing Video Maker

    Creates real estate listing videos from Airtable records and emails them to the agent.

    By Orshot · Video production · n8n, Airtable, Gmail, OpenAI

  • Property Inquiry Scorer

    Reads real estate inquiries in Gmail, scores each lead and files it in an Airtable CRM.

    By Marth - Business Automation · Real estate leads · n8n, Airtable, Gmail, OpenAI

  • Quote Request Router

    Routes quote requests from a Tally form into Airtable, alerts Slack and emails the client.

    By Baptiste Fort · Quotes · n8n, Airtable, Gmail, Slack

  • Voice Receptionist Call Booker

    A voice receptionist on Vapi that takes calls and books appointments into Google Calendar and Airtable.

    By SuperAgent · Phone answering · n8n, Airtable, Google Calendar
