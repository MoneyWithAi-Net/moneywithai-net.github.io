Questions

How do you pick the best Grok Bots?

With a published editorial score out of 100: money use (40), how complete the profile is (30), visuals such as official tool logos and a flow diagram (20) and ease (10). Downloads, payments and sponsors play no part.

Can a creator pay to rank higher?

No. Sponsor spots, when we sell them, are labelled and sit outside this list. The ranking only moves when the listing data or the published scoring rules change.

Which Grok Bot is number one right now?

Case Skeleton by paulopierrondi, with a score of 100. Builds the problem, solution and result outline of an automation case study for you to fill in.

Are the money levels guaranteed?

No. Money levels are our estimate of relative earning potential, not a promise or typical result. What you earn depends on your effort, skills, market and costs.
