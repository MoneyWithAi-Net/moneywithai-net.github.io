Questions

What is a Grok Bot template?

A setup someone shared with an official x.ai/bot link. Opening the link adds a copy of their bot to your Grok account, and you then connect your own tools and change the details for your business.

How many templates can I add in one click?

130 of our 307 listings have an official Add link. The others are shared as a post or page you follow by hand.

Which tools do the templates use most?

Grok (95), Email (6), X (6), SMS (3), Treg (3). Connect the tools a template lists before its first run.

Is my data shared with the creator?

Adding a template puts its setup in your own account. Your connected tools and data stay in your account, under the terms of Grok and of each tool.
