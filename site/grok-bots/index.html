Questions

What is a Grok Bot?

A Grok Bot is a saved setup for Grok on X: instructions plus the tools it may use, such as Gmail, Google Sheets or a CRM. You add it to your account and it runs the job on request or on a schedule.

How many Grok Bots are listed here?

307 setups across 7 jobs, and 130 of them have an official one-click Add link. Each listing links to the public post or page where its creator shared it.

Who made these bots?

Independent creators. We list public facts only (name, creator, tools, category and source) and credit the creator on every listing. Creators can ask us to fix or remove a listing at any time.

Is the directory free?

Yes. Browsing, the JSON API and the MCP endpoint are free. Each bot uses your own Grok account and the tools you connect, which have their own terms.
