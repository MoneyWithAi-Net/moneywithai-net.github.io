Questions

What business can I start with Grok Bot?

The 47 ideas here are services you sell to others or creator income you earn yourself, such as Build a Pitch Pack for an SEO Agency, Run a Brand's Instagram as a Studio, Run a Local SEO Content Engine. The bots do the research and drafting; you sell and deliver.

How much can I earn?

We do not publish income figures. Each idea has a money level (Low, Medium or High) that is our estimate of relative potential, not a promise or typical result. Results depend on your effort, skills, market and costs.

Do I need to code?

No. 12 of these ideas are marked Beginner. You add the bots, connect tools such as Gmail or Google Sheets, and review what they produce.
