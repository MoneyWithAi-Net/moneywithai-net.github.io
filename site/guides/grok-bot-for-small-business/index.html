Where Grok Bot fits in a small business

Grok Bot is an AI teammate with its own computer in the cloud: a browser, files and a terminal. It can work in websites that have no API and connect to apps like Gmail, Google Calendar, Slack and Notion. Its routines run on a schedule while your laptop is closed.

That makes it good at the office work between your tools. Think reading the inbox, checking a portal, filling a sheet and drafting the reply. It launched in beta on 11 Aug 2026, so expect changes and check the docs date on anything you rely on.

The design suits a cautious owner. Bots can prepare work and stop at an approval card, and emails from routines stay as drafts until you press Send.

Jobs to avoid, or keep on a short leash

Some jobs look like good automation but carry risk a small business cannot absorb. Keep a human in each of these, or leave them out of your first month.

  • Moving money. Payments, refunds, payroll and checkout. A card saved in Vault fills in at checkout without separate approval, so do not save a card if you want to approve every payment.
  • Filings and legal commitments. Tax returns, contracts and anything signed in your name.
  • Deleting or overwriting data. An approval stops the next action, but it does not undo work already completed.
  • Auto-posting in public. Reviews, social posts and community replies should be drafts you publish.
  • Hourly routines. Frequent routines drain the weekly pool fastest.
  • Regulated customer data. If you handle health or payment data, check your obligations before a bot reads it. Grok Bot needs cloud data storage.

How to pick your first bot

  1. List the tasks you did last week that took over 30 minutes and felt repetitive.
  2. Cross out anything that moves money, signs for you or posts publicly.
  3. From what is left, pick the one where a draft saves the most time. Replies to inquiries are a common winner.
  4. Time that task by hand for one week so you have a baseline in minutes.
  5. Find a matching setup in our small business list or by job in use cases.
  6. Vet the template before import with our vetting guide, then follow the setup steps.

Cost expectations

The cheapest plan that includes Grok Bot is X Subscription Premium at $30 a month, or $300 a year. Super ($100) and Ultra ($200) give 15 and 50 times the Plus usage, against 4 times for Premium. Prices exclude tax.

All usage comes from one weekly pool shared with Grok chat and Cursor. Past the pool, you pay from prepaid credit at API list prices. Set a monthly spending cap, because none applies if you leave it unset.

Example: a solo owner on Premium plus Google Workspace Business Starter at $7 a month (annual) pays about $37 a month in tools. Full breakdowns are in our pricing guide.

Security basics for an owner

Every bot on your account shares one cloud computer, with the same files, browser sessions and saved logins. xAI's docs say plainly not to treat separate bots as a security boundary. Deleting a bot does not remove files or sign-ins from that computer.

  • Least access: connect only the apps a job needs, use scoped service accounts where you can, and start read-only.
  • Approval gates: add Ask first rules for sends, payments, deletions and public posts (saved cards bypass this, so do not save one).
  • Clean up: sign out of sites you no longer need and remove sensitive files from the workspace.
  • Go deeper with our security risks guide and the security reviews hub.

Start small, then add the next bot

One bot doing one draft job well beats five bots you do not trust. Add a second only when the first runs a full week with drafts you would send as written.

Questions

Is Grok Bot worth it for a small business?

It can be if you have repetitive draft work, such as replies, follow-ups or invoice prep, and someone to approve it. At $30 a month for Premium, it pays off only if your review time is shorter than doing the job by hand.

Can Grok Bot answer customers on its own?

It can, but we do not recommend it at first. Emails from routines stay as drafts until you press Send, and you can add Ask first rules so every customer message needs your OK.

How many Grok Bots should a small business start with?

One. xAI's docs set no fixed limit, but all bots share one computer and one weekly usage pool. Add the next bot after the first runs a clean week.

Which plan does a small business need for Grok Bot?

Start on X Subscription Premium at $30 a month. Move to Super ($100) only if the usage page shows you hitting the weekly limit before it resets.

Is my business data safe with Grok Bot?

Logins in Vault are filled in without being shown to the model, but all your bots share the same computer and sessions. Connect only what each job needs and gate risky actions with approvals.
