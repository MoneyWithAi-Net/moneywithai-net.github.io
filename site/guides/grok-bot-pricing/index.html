Grok Bot Pricing and Plans: What a Small Business Pays

Grok Bot is included in The X Subscription Premium ($30 a month), Super ($100) and Ultra ($200) plans. Free and Plus do not include it. Usage comes from one weekly pool shared with Grok and Cursor, and extra usage is paid from prepaid credit.

Which plans include Grok Bot

Since October 2026, Grok Bot is sold inside The X Subscription, one bill that covers Grok, Cursor and X. xAI's plan page lists Grok Bot on Premium, Super and Ultra, and not on Free or Plus. Prices below exclude sales tax.

The usage column compares each plan with Plus. xAI does not publish the size of the Plus pool in hours or tasks, so the multipliers tell you relative headroom only.

  • Team seats: Premium $30, Super $100 and Ultra $200 per seat a month. Plus is not offered for teams.
  • Older Cursor plans: Pro ($20), Pro+ ($60), Ultra ($200), Teams Standard ($40 per user) and Teams Premium ($120 per user) still include Grok Bot. Cursor no longer sells these plans to new customers.
  • Linked Grok plans: an individual SuperGrok, SuperGrok Plus, SuperGrok Heavy or X Premium+ subscription can be linked to a Cursor account. SuperGrok Lite cannot. Current SuperGrok prices: not confirmed as of 15 Nov 2026.

How the weekly usage pool works

Each paid seat gets one weekly pool. Grok Bot, the Grok apps and Grok models in Cursor all draw from it, at rates that differ by product. Your usage page at grok.com/dashboard shows the next reset date.

xAI does not publish a fixed number of bot runs, hours or tokens per week. The only firm figures are the multipliers: Premium has 4 times the Plus pool, Super 15 times and Ultra 50 times.

This changed after launch. In August 2026 xAI said bot usage was separate from Grok and Cursor usage; the October 2026 docs say it now shares the pool. If you read an older review, check its date.

  • On team plans, each seat keeps its own weekly pool. Seats do not pool usage, but they share one prepaid balance for extra usage.
  • Routines are the biggest cost driver. Frequent routines drain the weekly pool fastest.
  • On a legacy Cursor plan with a linked SuperGrok, the two do not add up. Grok Bot uses whichever grants more weekly usage.

What happens when the pool runs out

After the weekly pool is spent, further use becomes Additional Usage. It is paid from prepaid credit that you buy as top-ups. xAI's launch post for The X Subscription says this is billed at API list prices.

The per-task cost of a top-up is not published in the docs we checked. Treat any "cost per bot run" figure you see elsewhere as not confirmed as of 15 Nov 2026.

  • Auto Top-up is off by default. It only runs after you turn it on in grok.com/dashboard.
  • Set a monthly spending cap. If you leave it unset, there is no cap on paid extra usage.
  • Adding credit does not raise the cap. Extra usage can stop even when credit remains, once the cap is reached.
  • Third-party models in Cursor are never in the pool. They spend credit from the first use.

What is verified and what is not

  • Verified on 15 Nov 2026: plan names, prices and which plans include Grok Bot. Also the usage multipliers, the shared weekly pool, prepaid top-ups, the spending cap and the trial terms.
  • Not confirmed as of 15 Nov 2026: the absolute size of any weekly pool and the dollar cost of a typical bot run. Top-up bundle sizes and current SuperGrok prices are also unconfirmed.

  • Plan figures come from xAI's docs, x.ai/pricing and Cursor's help center.

How to keep the bill predictable

  1. Start on Premium with one bot and one job. Move up only if the usage page shows you hitting the limit before the reset.
  2. Set a monthly spending cap in grok.com/dashboard before you add any routine, and leave Auto Top-up off for the first month.
  3. Schedule routines daily or a few times a day, not hourly. Routines must be at least 5 minutes apart, but that is a floor, not a target.
  4. Check the usage page each Friday for the first 4 weeks and write down what each routine costs you in pool share.

Questions

Is Grok Bot free?

No. The Free and Plus plans do not include it. New users can get a trial usage credit with a 7-day window, and it never turns into a paid plan.

How much does Grok Bot cost per month?

The cheapest plan that includes it is X Subscription Premium at $30 a month, or $300 a year. Super is $100 and Ultra is $200, with larger weekly pools. Prices exclude tax.

Does SuperGrok include Grok Bot?

An individual SuperGrok, SuperGrok Plus or SuperGrok Heavy subscription can be linked to a Cursor account to use Grok Bot. SuperGrok Lite cannot be linked. A link is permanent once created.

Do I need a Cursor subscription to use Grok Bot?

No separate Cursor plan is needed on The X Subscription, which already bundles Cursor. Grok Bot signs you in with a Cursor account, but Premium, Super or Ultra covers the usage.

What happens if my bots use up the weekly pool?

Further work is paid from prepaid top-up credit at API list prices. If you have no credit, or you hit your monthly spending cap, extra usage stops until the next reset.

Can a small team share one Grok Bot plan?

Each paid seat has its own weekly pool, and seats do not combine usage. Team seats cost the same as individual plans, and the team shares one prepaid balance for extra usage.
