Grok Bot vs Lindy: Which AI Teammate Fits a Small Business

Pick Grok Bot for a flat plan from $30 a month and a bot that works in its own cloud browser. Pick Lindy if your team lives in Slack and email and wants 1,500+ ready integrations, billed per seat plus credits. Either way, you set the approval rules.

The short answer

Both products are AI teammates that take tasks in plain language, connect to your apps and run recurring work. They differ in how you pay, where you talk to them and how approvals work.

Grok Bot suits an owner who wants a predictable flat bill and work done inside websites. Lindy suits a team that already runs on Slack and wants a shared assistant there. Neither is safe to run without approval rules you set yourself.

Pricing model compared

Grok Bot comes with X Subscription Premium, Super and Ultra. Usage draws from one weekly pool shared with Grok chat and Cursor, and extra usage is paid from prepaid credit at API list prices.

Lindy is priced per person. Each seat adds credits to one team pool, and credits cost $0.01 each on base plans. Monthly credits reset each cycle and do not roll over, while bought top-ups stay until used.

Lindy's docs name the Plus, Pro, Max and Enterprise plans but send you to the in-app billing page for prices. Its public pricing page did not load for us, so the seat prices below come from third-party sites and conflict.

Setup effort

Grok Bot starts with a desktop app for macOS, Windows or Linux, plus phone apps. You sign in, describe a task and hand it logins through a Vault or Secure Form. Our setup guide walks through the 8 steps.

Lindy starts from your accounts. You connect Google or Microsoft email and calendar, Slack and optionally iMessage or SMS, then give it tasks there. New Slack installations require a paid Slack plan, which is $7.25 per user a month on Pro billed annually.

  • Grok Bot needs more care up front because its computer can reach every login you give it.
  • Lindy needs more care around seats: anyone who mentions it in Slack can take a billed seat after a 7-day trial. Admins are emailed and can remove the seat.

Integrations and how each reaches your tools

Lindy lists 1,500+ integrations plus any MCP server, connected by OAuth or API key. When a tool has no integration, it can operate a browser or write its own API connection. Computer use is on Pro and Max only, per Lindy's billing docs.

Grok Bot connects to apps such as Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Slack and Notion, and supports any MCP or API. Its main strength is the cloud computer: a full browser, files and terminal that work in sites without an API. Routines can also start from Slack, GitHub, email and webhook events.

Approval controls

This is the biggest practical difference for a small business. Both can ask before acting, but the scope of that check differs.

  • Grok Bot caveat: all your bots share one cloud computer, files and logged-in sessions. A bot is not a security boundary, so gate risky actions with Ask first rules.
  • Lindy caveat: a team connection uses the connected account's permissions for everyone. Use least-access accounts for shared connections.

Where each wins

  • Grok Bot wins on a predictable flat price for a solo owner. It is also stronger for work inside websites and portals with no API, and its approvals apply in every chat.
  • Grok Bot also wins if you already pay for SuperGrok or a Cursor plan, since those can include it.
  • Lindy wins for a team that lives in Slack, for the size of its ready integration library, and for phone calls.
  • Lindy also wins on per-person credit caps, which help when several staff share one assistant.
  • Tie: both offer a 7-day free credit trial, routines on a schedule and MCP support.

How to decide

  1. Write down the one job you want done first and where your team talks today: Slack, email or nowhere in particular.
  2. If the job lives inside websites or portals, trial Grok Bot. If it lives in Slack threads and your CRM, trial Lindy.
  3. Run the same job on the 7-day trial with approvals on, and count drafts you would send as written.
  4. Compare the monthly cost at your real volume, not the entry price.

Questions

Is Lindy cheaper than Grok Bot?

For one person they start close: Grok Bot Premium is $30 a month, and third parties report Lindy entry seats from $29.99 to $49.99. Lindy's own prices were not confirmed as of 15 Nov 2026.

Does Grok Bot work in Slack like Lindy?

Yes. xAI's docs list desktop, mobile, Slack and X as places to work with Grok Bot, and Slack events can start routines. Lindy is built more around Slack threads.

Which has better approval controls?

Grok Bot applies Auto Review and Ask first rules in chats. Routines need a stop point written into the instruction, and their approval cards expire after 30 minutes. Lindy's guardrails apply only in shared Slack threads, not in DMs or its web app.

Can Lindy make phone calls?

Yes. Lindy's docs say calls use credits from 25 credits a minute, and the phone number has its own monthly fee. Grok Bot's docs do not list phone calling as a feature.

Do Grok Bot and Lindy have free trials?

Both give new users free usage credit with a 7-day window. Lindy needs no card for it, and Grok Bot's trial credit never turns into a paid plan.
