The first message

Internal note to your team: For the next two weeks, every new ticket arrives with an investigation note and a draft reply. Refunds are prepared but nothing is paid without your approval. Please mark each draft used, edited or rejected so we can judge it fairly. {your name}
Then
Run Support Ticket Investigator on those 10 closed tickets first and compare its notes with what really happened before you turn it on for new tickets.
  • Start on tickets already closed, then move to new ones.
  • Every refund and account change needs a person.

Weekly Cadence

  1. MondayYouApprove or reject queued refunds.The botsSupport Ticket Investigator investigates and routes new tickets.
  2. TuesdayYouReview drafts marked edited or rejected.The botsRefund Resolution Desk prepares refunds with evidence for approval.
  3. WednesdayYouApprove help article changes.The botsHelp Center Updater drafts articles from fixes and repeat questions.
  4. ThursdayYouHandle escalated tickets.The botsSupport Ticket Investigator files bugs in Linear with evidence.
  5. FridayYouRead the weekly quality report (15 minutes).The botsSupport Quality Coach scores a sample of conversations.
  6. WeekendYouOn-call person only.The botsTickets are investigated and wait for Monday.

When to Stop

Baseline first. Before you start, write down first reply time, time to resolution and tickets per person per week.

Day 30

  • More than half of drafts are rejected: fix the docs and refund rules you gave it before going further.

Day 60

  • Resolution time has not moved from the baseline: limit the bots to investigation notes only.

Day 90

  • Hours saved do not cover the tool bill: stop and keep only the help article updates.

E These thresholds are our rules of thumb, not benchmarks.

Rules to Follow

  • Refunds and consumer rightsRefund rights differ by country and by what you sell. Keep a written refund policy, apply it the same way to every customer, and keep a person approving every refund.
  • Data protection (EU and UK customers)If you hold personal data about people in the EU or UK, GDPR or UK GDPR applies: collect only what the job needs, tell people how you use it, and keep access to the connected accounts to the people who need it. Source.
  • Client confidentialityConnect only the accounts you or your client own, give each bot the least access it needs, and check client contracts for rules on where their data may be stored.
  • No income claimsNothing on this page promises income, savings or any business result. The value lines are labelled estimates with their basis. Bots draft and you approve. Source.

No. It prepares the refund with evidence. A person approves and pays every one.
