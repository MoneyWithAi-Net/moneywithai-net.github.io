The First 10

Who
10 past clients or contacts who complained about repetitive work.
Where to find them
Your client list and network.

The first message

Hi {first name}, you mentioned {task} takes hours each week. I run a short paid discovery that maps where time goes and what to automate first. Want details? If not, just reply no thanks. {your name}
Then
Run Pain in the Task on your own business first to test the interview.
  • Start with people who know you: past clients, referrals and contacts. This is not a cold list.
  • Include an easy way to say no thanks, and stop if they do.

Work out your own break-even

  • Clients needed to cover the tools = monthly tool bill / your monthly fee per client. Set your own fee; we do not publish a typical one.
  • Months to recover the setup = setup price ($1,500 founding, normally $2,500) / (monthly fees from clients you won with this - monthly tool bill).
  • Track two numbers from week 1: conversations started and clients signed. Use your own numbers, not ours.

When to Stop

Baseline first. Write down discoveries sold and builds closed a month.

Day 30

  • Interviews miss the real pain: add a live call.

Day 60

  • Discoveries do not lead to builds: fix the proposal.

Day 90

  • Fees do not cover tools and time: stop.

E These thresholds are our rules of thumb, not benchmarks.

Rules to Follow

  • Client confidentialityConnect only the accounts you or your client own, give each bot the least access it needs, and check client contracts for rules on where their data may be stored.
  • Data protection (EU and UK customers)If you hold personal data about people in the EU or UK, GDPR or UK GDPR applies: collect only what the job needs, tell people how you use it, and keep access to the connected accounts to the people who need it. Source.
