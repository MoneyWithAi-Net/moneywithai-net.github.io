The First 10

Who
10 local businesses you know that miss calls.
Where to find them
Businesses you buy from, past clients and your local business group.

The first message

Hi {first name}, I set up phone and chat receptionists that book appointments when you cannot pick up. I can show you a demo line set up for {business} in 15 minutes. If not, just reply no thanks. {your name}
Then
Set up one demo line on the Grok phone plan and call it yourself 10 times.
  • Start with people who know you: past clients, referrals and contacts. This is not a cold list.
  • Include an easy way to say no thanks, and stop if they do.

When to Stop

Baseline first. Write down missed calls a week per client before setup.

Day 30

  • Callers hang up more than they book: shorten the script.

Day 60

  • Clients cancel: improve handoff to a human.

Day 90

  • Monthly fees do not cover tools and time: stop.

E These thresholds are our rules of thumb, not benchmarks.

Rules to Follow

  • AI voices on calls (US)AI voices count as artificial voices under the TCPA. Use the voice bot for inbound calls; outbound calls with an AI voice need prior consent. Source.
  • Call recordingRecording rules differ by state and country; some require every party's consent. Tell callers if calls are recorded.
  • Calls and texts (US, TCPA)Marketing texts and automated calls to mobile numbers need the person's prior consent, and opt-out requests must be honoured. Reply to people who contacted you first; do not text cold lists. Source.
