Who It's For

Not for

The First 10

Who
Your last 10 customers whose job is finished, all of them, not only the happy ones.
Where to find them
Your booking calendar, invoice list or point-of-sale history for the last 30 days.

The first message

Hi {first name}, thank you for choosing {business} for your {service} on {day}. If you have a minute, an honest review helps other people in {town} decide: {review link}. Good or bad, we read every one and reply. Thank you, {your name}
Then
Paste your last 10 public reviews into Reply Desk and approve or edit each draft reply. That gives the bot your tone before it drafts new ones.
  • Ask every customer the same way. Do not offer discounts or gifts for reviews.
  • Do not ask only customers you expect to be happy.

Weekly Cadence

  1. MondayYouApprove weekend review replies (10 minutes). Check the call log for anything the bot could not answer.The botsReply Desk drafts replies to weekend reviews and messages. Phone Receptionist lists calls it booked or passed on.
  2. TuesdayYouSend review requests to customers from the last few days.The botsReply Desk drafts the request emails from your template.
  3. WednesdayYouApprove new reply drafts.The botsReply Desk drafts; Phone Receptionist answers and books.
  4. ThursdayYouFix one thing callers asked that the bot got wrong: update its notes on prices, hours or services.The botsPhone Receptionist uses the updated notes from the next call.
  5. FridayYouApprove drafts. Five-minute review of the week: reviews answered, calls missed, bookings made.The botsReply Desk drafts a short weekly summary.
  6. WeekendYouNothing required.The botsPhone Receptionist answers and books. Reply Desk queues drafts for Monday.

When to Stop

Baseline first. Before you start, write down: reviews in the last 90 days, how many got a reply, and roughly how many calls a week go unanswered.

Day 30

  • Fewer than 7 of 10 reply drafts are usable after small edits, even after two rounds of tone fixes: change the instructions or stop the reply bot.
  • Callers complain about the phone bot more than once a week: switch the line back to voicemail and review the call notes.

Day 60

  • Your share of reviews with a reply is not higher than your baseline: the approval step is the bottleneck; set a fixed daily slot or stop.
  • Booked calls from the bot are zero or near zero: the phone plan is not paying its way; cancel it and keep only Reply Desk.

Day 90

  • Hours saved a month x your hourly value is below the monthly tool bill: switch off the part that does not cover its cost.
  • If it does cover its cost, keep it and review again every quarter.

E These thresholds are our rules of thumb, not benchmarks.

Rules to Follow

  • Fake reviews and review suppression (US)The FTC rule on consumer reviews bans fake or false reviews, buying reviews, and suppressing negative reviews by threats or false claims. A bot must never write a review or pose as a customer. Source.
  • Google review policyGoogle does not allow businesses to offer incentives for reviews, to discourage negative reviews or to ask only happy customers. You may ask for genuine reviews without trying to influence the rating. Source.
  • AI voices on calls (US)The FCC ruled that AI-generated voices count as artificial voices under the TCPA. Outbound calls with an AI voice need prior consent. Keep the phone bot to answering inbound calls unless you have consent. Source.
  • Email rules (US, CAN-SPAM)Commercial email needs a working opt-out, and people cannot be asked to pay or do more than reply or visit one page to opt out. The rules treat transactional or relationship messages, such as a reminder about an invoice already owed, differently from marketing email. Keep subject lines honest either way. Source.
  • Data protection (EU and UK customers)If you hold personal data about people in the EU or UK, GDPR or UK GDPR applies: collect only what the job needs, tell people how you use it, and keep access to the connected accounts to the people who need it. Source.
  • No income claimsNothing on this page promises income, savings or any business result. The value lines are labelled estimates with their basis. Bots draft and you approve. Source.

FAQ

Will the bot post replies on its own?

No. Reply Desk drafts and you post. You stay the one who replies in public.

Can it ask only happy customers for reviews?

No, and you should not either. Google's policy and the FTC rule both count selective asking or suppressing negative reviews as manipulation. Ask everyone the same way.
