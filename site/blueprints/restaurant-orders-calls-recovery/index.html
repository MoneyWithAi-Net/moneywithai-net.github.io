Who It's For

  • Independent restaurants and small groups on one or more delivery apps.
  • Owners whose phone rings during service with nobody free to answer.

Not for

  • Chains with a central call centre or a POS vendor that already handles this.

The First 10

Who
Phase 1: your last 10 delivery orders that were cancelled, refunded or adjusted. Then the last 10 guests who left a review.
Where to find them
Each delivery app's merchant portal (orders, adjustments and payouts pages) and your Google Business Profile reviews.

The first message

Order {order id} on {date}: the full order was prepared and handed to the courier at {time}, then marked {cancelled/refunded} and deducted from our payout. Our records show it was ready on time. Please review and reimburse the {amount} adjustment. Prep ticket and handover time attached.
Then
Approve or reject each drafted request yourself before it is filed, and log the outcome: approved, denied or no answer.
  • File only requests your own records support.
  • Follow each platform's dispute window and terms in your merchant agreement.

Work out your own break-even

  • Hours the bot must save each month to cover its running cost = monthly tool bill / your hourly value.
  • Months to recover the setup = setup price ($1,500 founding, normally $2,500) / (hours saved a month x your hourly value - monthly tool bill).
  • Measure hours saved in week 1 (before) and week 4 (after) with a simple time log. Use your own numbers, not ours.
  • For phase 1, track reimbursements approved per month in your payout reports. That is your number, from the platform.

Start with phase 1. Add the phone line only if phase 1 is working and calls are a real problem for you.

Weekly Cadence

  1. MondayYouApprove recovery requests for last week's orders and weekend review replies (20 minutes).The botsOrder Recovery scans each merchant portal and drafts requests. Reply Desk drafts review replies.
  2. TuesdayYouRead the phone bot's call notes from the weekend (phase 2).The botsPhone Table Booker answers and books; logs anything it passed to staff.
  3. WednesdayYouUpdate menu, hours and specials notes the bots use.The botsChat ordering and phone bot pick up the new notes.
  4. ThursdayYouApprove the second batch of recovery requests and new review replies.The botsOrder Recovery checks answers on earlier requests.
  5. Friday and SaturdayYouService. Nothing required from you.The botsPhone Table Booker covers the rush. Chat ordering sends tickets to the POS (phase 3).
  6. SundayYouFive-minute look at the week: requests filed and approved, calls answered, tables booked.The botsWrites the weekly summary.

When to Stop

Baseline first. Before you start, pull 90 days of delivery adjustments from each portal and estimate how many calls go unanswered at peak (ask staff to tally for one week).

Day 30

  • Platforms deny most requests the bot drafted: tighten which orders qualify or stop phase 1.
  • Guests complain about the phone bot: send calls back to staff and review the notes before trying again.

Day 60

  • Approved reimbursements in your payout reports do not cover the Grok Bot plan: stop order recovery.
  • Tables booked by the phone bot are near zero: cancel the voice line.

Day 90

  • Keep only the phases whose value is above their monthly cost on your own numbers.

E These thresholds are our rules of thumb, not benchmarks.

Rules to Follow

  • Delivery platform termsFile only disputes your records support, within each platform's window. Repeated unsupported claims can put your merchant account at risk.
  • AI voices on calls (US)AI voices count as artificial voices under the TCPA. Use the voice bot for inbound calls; outbound calls with an AI voice need prior consent. Source.
  • Call recordingRecording rules differ by state and country; some require every party's consent. Tell callers if calls are recorded.
  • ReviewsReply to reviews; never write them or offer rewards for them. Source.
  • Data protection (EU and UK customers)If you hold personal data about people in the EU or UK, GDPR or UK GDPR applies: collect only what the job needs, tell people how you use it, and keep access to the connected accounts to the people who need it. Source.
  • No income claimsNothing on this page promises income, savings or any business result. The value lines are labelled estimates with their basis. Bots draft and you approve. Source.

FAQ

Do I need all four bots?

No. Start with order recovery and review replies. Add the phone line, then chat ordering, only when the first phase is working.

Will the phone bot take payments?

Not in this setup. It answers questions and books tables; payment stays with your POS.
