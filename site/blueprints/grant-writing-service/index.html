Who It's For

  • Consultants, NGO staff and founders who already write proposals.
  • Advisers whose clients ask "is there funding for this?"

Not for

  • Anyone who wants applications submitted automatically. The bot never submits.

The Bot Stack

  • GrantOgreby @HoneybadgerFan4Finds live programmes and prepares the pack a person files.

Full write-up with the flow diagram: Offer Grant Finding as a Service.

The First 10

Who
10 past clients or contacts with a project that could fit public or foundation funding.
Where to find them
Your client list and the organisations you have worked with in the last two years.

The first message

Hi {first name}, I now help organisations find live funding programmes and prepare the application pack. For {project}, I can run a first search and send you the 3 best matches with deadlines. Would that be useful? If not, just reply no thanks. {your name}
Then
Run GrantOgre for one client you know well and check every match on its official page before you show it.
  • Start with people who know you: past clients, referrals and contacts. This is not a cold list.
  • Include an easy way to say no thanks, and stop if they do.

  • Clients needed to cover the tools = monthly tool bill / your monthly fee per client. Set your own fee; we do not publish a typical one.

  • Track two numbers from week 1: conversations started and clients signed. Use your own numbers, not ours.

When to Stop

Baseline first. Write down how long one funding search and pack takes you today.

Day 30

  • More than half the matches fail eligibility on the official page: narrow the search criteria.

Day 60

  • Clients do not pay for research packs: offer it as part of a larger project instead.

Day 90

  • Fees from packs do not cover the tool bill and your time: stop.

E These thresholds are our rules of thumb, not benchmarks.
