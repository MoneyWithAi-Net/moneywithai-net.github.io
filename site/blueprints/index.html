Questions

What is a blueprint?

An operating plan for one use case in a business you already run: who to start with and the exact first message, what the tools cost, a weekly cadence, the numbers that tell you to stop, the rules to follow and what is typical for the problem.

How current are the prices?

Every price links to the vendor page and shows the date we checked it (27 Oct 2026 for this set). List prices come first; offer prices are shown only with their terms. Prices change, so check the vendor page before you buy.

What do the V, E and A labels mean?

V: verified by us on the linked page on the checked date. E: our estimate, with its basis. A: the source's own claim, linked.

Do blueprints promise results?

No. A blueprint is operating help, not legal, tax or financial advice. Nothing in it promises income, savings or any business result, and the stop thresholds are our rules of thumb, not benchmarks.
