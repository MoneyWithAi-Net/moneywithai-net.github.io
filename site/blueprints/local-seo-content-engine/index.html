The First 10

Who
10 local business clients or contacts who serve several towns.
Where to find them
Your current clients, past clients and local business groups.

The first message

Hi {first name}, customers in {town} search for {service} but {business} has no page for that town yet. I can draft three town pages for you to approve before anything goes live. Want to see a sample? If not, just reply no thanks. {your name}
Then
Run Competitor Intel for one client and share the first weekly report as a free sample.
  • Start with people who know you: past clients, referrals and contacts. This is not a cold list.
  • Include an easy way to say no thanks, and stop if they do.

Weekly Cadence

  1. MondayYouApprove this week's content plan.The botsContent Planner Manager plans local topics and keeps the calendar.
  2. TuesdayYouEdit drafts for local facts.The botsGeo-Targeted SEO Publisher drafts city pages.
  3. WednesdayYouApprove pages to publish.The botsPublisher publishes only approved pages.
  4. ThursdayYouRead the competitor report.The botsCompetitor Intel files its weekly report.
  5. FridayYouReview: pages live, impressions, calls or forms.The botsPlanner updates next week.
  6. WeekendYouNothing required.The botsNothing publishes.

When to Stop

Baseline first. Write down search impressions and leads per client before the first page.

Day 30

  • Drafts need heavy fact fixes: give the publisher a local facts sheet per town.

Day 60

  • No new impressions on published pages: rework topics with the client.

Day 90

  • Retainer income does not cover tools and time: stop or cut to the report only.

E These thresholds are our rules of thumb, not benchmarks.
