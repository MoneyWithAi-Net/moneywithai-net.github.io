The First 10

Who
The 10 largest recurring software charges on your own cards and invoices from the last 3 months.
Where to find them
Card statements, billing emails in Gmail and your accounting software.

The first message

Hi {account manager}, we are reviewing our tools ahead of our renewal on {date}. We use {product} on {seats} seats today and are deciding whether to keep it, reduce seats or move. Could you share your best price for {plan} on {term}, or any option for fewer seats? Thanks, {your name}
Then
Let Watchdog Receipts list every other charge, and Reaper flag tools with no recent use. Work through them 10 at a time.
  • These are your own vendors, not prospects. Send from your own inbox.
  • Read the contract notice terms before you send a cancellation.

Work out your own break-even

  • Savings needed to cover the tools = monthly tool bill. Anything you cancel or cut above that is money kept.
  • Months to recover the setup = setup price ($1,500 founding, normally $2,500) / (monthly savings found - monthly tool bill).
  • Write down your current monthly total in week 1 and compare in week 4. Use your own numbers, not ours.

When to Stop

Baseline first. Before you start, write down your total monthly software spend and the number of paid tools.

Day 30

  • No tool cut, downgraded or renegotiated after the first review: your stack is already lean; keep only the renewal reminders.

Day 60

  • Savings found are below the monthly tool bill: drop the paid pieces and keep the free sheet and reminders.

Day 90

  • Spend has crept back to the baseline: make renewal approval a rule, not a reminder.

E These thresholds are our rules of thumb, not benchmarks.

Rules to Follow

  • Contract notice termsMany contracts renew automatically and need written notice by a set date. Read each contract yourself before you cancel or negotiate; a bot reminder is not legal advice.
  • Client confidentialityConnect only the accounts you or your client own, give each bot the least access it needs, and check client contracts for rules on where their data may be stored.
  • Data protection (EU and UK customers)If you hold personal data about people in the EU or UK, GDPR or UK GDPR applies: collect only what the job needs, tell people how you use it, and keep access to the connected accounts to the people who need it. Source.

FAQ

Will it cancel tools for me?

No. It finds charges, flags waste and drafts emails. You cancel or keep each one.

Does it need bank access?

No. It works from billing emails and the statements you export. Give it the least access that works.
