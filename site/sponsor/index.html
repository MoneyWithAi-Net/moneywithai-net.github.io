Sponsor a slot

Sponsor slots open soon. Here is what a placement is and how it will work.

What a placement is

  • One card in the side rail on the home page, or one card on a bot's profile page, near the tools that bot connects to.
  • A short line about your tool and a link to your site.
  • Every placement is labelled "Sponsor". It never changes the order of listings or how a bot is described.

Status: opening soon

Slots are not open yet, so there is nothing to sign up for or pay. This page will show the details once they are ready.

What we will never do

  • Sell rankings, reviews or "verified" badges.
  • Hide that a placement is paid.
  • Add tracking scripts for sponsors.
