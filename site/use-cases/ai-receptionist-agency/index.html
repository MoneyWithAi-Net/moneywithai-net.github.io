Steps

  1. Add the 5 bots in the stack: Voice Receptionist Call Booker, Voice Booking Line, Virtual Front Desk Booker, Phone Receptionist and Case Skeleton.
  2. Connect the tools they use: Airtable, Google Calendar, Google Sheets, OpenAI and Bland.
  3. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  4. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  5. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  6. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.

A Grok Bot plan, plus an account on each tool the stack connects to: n8n, Airtable, Google Calendar, Google Sheets, OpenAI, Bland, Grok, Cal.com. We show a price only when we have checked it on a public page, with the source and date below.

  • V Grok Bot, Premium (The X Subscription): $30 a month. Free and Plus plans do not include Grok Bot. Super is $100, Ultra $200. Source, checked 2026-10-27.
  • V Phone Receptionist phone plan, Agent Phone Plan (from the bot share card): $15 first month, then $30 a month Source, checked 2026-10-27.
  • V n8n, Community Edition (self-hosted) or Cloud Starter: Community $0 licence; Starter 20 euros a month billed annually; Pro 50 euros a month billed annually. Source, checked 2026-10-27.
  • V Airtable, Free or Team: Free $0. Team $20 per user a month billed annually. Business $45 per user a month billed annually. Source, checked 2026-10-29.
  • V Bland, Start: $0 platform fee, $0.14 per talk minute, $0.05 per transfer minute, 100 calls a day cap. Includes an inbound number. Source, checked 2026-10-27.
  • V Cal.com, Individuals (Free) or Teams: Individuals free, 1 user. Teams $12 per user a month billed yearly. Source, checked 2026-10-29.
  • V OpenAI API, Pay as you go, gpt-4.1-mini: $0.40 per 1M input tokens, $1.60 per 1M output tokens Source, checked 2026-10-27.
  • V Google Calendar, Personal Google account: $0. Google says anyone with a personal Google Account can create one booking page; Workspace plans add more. Source, checked 2026-10-27.

Guardrails

  • A person stays reachable for anything the bot is unsure about.
  • You need your own accounts for the connected tools; check their current plans first.
  • Check local rules on cold outreach, calls and texts before contacting anyone.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Tell callers they are speaking with an AI where required.
  • Test with real call scenarios first.
  • Start with one customer segment and add more only when the flow runs cleanly.
