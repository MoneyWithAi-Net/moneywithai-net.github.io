Who it's for

  • Home builders who sell from plans.
  • Remodelers and architects showing clients a layout.
  • Makers working from part drawings.

What it does

  • Plan to 3D: Turns a 2D drawing into a 3D model with a preview image and rough quantities.
  • Plan Precheck Bot: Checks a plan set for likely issues and prepares markups for a licensed reviewer.

How it helps your business

  • Clients see the layout and decide sooner.
  • Early previews take minutes, not hours of drafting.

Steps

  1. Add the 2 bots in the stack: Plan to 3D and Plan Precheck Bot.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Share plans only when you have the rights to them.
  • Measure before you order anything from the quantities.
  • Keep it small at first: one plan and one client.
