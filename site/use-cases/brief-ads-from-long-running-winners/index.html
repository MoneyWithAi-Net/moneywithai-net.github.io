Who it's for

  • Boutiques running Meta or TikTok ads
  • Creators who sell their own products
  • Small shops briefing a freelancer

What it does

  • Winning Ad Spy & Boutique Creative Studio: Breaks down long-running niche ads and drafts concepts and storyboards for your brand.
  • Competitor Ad Watch: Posts new competitor creatives and angles to Slack.
  • Ad Creative Generator: Generates fresh creatives from your product details.

How it helps your business

  • Less test budget on weak creative.
  • Faster briefs without an agency.
  • Estimate math: one or two weak tests skipped a month at $25 to $150 each = about $50 to $300 a month.

Steps

  1. Add the 3 bots in the stack: Winning Ad Spy & Boutique Creative Studio, Competitor Ad Watch and Ad Creative Generator.
  2. List your product, audience and two or three competitors.
  3. Review the long-running ad breakdowns.
  4. Brief one original concept and make it.
  5. Test it on a small budget before you scale.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Long-running ads hint at what works; they are not proof of profit.
  • Make original creative every time.
  • Keep it small at first: one angle and one test budget.
