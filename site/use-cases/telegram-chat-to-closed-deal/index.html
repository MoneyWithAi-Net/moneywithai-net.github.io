What it does

  • Telegram Sales Handoff Bot: Qualifies chat leads and hands warm ones to a human.
  • Speed-to-Lead Closer: Sends approved answers and a payment link.
  • Prospect Meeting Booker: Books the sales call.
  • Post Call Assistant: Drafts the follow-up after the call.

How it helps your business

  • No lead waits hours for a first answer.
  • Closers only talk to qualified people.

Steps

  1. Add the 4 bots in the stack: Telegram Sales Handoff Bot, Speed-to-Lead Closer, Prospect Meeting Booker and Post Call Assistant.
  2. Connect the tools they use: OpenAI, Redis, Telegram, Stripe, Gmail and Google Calendar.
  3. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  4. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  5. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  6. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.

Guardrails

  • A person stays reachable for anything the bot is unsure about.
  • Every message and document stays a draft until you approve it.
  • The bots never pay, sign or accept terms for you.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Write down your fit rules first; the bot follows them.
  • Answer the best leads personally and fast.
  • Start with one customer segment and add more only when the flow runs cleanly.
