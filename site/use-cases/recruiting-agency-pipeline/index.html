Who It's For

  • Independent recruiters.
  • Small businesses hiring

What It Does

  • Job to Shortlist Recruiter: Turns a job into a candidate shortlist.
  • Mappy (Talent Map): Maps people at target companies.
  • Resume Ranking Sheet: Scores resumes against the job.
  • Resume and Voice Interview Screener: Runs a first behavioral interview.
  • Talent Matchmaker: Matches candidates and roles.

How to Set It Up

Time to Set Up
About one to two days (our estimate)
Difficulty
Advanced
Tools Needed
  • Grok Bot: Grok Bot account needed
  • n8n: Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • Airtable: Free plan available
  • Claude: Free plan available; API is pay as you go
  • OpenAI: Pay as you go (API billed per token)
  • Google Drive: Free with a Google account (15 GB); more storage is paid
  • Gemini: Pay as you go or paid plan
  • Google Sheets: Free with a Google account
  • Notion: Free plan available
  1. Add the 5 bots in the stack: Job to Shortlist Recruiter, Mappy (Talent Map), Resume Ranking Sheet, Resume and Voice Interview Screener and Talent Matchmaker.
  2. Connect the tools they use, starting with Airtable, Claude, OpenAI, Google Drive, Gemini; the full list is below.
  3. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  4. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  5. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  6. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.
