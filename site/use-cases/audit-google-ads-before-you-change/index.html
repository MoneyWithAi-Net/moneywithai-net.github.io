Who it's for

  • Owners spending on Google Search ads.
  • Agencies managing client accounts.
  • Local businesses that want weekly checks without auto-changes.

What it does

  • Google Ads Manager: Audits search terms and negatives, suggests Search builds and weekly checks, and waits for your yes.
  • Google Ads Performance Analyst: Reviews accounts and campaigns and reports wasted spend and next fixes.
  • Google Ads Optimizer: Runs a scheduled review that proposes changes toward your goal.

How it helps your business

  • Less wasted Search spend from clearer reviews.
  • Weekly checks without unsupervised changes.

Time to set up

Tools needed

Steps

  1. Add the 3 bots in the stack: Google Ads Manager, Google Ads Performance Analyst and Google Ads Optimizer.
  2. Connect Google Ads with read access first.
  3. Run one audit on a small campaign.
  4. Compare the waste list across the bots.
  5. Approve each change by hand.

Questions

What tools do I need to audit Google Ads this way?

The 3 bots in the stack, plus the tools listed. Each tool may need its own plan.

Will these bots change my account on their own?

Keep them on recommend-and-wait. Google Ads Manager says it changes nothing without your yes, and you should approve every change from the others too.
