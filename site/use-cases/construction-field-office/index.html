Who It's For

  • Small contractors and project managers
  • Builders and contractors

What It Does

  • Construction Manager Bot: Turns photos into issues and RFIs.
  • Contractor Daily: Builds the daily jobsite packet.
  • Plan Precheck Bot: Prechecks plan sets.
  • Grant (General Manager): Oversees dispatch and back office.
  • Construction Chief of Staff: Coordinates the office.

How It Makes Money

  • Fewer missed issues and disputes.
  • Daily records without paperwork.
  • Estimate math: Avoided rework and admin hours = about $500 to $4k a month.

How to Set It Up

Time to set up
About half a day (our estimate)
Difficulty
Intermediate

Tools needed

  • Grok BotGrok Bot account needed
  • EmailAny email account you already use
  • Google CalendarFree with a Google account

Steps

  1. Add the 5 bots in the stack: Construction Manager Bot, Contractor Daily, Plan Precheck Bot, Grant (General Manager) and Construction Chief of Staff.
  2. Connect the tools they use: Email and Google Calendar.
  3. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  4. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  5. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  6. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.
