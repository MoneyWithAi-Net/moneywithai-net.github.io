What It Does

  • Podcast Guest Briefer: Researches guests before recording.
  • Episode Launch Crew: Publishes to Castos and YouTube.
  • Clip Bot: Cuts captioned highlights.
  • Podcast Clip Publisher: Turns highlights into X posts.
  • Article and Newsletter Writer: Writes the episode newsletter.

How to Set It Up

Time to Set Up
About half a day (our estimate)
Difficulty
Intermediate
Tools Needed
  • Grok Bot: Grok Bot account needed
  • Gmail: Free with a Google account
  • Google Docs: Free plan available
  • Castos: Paid plan
  • Google Drive: Free with a Google account (15 GB); more storage is paid
  • YouTube: Check the vendor's pricing
  • Slack: Free plan available
  • X: Free account; the X API is pay per use
  • Squarespace: Check the vendor's pricing
  1. Add the 5 bots in the stack: Podcast Guest Briefer, Episode Launch Crew, Clip Bot, Podcast Clip Publisher and Article and Newsletter Writer.
  2. Connect the tools they use, starting with Gmail, Google Docs, Castos, Google Drive, YouTube; the full list is below.
  3. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  4. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  5. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  6. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.
