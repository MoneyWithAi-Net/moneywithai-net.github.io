Who it's for

  • Small computer and phone repair shops
  • Resellers who buy and sell PC parts on eBay
  • IT providers who quote small office installs

What it does

  • Computer Shop Bot: Prices parts for profit after fees, tracks orders and shipping, and drafts network install quotes.
  • eBay Lister: Makes an eBay draft from one photo with sold comps and a price.
  • Competitor Price Alert: Emails you when competitor prices change.

How it helps your business

  • Parts priced to profit after fees.
  • Less time on listings, tracking and quotes.
  • Estimate math: 3 to 6 hours saved a month at $25 to $50 an hour = about $75 to $300 a month.

How to set it up

Time to set up
About 1 to 2 hours (our estimate)
Difficulty
Beginner

Tools needed

  • Grok BotGrok Bot account needed
  • eBaySeller account; eBay charges selling fees

Steps

  1. Add the 3 bots in the stack: Computer Shop Bot, eBay Lister and Competitor Price Alert.
  2. Connect the eBay account you sell from.
  3. Give it your eBay fees, shipping costs and target margin.
  4. Price 5 items you already sold and compare with your own numbers.
  5. List new stock from photos and review every draft before it goes live.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Update fees when eBay or your carrier changes them.
  • Price from sold listings, not asking prices.
  • Keep it small at first: price one shelf of stock before listing everything.
