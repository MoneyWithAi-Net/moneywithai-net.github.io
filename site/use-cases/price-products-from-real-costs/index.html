Who it's for

  • Online stores and makers with many products or variants.
  • Shops whose supplier costs change often.
  • Wholesalers updating price lists for retailers.

What it does

  • Product Price Writer: Suggests retail prices from your costs for each product and variant.
  • Product Cataloger: Builds a catalog with the approved prices.

How it helps your business

  • Products priced below cost get fixed.
  • Buyers always see current prices.
  • Estimate math: 1 to 3 percent better margin on $2,000 to $10,000 of monthly sales = about $20 to $300 a month.

How to set it up

Time to set up
About 1 to 2 hours (our estimate)
Difficulty

Tools needed

  • Grok BotGrok Bot account needed
  • Google SheetsFree with a Google account

Steps

  1. Add the 2 bots in the stack: Product Price Writer and Product Cataloger.
  2. Put product costs, including shipping and fees, in one Google Sheet.
  3. Tell it your target margin and any price points you never go below.
  4. Check every suggested price on a small set of products first.
  5. Approve prices, update your store, then build the catalog for buyers.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Include shipping, packaging and payment fees in the cost, not just the product.
  • Change prices in small steps and watch what sells.
  • Keep it small at first: fix the products priced below cost before anything else.
