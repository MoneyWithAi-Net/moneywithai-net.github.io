Steps

  1. Add the 2 bots in the stack: Security Agent and Chief of Staff (Aryaman).
  2. Answer the setup questions and pick only the activities you need to log.
  3. Approve each safety rule and red flag one by one.
  4. Ask each active bot to send its daily log and check the first record by hand.
  5. Review the record every week with your chief of staff brief.

Guardrails

  • Log counts and times, not message contents, unless you truly need them.
  • A log is not a lock: keep approval steps on purchases, sends and deletions.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Start with purchases and outside messages; they carry the most risk.
  • Keep the rule list short so every bot can follow it.
  • Keep it small at first: two or three bots logged well beats ten logged loosely.

Questions

What tools do I need to know what every bot on my team did?

The 2 bots in the stack, plus the tools listed. Each tool may need its own plan.

Does this stop a bot from doing something wrong?

It records and flags. Keep approval steps on anything that buys, sends or deletes.
