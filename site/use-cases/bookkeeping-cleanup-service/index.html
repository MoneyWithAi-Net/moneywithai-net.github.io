What it does

  • Bookkeeping Auditor: Audits books against bank and card records.
  • Invoice OCR Checker: Validates invoice PDFs into Sheets.
  • Receipt Scanner / Expense Tracking: Logs receipts into a sheet.
  • Ledger: Handles recurring finance admin.
  • Tax Firm OS: Runs the practice back office.

How it helps your business

  • Cleanups are a clear, paid first project.
  • Monthly bookkeeping follows.
  • Estimate math: 1 to 2 cleanups plus 3 to 8 monthly clients = about $1k to $6k a month.

Tools needed

Steps

  1. Add the 5 bots in the stack: Bookkeeping Auditor, Invoice OCR Checker, Receipt Scanner / Expense Tracking, Ledger and Tax Firm OS.
  2. Connect the tools they use: QuickBooks, Google Sheets and OpenRouter.
  3. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  4. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  5. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  6. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Reconcile with your bank every month.
  • Keep an accountant in the loop for tax.
  • Start with one customer segment and add more only when the flow runs cleanly.
