Who It's For

  • Real estate agents, wholesalers and small investors.
  • Small brokerages

What It Does

  • Motivated Seller Finder: Flags likely motivated sellers from property data.
  • Seller Lead Skip Tracer: Skip-traces owners and pushes leads to HubSpot.
  • SMS Property Lead Qualifier: Qualifies replies over SMS and logs answers.
  • Buy to Rent Property: Underwrites the deal as a rental.

How It Makes Money

  • Earlier contact with motivated sellers means better deals.
  • Underwriting filters out deals that do not pencil.
  • Estimate math: 0 to 1 closed deal a month at a $5,000 to $10,000 fee = about $0 to $10k a month.

Steps

  1. Add the 4 bots in the stack: Motivated Seller Finder, Seller Lead Skip Tracer, SMS Property Lead Qualifier and Buy to Rent Property.
  2. Connect the tools they use, starting with Zillapi, HubSpot, Google Sheets, OpenAI, Postgres; the full list is below.
  3. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  4. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  5. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  6. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.

Tips and Pitfalls

  • Check do-not-call and data rules before you contact owners.
  • Skip-traced data can be wrong; verify before outreach.
  • Start with one customer segment and add more only when the flow runs cleanly.
