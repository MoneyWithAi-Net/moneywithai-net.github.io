Who it's for

  • Owners who track suppliers, prices or rule changes.
  • Founders who follow a market or a competitor.
  • Operators who want fewer, better alerts.

What it does

  • When It Matters: Keeps watch on what you keep checking and messages you only when it would change your answer.
  • Competitor Watch: Tracks competitor pricing, product and hiring pages from a list of URLs.
  • Market Research: Reads your watchlist and writes source-graded market notes.

How it helps your business

  • Hours back from manual checking.
  • Price and rule changes caught before they cost you.

Steps

  1. Add the 3 bots in the stack: When It Matters, Competitor Watch and Market Research.
  2. Give When It Matters one thing you check every week.
  3. Add 3 to 5 competitor pages to Competitor Watch.
  4. Set a weekly market note for your field.
  5. Drop any alert that does not change a decision.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Start with one topic and a few pages.
  • Say what counts as a real change in plain words.
  • Keep it small at first: watch one thing for a week before adding more.
