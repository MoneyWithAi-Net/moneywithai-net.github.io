Steps

  1. Add Legend Lead Ops and give it your services and service area.
  2. Test it on a few past inquiries.
  3. Decide whether you need phone answering, and check its plan first.
  4. Use Reply Desk for reviews and messages.

Guardrails

  • Quotes and messages stay drafts until you approve them.
  • Check the phone plan's current price before signing up.
