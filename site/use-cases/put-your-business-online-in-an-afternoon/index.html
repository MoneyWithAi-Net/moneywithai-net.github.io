Who it's for

  • Local service businesses with no website yet.
  • Owners who have never used an AI bot.
  • Side businesses that need one page for customers.

What it does

  • ShopFront: Builds and publishes a one-page site from your answers, then helps you add photos, a contact form or your own domain.
  • WebsiteAudit: Runs a free audit on any URL and explains the top issues in plain words.
  • Web Design Assistant: Turns a website brief into page structures and designs when you need more pages.

How it helps your business

  • Customers who search for you can call or message you.
  • You skip a big upfront website cost before you have one page live.

Steps

  1. Add the 3 bots in the stack: ShopFront, WebsiteAudit and Web Design Assistant.
  2. Write down your services, city and phone number.
  3. Let ShopFront build the page and check every word.
  4. Run WebsiteAudit on the live link and fix what it flags.
  5. Add the link to your Google Business Profile and social bios.

Tips and pitfalls

  • One page done today beats a full site planned for months.
  • Put your phone number at the top.
  • Keep it small at first: launch one page, then add photos and a form.
