  • Invoice OCR Checker: Validates invoice fields.

Steps

  1. Add the 4 bots in the stack: Invoice Approval Router, Invoice OCR Checker, Haggle Bot and Contract Renewal Reminder.
  2. Connect the tools they use: Gmail, Google Drive, Google Sheets, OpenAI, OpenRouter and Slack.
  3. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  4. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  5. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  6. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Check totals and tax before sending.
  • Keep a record of every automated invoice.
