Steps

  1. Export open invoices into a Google Sheet.
  2. Import the n8n template and connect Gmail, Sheets and OpenAI.
  3. Approve the first ten drafts by hand.
  4. Schedule twice-weekly runs with an approval step.

Guardrails

  • Every reminder stays a draft until you approve it.
  • Chase only amounts that are genuinely owed and past due.
  • This is admin help, not legal or financial advice.
