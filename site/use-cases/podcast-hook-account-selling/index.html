Who It's For

  • Founders and account executives selling to larger companies.
  • B2B agencies
  • Consultants and freelancers

What It Does

  • PG: Finds personal hooks from podcasts, webinars and posts.
  • Meeting Brief: Preps each call with attendees and open loops.
  • Echo: Turns the call into recap slides.
  • Deal Desk Autopilot: Flags stalled steps and drafts follow-ups.

How It Makes Money

  • Personal hooks lift reply rates with senior buyers.
  • Recap slides keep the champion selling inside.
  • Estimate math: 1 extra deal a quarter at $6,000 to $45,000 = about $2k to $15k a month.

Steps

  1. Add the 4 bots in the stack: PG, Meeting Brief, Echo and Deal Desk Autopilot.
  2. Connect the tools they use: X, LinkedIn, Gmail and Google Calendar.
  3. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  4. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  5. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  6. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.
