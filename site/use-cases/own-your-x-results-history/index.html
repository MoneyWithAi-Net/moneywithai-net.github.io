Who it's for

  • Founders and consultants who win clients on X
  • Creators who post most days

What it does

  • Foreman: Lists unanswered replies, tracks followers and keeps a dated analytics history.
  • Morning Post Planner: Hands you a morning brief of what works in your niche, with drafts.
  • X Posting Advisor: Suggests when to post, quote or wait.

How it helps your business

Steps

  1. Add the 3 bots in the stack: Foreman, Morning Post Planner and X Posting Advisor.
  2. Connect X with the account you control and set a daily spend cap.
  3. Give the planner a short watchlist and a few posts of yours you like.
  4. Run each bot once and check the lists and drafts by hand.
  5. Schedule the morning brief and the daily reply list, and keep posting by hand.
