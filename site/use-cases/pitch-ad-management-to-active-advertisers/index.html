Who It's For

  • Freelance media buyers and small performance agencies.
  • B2B agencies
  • Consultants and freelancers

What It Does

  • Ad-Active Lead Finder: Finds companies running ads in your niche.
  • Site Audit: Scores each landing page and lists fixes.
  • Customer-Voice Ad Writer: Writes sample ad copy from real customer words.
  • Prospect Meeting Booker: Drafts a meeting request to the decision-maker.

How It Makes Money

  • Active advertisers already have a budget, so the pitch is about results, not convincing.
  • Sample angles make the pitch concrete.
  • Estimate math: 1 to 3 clients at $1,000 to $2,000 a month = about $1k to $6k a month.

Steps

  1. Add the 4 bots in the stack: Ad-Active Lead Finder, Site Audit, Customer-Voice Ad Writer and Prospect Meeting Booker.
  2. Connect the tools they use, starting with Meta Ad Library, Google Ads Transparency, Google Maps, GoHighLevel, HubSpot; the full list is below.
  3. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  4. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  5. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  6. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.
