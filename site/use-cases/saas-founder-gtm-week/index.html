How It Makes Money

  • A solo founder keeps outbound and follow-up going every week.
  • Release notes give sales a reason to reach out.
  • Estimate math: 5 to 25 new customers a month at $100 to $200 = about $500 to $5k a month.

Who It's For

  • Solo and two-person SaaS teams.
  • B2B agencies
  • Consultants and freelancers

What It Does

  • Sales Orchestrator: Routes each request to one specialist bot.
  • SaaSbot: Runs morning prep, outbound and reply handling.
  • Lookalike Scout: Adds lookalike accounts to the list.
  • Deal Desk Autopilot: Keeps open deals moving.
  • Ship Note: Turns releases into customer emails.

Time to Set Up

Tools Needed
  • Grok Bot: Grok Bot account needed
  • Treg: Check the vendor's pricing
  • Gmail: Free with a Google account

  1. Add the 5 bots in the stack: Sales Orchestrator, SaaSbot, Lookalike Scout, Deal Desk Autopilot and Ship Note.
  2. Connect the tools they use: Treg, Gmail and Google Calendar.
  3. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  4. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  5. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  6. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.
