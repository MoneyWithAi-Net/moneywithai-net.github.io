Who it's for

  • Owners with an offer or sales page that gets visits but few buyers.
  • Consultants, coaches and course sellers.
  • Small stores launching a new product page.

What it does

  • The Walk-Away: Gives a buy-or-leave verdict on one page, the line that loses trust and the smallest fix.
  • Fresh Eyes Product Auditor: Walks the site as a first-time customer and ranks usability fixes.
  • Site Audit: Checks speed, content and conversion basics with evidence.

How it helps your business

  • More visitors buy from a page that answers their worries.
  • Traffic you already pay for works harder.
  • Estimate math: one to two extra sales a month at $50 to $250 each = about $50 to $500 a month.

Steps

  1. Add the 3 bots in the stack: The Walk-Away, Fresh Eyes Product Auditor and Site Audit.
  2. Pick the one page that matters most for sales.
  3. Run the stranger-eyes review first and write down the line it flags.
  4. Run the usability and site checks to rule out speed or layout problems.
  5. Make one change, wait for some real visits, then review the page again.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Fix trust gaps first: unclear price, missing proof or a vague next step.
  • Ask a real customer to read the page too; bots are a second opinion.
  • Keep it small at first: one page fixed well beats five pages half changed.
