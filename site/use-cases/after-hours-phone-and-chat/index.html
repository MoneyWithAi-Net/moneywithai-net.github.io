Who it's for

  • Clinics, salons and service firms

What it does

  • Chat Voice and Phone Agent: Answers chat and phone from your docs and books.
  • CosVoice Bot Phone Line: Gives your bot a phone number and summaries.
  • Telegram Docs Support Bot: Answers on Telegram from a knowledge base.
  • Slack Stand-In: Covers a Slack channel for staff.

How it helps your business

  • Late inquiries become bookings.
  • Staff start the day with a clear list.
  • Estimate math: 3 to 15 extra bookings = about $300 to $3k a month.

Steps

  1. Add the 4 bots in the stack: Chat Voice and Phone Agent, CosVoice Bot Phone Line, Telegram Docs Support Bot and Slack Stand-In.
  2. Connect the tools they use, starting with Google Calendar, Google Drive, OpenAI, Qdrant, Gmail; the full list is below.
  3. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  4. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  5. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  6. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.

Guardrails

  • A person stays reachable for anything the bot is unsure about.
  • You need your own accounts for the connected tools; check their current plans first.
  • Handle customer and candidate data under your local privacy rules.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Tell callers they are speaking with an AI where required.
  • Test with real call scenarios first.
  • Start with one customer segment and add more only when the flow runs cleanly.
