How It Makes Money

  • Old leads you already paid for get a second chance.
  • Interested people land on your calendar.
  • Estimate math: 1 to 3 revived customers a month at $500 to $1,000 = about $500 to $3k a month.

Who It's For

  • Service businesses and agencies with a stale lead list.
  • Small sales teams
  • Agencies with a shared CRM

What It Does

  • Lead Enricher: Refreshes contact details.
  • Sheet to Voice Sales Caller: Calls the list with a voice agent and logs outcomes.
  • Prospect Meeting Booker: Books calls with interested leads.
  • Churn Win-Back Loop: Drafts a win-back email for the rest.

  1. Add the 4 bots in the stack: Lead Enricher, Sheet to Voice Sales Caller, Prospect Meeting Booker and Churn Win-Back Loop.
  2. Connect the tools they use: Weft, OneShot Agent, Google Sheets, Slack, Gmail and Google Calendar.
  3. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  4. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  5. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  6. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.

Tips and Pitfalls

  • Back up or export your CRM before the first cleanup run.
  • Review merges by hand; wrong merges are hard to undo.
  • Start with one customer segment and add more only when the flow runs cleanly.
