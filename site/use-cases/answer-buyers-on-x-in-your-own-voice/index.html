Who it's for

  • Founders who sell on X.
  • Consultants and agencies that get enquiries from posts.
  • Small shops that want to answer questions quickly.

What it does

  • Buyer Intent Reply Queue: Queues posts that show buying intent and sends the drafts to Slack.
  • Reply Drafter: Drafts a first take in your voice for posts worth replying to.

How it helps your business

  • You answer buyers while they are still looking.
  • Drafts cut the time each reply takes, and you still choose what to post.
  • Estimate math: one or two extra enquiries a month at a fee you set = the range above.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Reply with something useful before you mention what you sell.
  • Skip posts where the buyer is clearly not your customer.
  • Keep your voice natural and short.
