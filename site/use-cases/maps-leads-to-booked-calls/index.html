How It Makes Money

  • Each booked call is a shot at a website, SEO or care-plan client.
  • Audits make the first message specific instead of generic.
  • Estimate math: 1 to 4 new local clients a month at $500 to $1,000 each = about $500 to $4k a month.

Who It's For

  • Freelance web and SEO providers who sell to local businesses.
  • Local SEO freelancers
  • Small web and marketing agencies

What It Does

  • Maps Lead List Builder: Pulls businesses for one niche and city into a sheet.
  • WebsiteAudit: Runs a quick audit on each site and lists the top issues.
  • Site Research: Writes a short evidence brief for the best prospects.
  • Lead Enricher: Finds verified emails and a recent hook for each contact.
  • Prospect Meeting Booker: Drafts a meeting request with times that suit you.

Tips and Pitfalls

  • Start with one city and one trade so you can judge the list by hand.
  • Public listing data goes stale; recheck a business before you pitch it.
  • Start with one customer segment and add more only when the flow runs cleanly.
