  • You spend your time on the leads most likely to pay.

Who It's For

  • Consultants, freelancers and creators who get inbound requests.
  • Consultants and agencies
  • Professional services firms

What It Does

  • Lead Vetting: Screens leads from your inbox and sends a short decision summary to Slack.
  • Inbox Triage: Sorts the rest of your email and DMs into tasks.
  • Puts go or no-go summaries in slack and time on the leads likely to pay in front of you.

  1. Add the 2 bots in the stack: Lead Vetting and Inbox Triage.

  3. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  4. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
