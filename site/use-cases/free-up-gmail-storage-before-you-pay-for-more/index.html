Who it's for

  • Owners near their Gmail storage limit
  • Founders whose inbox is buried in promotions
  • Small teams that share one busy mailbox

What it does

  • Gmail to Drive Archiver: Saves your biggest emails and attachments to Drive, filed by sender, and waits for your yes before trash.
  • Unsub Watch: Drops the marketing lists you pick and never touches receipts, billing or 2FA mail.
  • Gmail Cleanup Chief of Staff: Shows the senders that fill your inbox and moves only approved bulk mail.

How it helps your business

  • Storage upgrades put off or skipped.
  • Less time lost in a crowded inbox.
  • Estimate math: 1 to 2 hours saved a month at $20 to $50 an hour = about $20 to $100 a month.

  1. Add the 3 bots in the stack: Gmail to Drive Archiver, Unsub Watch and Gmail Cleanup Chief of Staff.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Gmail and Drive share one Google storage pool on most plans, so archive the right things and delete what you truly do not need.
  • Keep billing and receipt mail on the safe list.
  • Keep it small at first: one batch of the largest emails before a full sweep.

Questions

What could clearing Gmail storage be worth?

Our estimate is $20 to $100 a month from inbox time saved and a storage upgrade put off. It is an estimate, not a promise.

What tools do I need to free up Gmail storage this way?

The 3 bots in the stack, plus the tools listed. Each tool may need its own plan.

Will this delete my important email?

Not on its own. The archiver waits for your yes before any trash, and Unsub Watch leaves banks, receipts, 2FA, shipping and billing mail alone.
