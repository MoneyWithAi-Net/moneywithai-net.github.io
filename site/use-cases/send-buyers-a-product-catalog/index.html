Who it's for

  • Makers and shops that sell to stores or at markets.
  • Wholesalers who send line sheets to retailers.
  • Online sellers who also take bulk orders.

What it does

  • Product Cataloger: Builds catalog pages with a name, photo, SKU, price, colors and stock for each item, and flags gaps.
  • Shopify Product Image Maker: Makes cleaner product images where your photos are weak.

How it helps your business

  • Buyers order from a clear catalog instead of waiting on you for prices.
  • Updating a catalog takes minutes once the product list is clean.
  • Estimate math: 3 to 6 hours saved a month at $25 to $50 an hour = about $75 to $300 a month.

Time to set up

Tools needed

Steps

  1. Add the 2 bots in the stack: Product Cataloger and Shopify Product Image Maker.
  2. Put your product photos and price list in one place.
  3. Decide whether to keep your SKUs or let the cataloger suggest a simple system.
  4. Run one category first and check every line by hand.
  5. Build the full catalog, review the PDF, then send it yourself.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Keep one master product list so every new catalog starts from it.
  • Date each catalog so buyers know which prices are current.
  • Keep it small at first: one category done well beats a full catalog full of gaps.
