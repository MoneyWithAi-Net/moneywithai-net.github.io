Who it's for

  • Freelancers and agencies that sell services.
  • Side-business owners who sell products or classes.
  • Sales teams that want warm conversations.

What it does

  • Lead Finder: Finds public posts asking for what you sell, logs them in a Google Sheet and drafts a first message for the best matches.
  • Lead Pipeline Desk: Scores leads, merges duplicates and flags stuck deals from your sheet, sending nothing.

How it helps your business

  • You talk to buyers who have already said they need what you sell.
  • Scored leads mean your time goes to the likely ones first.
  • Estimate math: 2 to 4 extra calls a month at a fee you set = the range above.

Steps

  1. Add the 2 bots in the stack: Lead Finder and Lead Pipeline Desk.
  2. Write down the service, the buyer and the platforms to search.
  3. Run one platform for a week and check every match by hand.
  4. Set the scoring rules with the desk bot, then review the top leads each day.

Guardrails

  • Read each post before you reply, and follow each platform's rules on messages.
  • Lead with help, not a pitch. Nothing is sent without you.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Keep the search tight. A narrow service brings better matches than a broad one.
  • Log the outcome of every reply so the scoring improves.
  • Stop after a polite first message unless the buyer replies.
