Who it's for

  • Print-on-demand sellers on Etsy or Shopify.
  • Digital printable shops.
  • Boutique sellers using Printify or similar partners.

What it does

  • POD & Printable Boutique Studio: Writes SEO-minded titles and tags, audits mockups and runs margin math from your costs.
  • Shopify Product Image Maker: Makes clean product images for listings.
  • Product Price Writer: Helps write clear price copy once margins are set.

How it helps your business

  • Prices set from real costs after fees.
  • Fewer loss-making listings.

How to set it up

Time to set up
About 40 to 60 minutes for a first SKU (our estimate)
Difficulty
Beginner

Tools needed

  • Grok BotGrok Bot account needed
  • Shopify, Etsy, Printify or GumroadYour storefront; each has its own plan and fees

Steps

  1. Add the 3 bots in the stack: POD & Printable Boutique Studio, Shopify Product Image Maker and Product Price Writer.
  2. Write down real costs for one product.
  3. Ask for titles, tags and a margin check.
  4. Make listing images.
  5. Publish only when the margin holds after fees.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Fees kill thin margins; include them.
  • Do not invent competitor prices.
  • Keep it small at first: one SKU before a whole catalog.
