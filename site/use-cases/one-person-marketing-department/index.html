Who It's For

  • Founders without a marketing hire
  • Marketers and small agencies
  • Founders writing their own copy

What It Does

  • Marketing Bot (CMO): Plans offers, copy and conversion fixes.
  • Human Copywriter: Rewrites copy so it sounds human.
  • Social Media GTM Bot: Runs social across platforms.
  • Graphic Design Assistant: Makes on-brand graphics in Canva.
  • Marketing Context Collector: Pulls numbers and notes into a brief.

How It Makes Money

  • Marketing keeps shipping while you run the business.
  • Decisions come from your data, not guesses.
  • Estimate math: A 5 to 20 percent lift on an existing small revenue base = about $500 to $5k a month.

Steps

  1. Add the 5 bots in the stack: Marketing Bot (CMO), Human Copywriter, Social Media GTM Bot, Graphic Design Assistant and Marketing Context Collector.
  2. Connect the tools they use, starting with Canva, Google Drive, Gmail, Slack, Notion; the full list is below.
  3. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  4. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  5. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  6. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.
