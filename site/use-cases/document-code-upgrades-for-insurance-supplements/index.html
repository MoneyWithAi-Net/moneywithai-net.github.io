Who it's for

  • Roofers and restoration crews on insurance jobs.
  • Plumbing and water-mitigation contractors.
  • Small trade offices without a supplement specialist.

What it does

  • Contractor Supplement & Missed-Call Front Desk: Drafts supplement letters from photos and notes, sorts missed calls and chases open estimates.
  • Contractor Daily: Turns jobsite notes into one clean daily packet.
  • Contractor Front Desk: Sorts missed calls and web leads so real jobs get a reply.

How it helps your business

  • Paid for required code work instead of absorbing it.
  • Fewer missed calls and open estimates.

Steps

  1. Add the 3 bots in the stack: Contractor Supplement & Missed-Call Front Desk, Contractor Daily and Contractor Front Desk.
  2. Pick one recent insurance job with good photos.
  3. Build its daily packet and a draft supplement letter.
  4. Check every code section with your local code.
  5. Send the letter yourself and track the reply.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Take before, during and after photos on every job.
  • Cite only code sections you have checked.
  • Keep it small at first: one job end to end before you roll it out.
