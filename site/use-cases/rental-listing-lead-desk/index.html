Who it's for

  • Small landlords and property managers
  • Real estate agents
  • Small brokerages

What it does

  • RENTALS: Answers Marketplace listing leads and books showings.
  • Property Inquiry Scorer: Scores inquiries from email and files them in a CRM.
  • Phone Receptionist: Answers the rental line and books calls.
  • Landlord Ledger: Records rent and costs once a lease starts.

How it helps your business

  • Fewer vacant days on each unit.
  • You only show to qualified renters.
  • Estimate math: 1 to 2 fewer vacant weeks across a few units = about $300 to $2k a month.

Steps

  1. Add the 4 bots in the stack: RENTALS, Property Inquiry Scorer, Phone Receptionist and Landlord Ledger.
  2. Connect the tools they use: Airtable, Gmail, OpenAI, Google Calendar and Bland.
  3. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  4. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  5. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  6. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.
