Who it's for

  • Online shops that compete on price
  • Makers who sell next to bigger brands
  • Resellers who watch margins closely

What it does

  • Competitor Price Alert: Monitors competitor prices and emails you when they change.
  • Product Price Writer: Suggests retail prices from your costs for each Shopify product and variant.

How it helps your business

  • You see rival price moves in time to decide.
  • Prices come from your real costs, which protects margin.
  • Estimate math: a few faster price decisions a month on the affected products = the range above.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Do not match a rival who sells at a loss to you.
  • Recheck costs after each supplier change.
  • Watch fewer products well rather than many badly.
