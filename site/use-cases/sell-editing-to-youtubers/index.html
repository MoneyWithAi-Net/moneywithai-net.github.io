Who It's For

  • Video editors and small post-production studios
  • B2B agencies
  • Consultants and freelancers

What It Does

  • YouTube Creator Outreach Finder: Finds YouTube channels and verified emails in a niche.
  • YouTube Outlier Finder: Spots each channel's best-performing videos.
  • Shorty: Cuts sample Shorts from those videos.
  • Best Video Editor: Helps finish review-ready edits once hired.

How It Makes Money

  • A free sample made from their content is hard to ignore.
  • Monthly packages give steady income.
  • Estimate math: 1 to 5 clients at $800 to $1,000 a month = about $800 to $5k a month.

Steps

  1. Add the 4 bots in the stack: YouTube Creator Outreach Finder, YouTube Outlier Finder, Shorty and Best Video Editor.
  2. Connect the tools they use: Gmail and YouTube.
  3. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  4. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  5. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  6. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.
