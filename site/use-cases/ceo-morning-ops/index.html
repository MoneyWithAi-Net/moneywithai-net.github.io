What It Does

  • CEO Morning Secretary: Writes and reads the morning brief.
  • Jess (Executive Assistant): Answers staff from a playbook.
  • Inbox to Asana: Turns mail into Asana tasks.
  • Meeting Notes Assistant: Writes meeting notes.

How to Set It Up

Time to Set Up
About 2 to 4 hours (our estimate)
Difficulty
Beginner
Tools Needed
  • Grok Bot: Grok Bot account needed
  • Gmail: Free with a Google account
  • Asana: Free plan available
  • Google Calendar: Free with a Google account
  • Google Docs: Free plan available
  1. Add the 4 bots in the stack: CEO Morning Secretary, Jess (Executive Assistant), Inbox to Asana and Meeting Notes Assistant.
  2. Connect the tools they use: Gmail, Asana, Google Calendar and Google Docs.
  3. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  4. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  5. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  6. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.
