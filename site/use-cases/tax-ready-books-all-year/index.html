Who it's for

  • Owner-run small businesses.
  • Freelancers and sole traders.
  • Side businesses that mix personal and business spending.

What it does

  • Small Business Bookkeeper: Logs activity into your accounting software and keeps tax worksheets current.
  • Receipt Scanner / Expense Tracking: Turns receipt emails and photos into line items.
  • Watchdog Receipts: Finds receipts, renewals and trials in your inbox.

How it helps your business

  • No rushed catch-up work before the deadline.
  • Fewer lost receipts and missed deductions.

Steps

  1. Add the 3 bots in the stack: Small Business Bookkeeper, Receipt Scanner / Expense Tracking and Watchdog Receipts.
  2. Connect your accounting software and the inbox where receipts arrive, read-only where you can.
  3. Agree the categories with your accountant first.
  4. Run one week of activity and check every entry by hand.
  5. Keep a weekly review and keep originals of every receipt.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Keep original receipts; a log does not replace the document.
  • Separate business and personal cards if you can; it makes every entry easier.
  • Check a sample every week rather than trusting a full quarter at once.

Questions

What tools do I need to be ready for tax time all year?

The 3 bots in the stack, plus the tools listed. Each tool may need its own plan.

Will these bots file my taxes?

No. They keep records and worksheets ready. Filing and payments stay with you or your accountant.
