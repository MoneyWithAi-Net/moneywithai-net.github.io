Who it's for

  • SEO, AEO and GEO agencies and freelancers
  • Small web and marketing agencies

What it does

  • Lead Scout: Finds local businesses with likely search gaps.
  • Site Research: Turns each lead into an evidence-backed gap brief.
  • Kickbox to HubSpot Importer: Verifies emails and writes the results back to HubSpot.

How it helps your business

  • Specific audits win more discovery calls than generic offers.
  • Clean contact data protects your sending reputation.
  • Estimate math: 1 to 2 new SEO retainers a month at $500 to $1,500 each = about $500 to $3k a month.

How to set it up

Time to set up

Tools needed

  • HubSpotFree CRM available; paid tiers

Steps

  1. Add the 3 bots in the stack: Lead Scout, Site Research and Kickbox to HubSpot Importer.
  2. Connect the tools they use: Kickbox, HubSpot and Google Sheets.
  3. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  4. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  5. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  6. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Start with one city and one trade so you can judge the list by hand.
  • Public listing data goes stale; recheck a business before you pitch it.
  • Start with one customer segment and add more only when the flow runs cleanly.
