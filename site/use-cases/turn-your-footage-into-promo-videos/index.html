Who it's for

  • Small brands, venues and shops with phone footage.
  • Musicians and creators who sell their own work.
  • Event businesses that promote each date.

What it does

  • Vidness: Turns footage, stills or a song into a finished video cut to the beat, with captions.
  • Clipper: Cuts short clips for each channel.
  • Video Editor: Makes a short with a title and thumbnail.

How it helps your business

  • Regular promos for every launch, event and offer.
  • Editing fees stay in the business.
  • Estimate math: 1 to 3 promos a month at $50 to $100 each = about $50 to $300 a month.

How to set it up

Time to set up
About 1 to 2 hours (our estimate)

Tools needed

  • Grok BotGrok Bot account needed

Steps

  1. Add the 3 bots in the stack: Vidness, Clipper and Video Editor.
  2. Put your footage, photos and music in one folder.
  3. Make one 30 to 60 second promo and try 2 styles.
  4. Cut clips from the version you like.
  5. Check every caption and post the videos yourself.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Keep a short style note so every video looks like the same brand.
  • Put the offer and date in the first seconds.
  • Keep it small at first: one promo a week beats a backlog of half-done edits.

Questions

What could promo videos from my own footage be worth?

Our estimate is $50 to $300 a month in editing costs saved, assuming 1 to 3 promos a month. Any extra sales they bring are not counted.

What tools do I need to turn my footage into promo videos?

The 3 bots in the stack, plus the tools listed. Each tool may need its own plan.

Can I use any song I like?

Only if you own it or have a license. Platforms may mute or remove videos with music you do not have rights to.
