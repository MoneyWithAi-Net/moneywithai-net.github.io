Who it's for

  • Small businesses that sell and support customers on WhatsApp.
  • Owners who run client or supplier groups on WhatsApp.
  • Teams in regions where WhatsApp is the main business channel.

What it does

  • WhatsApper: Reads your chats and drafts replies in your voice, sent only after you approve the exact text.
  • WhatsApp Help Desk Assistant: Answers common questions with memory of each chat.
  • Multilingual Support Inbox: Helps you reply to customers who write in other languages.

How it helps your business

  • Buyers get answers while they are still deciding.
  • Less time typing the same replies.

How to set it up

Time to set up

Tools needed

  • Grok BotGrok Bot account needed

Steps

  1. Add the 3 bots in the stack: WhatsApper, WhatsApp Help Desk Assistant and Multilingual Support Inbox.
  2. Link the WhatsApp number you use for business.
  3. Write down your prices, hours and the answers you give most often.
  4. Ask for drafts on a handful of chats and approve each one by hand.
  5. Once the drafts sound like you, clear waiting chats once or twice a day.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Keep the approval step on for every message to a customer.
  • Keep one short list of standard answers so drafts stay accurate.
  • Keep it small at first: clear one day of chats before adding the other bots.
