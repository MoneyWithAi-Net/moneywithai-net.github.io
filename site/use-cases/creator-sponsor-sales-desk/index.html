Who it's for

  • Creators, podcasters and newsletter owners getting brand emails.
  • SaaS and affiliate marketers

What it does

  • Sponsor Inbound Scout: Flags real sponsor emails and screens out scams.
  • Sponsorship Negotiator: Researches the brand and drafts a rate reply.
  • CPM: Prices the deal from public view counts.
  • Invoice Hunter: Chases the invoice after the post runs.

How it helps your business

  • Legit sponsors get a fast, confident answer.
  • Pricing from real view counts avoids underselling.
  • Estimate math: 1 to 3 sponsored placements a month at $300 to $1,000 = about $300 to $3k a month.

Steps

  1. Add the 4 bots in the stack: Sponsor Inbound Scout, Sponsorship Negotiator, CPM and Invoice Hunter.
  2. Connect the tools they use: Gmail, Instagram and TikTok.
  3. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  4. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  5. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  6. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.
