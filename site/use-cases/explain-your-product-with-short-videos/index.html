Who it's for

  • Small brands and founders who need product videos.
  • Marketers who post short clips every week.
  • Service businesses that explain the same thing to every new customer.

What it does

  • Director: Plans, shoots and cuts a short video from your brief.
  • Video Editor: Makes shorts, titles, thumbnails and product videos from footage.
  • Clipper: Cuts clips and captioned GIFs for posts.

How it helps your business

  • Simple explainers and ads no longer need a paid edit each time.
  • Buyers get answers from a video instead of a call.

How to set it up

Time to set up
About 1 to 2 hours (our estimate)
Difficulty
Beginner

Tools needed

  • Grok BotGrok Bot account needed; video uses your Grok Bot usage

Steps

  1. Add the 3 bots in the stack: Director, Video Editor and Clipper.
  2. Write a short brief for one product or one common customer question.
  3. Approve the plan before it makes shots, then review the first cut scene by scene.
  4. Make a short and a thumbnail, then cut a few clips for posts.
  5. Keep your best brief as a template for the next video.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Start with the question customers ask most; that video pays back fastest.
  • Keep videos short and to one message.
  • Keep it small at first: one good explainer beats five rushed ones.
