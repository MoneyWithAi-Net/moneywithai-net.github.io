How It Makes Money

  • Owned audience plus sponsor income.
  • Bots cut writing time per issue.
  • Estimate math: 0 to 4 sponsor slots a month at $100 to $750 = about $0 to $3k a month.

Who It's For

  • Writers and niche experts.
  • Newsletter creators
  • Ecommerce brands

What It Does

  • Daily Newsletter Sender: Writes and sends the issue to your list.
  • Article and Newsletter Writer: Drafts long pieces from your sources.
  • Situation monitor: Turns your saves into a weekly roundup.
  • Sponsor Inbound Scout: Flags real sponsor emails.
  • Sponsorship Negotiator: Drafts rate replies.

How to Set It Up

Time to Set Up
About half a day (our estimate)
Difficulty
Intermediate
Tools Needed
  • Grok Bot: Grok Bot account needed
  • n8n: Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • Microsoft Excel: Paid plan
  • Microsoft Outlook: Paid plan
  • OpenAI: Pay as you go (API billed per token)
  • Google Docs: Free plan available
  • Gmail: Free with a Google account
  • Squarespace: Check the vendor's pricing
  1. Add the 5 bots in the stack: Daily Newsletter Sender, Article and Newsletter Writer, Situation monitor, Sponsor Inbound Scout and Sponsorship Negotiator.
  2. Connect the tools they use: Microsoft Excel, Microsoft Outlook, OpenAI, Google Docs, Gmail and Squarespace.
  3. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  4. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  5. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  6. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.
