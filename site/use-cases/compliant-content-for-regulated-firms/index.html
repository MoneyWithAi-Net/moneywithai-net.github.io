Who It's For

  • Marketers serving insurance, finance or health firms.
  • Marketers and small agencies
  • Founders writing their own copy

What It Does

  • Human Copywriter: Drafts human-sounding copy.
  • Crosscheck: Checks claims against sources.
  • Sentinel: Reviews for disclosure and claim issues.
  • Mailchimp Monthly Blast: Drafts the monthly member email.

How It Makes Money

  • Regulated firms pay for safe, steady marketing.
  • Fewer compliance surprises.
  • Estimate math: 1 to 3 clients at $1,000 to $1,700 = about $1k to $5k a month.

How to Set It Up

Time to set up
About one to two days (our estimate)
Difficulty
Advanced

Tools needed

  • Grok BotGrok Bot account needed

Steps

  1. Add the 4 bots in the stack: Human Copywriter, Crosscheck, Sentinel and Mailchimp Monthly Blast.
  2. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  3. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  4. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  5. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.
