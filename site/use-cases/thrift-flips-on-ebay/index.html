Flip Thrift Finds on eBay

E-Commerce $ Low E Sell a Service Beginner 1 Bot

Photograph an item and get an eBay draft with sold comps, a title and a price to review.

How It Works

Money Potential: $ Low E

Money tiers are our estimates (E) of relative earning potential, each with its assumption. They are not promises, income claims or advice. Results depend on your market, offer and effort.

How It Makes Money

  • You list more items per hour.
  • Sold comps help you price to sell, not to sit.

Who It's For

  • Resellers, collectors and anyone clearing out a garage.

Steps

  1. Add the bot and photograph one item.
  2. Check the comps and condition notes.
  3. Publish the listing yourself.

Guardrails

  • The bot drafts only; it never lists or messages buyers.
