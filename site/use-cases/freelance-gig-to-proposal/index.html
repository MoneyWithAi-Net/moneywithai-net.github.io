How It Makes Money

  • You answer high-fit gigs first, while competition is low.
  • Ready case studies and proposals shorten the sale.
  • Estimate math: 1 to 3 projects a month at $1,000 to $1,700 = about $1k to $5k a month.

Who It's For

  • Freelance designers, developers and marketers.
  • B2B agencies
  • Consultants and freelancers

What It Does

  • Contra Job Scraper: Checks Contra every six hours and logs new gigs.
  • Lead Vetting: Scores each gig against your fit rules.
  • Case Skeleton: Outlines a matching case study for you to fill.
  • Post Call Assistant: Drops to-dos and a follow-up after the call.
  • Post Call Proposal Builder: Turns call notes into a proposal deck.

How to Set It Up

Time to Set Up
About 2 to 4 hours (our estimate)
Difficulty
Beginner
Tools Needed
  • Grok Bot: Grok Bot account needed
  • n8n: Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • Email: Any email account you already use
  • Slack: Free plan available
  • Gmail: Free with a Google account
  • Google Drive: Free with a Google account (15 GB); more storage is paid
  • Google Slides: Free plan available
  • OpenAI: Pay as you go (API billed per token)
  1. Add the 5 bots in the stack: Contra Job Scraper, Lead Vetting, Case Skeleton, Post Call Assistant and Post Call Proposal Builder.
  2. Connect the tools they use: Email, Slack, Gmail, Google Drive, Google Slides and OpenAI.
  3. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  4. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  5. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  6. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.
