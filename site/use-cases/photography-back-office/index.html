Who it's for

  • Real estate, event and drone photographers
  • Service businesses with bookings
  • Event and photo businesses

What it does

  • Photo & Video Shoot Confirmations: Emails clients a forecast before outdoor shoots and drafts reschedules.
  • PhotoFix: Edits listing photos and files them in Drive or Dropbox.
  • Travel Expenses: Logs trip costs and exports a report.

How it helps your business

  • Fewer wasted trips and rained-out shoots.
  • Faster delivery and cleaner expense claims.
  • Estimate math: 5 to 12 admin hours saved a month at $20 to $50 an hour = about $100 to $600 a month.

How to set it up

Time to set up
About 1 to 2 hours (our estimate)
Difficulty
Beginner

Tools needed

  • Grok BotGrok Bot account needed
  • EmailAny email account you already use
  • Google DriveFree with a Google account (15 GB); more storage is paid
  • DropboxFree plan available

Steps

  1. Add the 3 bots in the stack: Photo & Video Shoot Confirmations, PhotoFix and Travel Expenses.
  2. Connect the tools they use: Email, Google Drive and Dropbox.
  3. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  4. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  5. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  6. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.
