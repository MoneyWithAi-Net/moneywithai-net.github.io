How It Makes Money

  • Project managers spend more time on site and less in the inbox.
  • Fewer dropped requests and late replies.
  • Estimate math: 8 to 30 office hours freed a month at $50 to $100 an hour = about $400 to $3k a month.

Who It's For

  • Commercial and industrial construction teams.
  • Builders and contractors
  • Construction project managers

What It Does

  • Construction Chief of Staff: Reads mail and calendar and routes work.
  • Inbox Triage: Sorts incoming email and DMs.
  • Notion Memory Keeper: Keeps decisions and context in one Notion page.

How to Set It Up

Time to Set Up
About 2 to 4 hours (our estimate)
Difficulty

Tools Needed
  • Grok Bot: Grok Bot account needed
  • Email: Any email account you already use
  • Google Calendar: Free with a Google account
  • Facebook: Free plan available
  • X: Free account; the X API is pay per use
  • LinkedIn: Check the vendor's pricing
  • Trello: Free plan available
  • Notion: Free plan available
  1. Add the 3 bots in the stack: Construction Chief of Staff, Inbox Triage and Notion Memory Keeper.
  2. Connect the tools they use, starting with Email, Google Calendar, Facebook, X, LinkedIn; the full list is below.
  3. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  4. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  5. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  6. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.
