Who it's for

  • Real estate agents who stay in touch with past buyers.
  • Appraisers and property consultants.
  • Homeowners who want to check their own assessment.

What it does

  • Property Tax Appeal: Compares the assessment with recent nearby sales and drafts the appeal and evidence summary under county rules.
  • Post-Call Assistant: Turns each client call into to-dos and a follow-up draft.

How it helps your business

  • Past clients get real help after the sale.
  • Drafting an appeal takes minutes instead of hours.
  • Estimate math: 2 to 5 appeals a month, 1 to 2 hours saved each at $25 to $50 an hour = about $100 to $500 a month.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Watch county deadlines; late appeals are usually refused.
  • Keep a copy of the evidence for the client.
  • Keep it small at first: try it on one past client who asked about their taxes.

Questions
