Who it's for

  • B2B consultants and founders
  • Small brands
  • Social media freelancers

What it does

  • LinkedIn Post Approval Flow: Drafts posts and publishes only after email approval.
  • Competitor Audience Watch: Tracks who engages with rivals' posts.
  • LinkedIn Desk: Vets incoming invitations.
  • LinkedIn Lead Outreach: Logs comments into Notion and drafts capped replies.

How it helps your business

  • Content builds trust; tracking turns it into meetings.
  • Competitor engagement shows who is shopping.
  • Estimate math: 1 to 2 extra clients a month = about $500 to $4k a month.

Steps

  1. Add the 4 bots in the stack: LinkedIn Post Approval Flow, Competitor Audience Watch, LinkedIn Desk and LinkedIn Lead Outreach.
  2. Connect the tools they use: Gmail, Google Sheets, LinkedIn, OpenAI, Signado and Notion.
  3. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  4. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  5. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  6. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.

Guardrails

  • Keep daily sending caps low and follow each platform's rules on automation.
  • Nothing is posted publicly without your OK.
  • Every message and document stays a draft until you approve it.

Results to Expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • Our estimate: $500 to $4k a month. Basis: 1 to 2 extra clients a month. Not a promise of income.
  • What you should see first: steady posts, lead list in Notion and booked calls.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Keep a human approval step before anything is posted.
  • Use images you own or have rights to.
  • Start with one customer segment and add more only when the flow runs cleanly.
