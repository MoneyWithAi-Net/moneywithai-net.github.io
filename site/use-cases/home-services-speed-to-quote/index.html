Who it's for

  • Landscapers, cleaners, roofers and other trades
  • Clinics and salons
  • Trades and home services

What it does

  • Voice Receptionist Call Booker: Answers calls and books appointments.
  • Speed-to-Lead Closer: Replies to web leads the moment they land.
  • Quote Follow Up Sequence: Sends quote follow-ups on day 3, 7 and 14.
  • Reply Desk: Drafts replies to reviews and messages.

How it helps your business

  • Fewer jobs lost to the next contractor who called back first.
  • Quotes that would have gone cold get a second look.
  • Estimate math: 2 to 6 extra jobs a month at $500 to $1,000 = about $1k to $6k a month.

Steps

  1. Add the 4 bots in the stack: Voice Receptionist Call Booker, Speed-to-Lead Closer, Quote Follow Up Sequence and Reply Desk.
  2. Connect the tools they use: Airtable, Google Calendar, Stripe, Gmail, Google Sheets and Email.
  3. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  4. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  5. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  6. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.

Guardrails

  • Check local rules on cold outreach, calls and texts before contacting anyone.
  • You need your own accounts for the connected tools; check their current plans first.
  • A person stays reachable for anything the bot is unsure about.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Tell callers they are speaking with an AI where required.
  • Test with real call scenarios first.
  • Start with one customer segment and add more only when the flow runs cleanly.
