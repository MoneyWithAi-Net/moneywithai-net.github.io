Who It's For

  • Solo creators and founders who publish daily
  • YouTube creators
  • Small media teams

What It Does

  • Content Idea Generator: Suggests ideas from your content and trends.
  • Scripter Orc: Writes scripts in your channel's style.
  • Shorty: Cuts Shorts from long videos.
  • X Posting Curator: Sources, hooks and fact-checks X posts.
  • Cross-Platform Publisher: Publishes to every platform at once.

How It Makes Money

  • Consistent output without hiring a team.
  • Fan tracking shows which posts build loyalty.
  • Estimate math: Audience growth that supports 1 to 3 sponsor deals = about $200 to $3k a month.

Steps

  1. Add the 6 bots in the stack: Content Idea Generator, Scripter Orc, Shorty, X Posting Curator, Cross-Platform Publisher and X Top 100 Fans (Weekly).
  2. Connect the tools they use: YouTube, Google Trends, Notion and PostBridge.
  3. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  4. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  5. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  6. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.
