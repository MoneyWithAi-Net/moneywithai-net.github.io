Who it's for

  • SaaS and B2B service founders
  • B2B agencies
  • Consultants and freelancers

What it does

  • Lookalike Scout: Lists similar companies and decision-makers.
  • Lead Enricher: Verifies emails and adds a recent hook.
  • Cold Email Campaign Team: Drafts a full cold email sequence with specialist models.
  • Deal Desk Autopilot: Flags stalled deals and drafts the next step.

How it helps your business

  • Lookalikes of a real customer convert better than broad lists.
  • Stalled deals get a nudge instead of going quiet.
  • Estimate math: 1 to 4 deals a month at $1,500 to $2,000 = about $1.5k to $8k a month.

Steps

  1. Add the 4 bots in the stack: Lookalike Scout, Lead Enricher, Cold Email Campaign Team and Deal Desk Autopilot.
  2. Connect the tools they use, starting with Treg, Weft, OneShot Agent, Google Sheets, Slack; the full list is below.
  3. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  4. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  5. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  6. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.

Guardrails

  • Keep daily sending caps low and follow each platform's rules on automation.
  • Check local rules on cold outreach, calls and texts before contacting anyone.
  • Every message and document stays a draft until you approve it.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Verify emails before sending to protect your domain.
  • Keep volume low and messages specific.
  • Start with one customer segment and add more only when the flow runs cleanly.
