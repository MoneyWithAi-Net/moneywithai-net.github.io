Who it's for

  • Owners paying for apps they use one feature of
  • Small teams without a developer
  • Founders who prototype before hiring

What it does

  • APP REPLICATOR: Helps you rebuild the one feature you use from a paid app on a free or cheap stack, and says when a rebuild is not worth it.
  • Grok Build: Runs the Grok Build command line tool on an agent computer for apps, code and deep research.
  • Tech Lead: Holds changes until the diff matches the claim and real tests pass.

How it helps your business

  • One less subscription for a feature you can build.
  • No developer bill for small tools.
  • Estimate math: one $20 to $100 a month app replaced = about $20 to $100 a month.

Steps

  1. Add the 3 bots in the stack: APP REPLICATOR, Grok Build and Tech Lead.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Rebuild one feature, not a whole product.
  • Keep backups of anything the tool stores.
  • Keep it small at first: run the new tool next to the old app for a few weeks.
