Who it's for

  • Small businesses with several vendor contracts.
  • Office managers and founders who sign up for tools.
  • Agencies paying for software on behalf of clients.

What it does

  • Contract Renewal Reminder: Sends Slack and email reminders before renewal dates.
  • Watchdog Receipts: Finds renewals and trials in your inbox.

How it helps your business

  • Unwanted renewals get cancelled in time.
  • Early reminders give you room to negotiate.

Steps

  1. Add the 2 bots in the stack: Contract Renewal Reminder and Watchdog Receipts.
  2. Create one sheet with each contract, its renewal date and notice period.
  3. Connect the inbox where invoices and renewal notices arrive.
  4. Run both once and check the list against your bank statement.
  5. Set the reminder lead time to match your longest notice period.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Record the notice period, not just the renewal date.
  • Ask for a better price before you cancel; many vendors offer one.
  • Keep it small at first: start with the ten biggest contracts.

Questions

What tools do I need to catch contracts before they auto-renew?

The 2 bots in the stack, plus the tools listed. Each tool may need its own plan.

Will the bots cancel contracts for me?

No. They remind you and list what is coming up. You cancel, renew or negotiate yourself.
