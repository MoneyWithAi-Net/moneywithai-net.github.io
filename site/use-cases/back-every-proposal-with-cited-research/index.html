Value tiers are our rough estimates (labelled Estimate) of relative value to the business, each with its assumption. They are not promises, income claims or advice. Results depend on your market, offer and effort.

Who it's for

  • Consultants and agencies that write proposals
  • Founders who pitch partners or investors
  • Freelancers who write client briefs

What it does

  • Research: Gives cited answers from primary sources and separates facts from interpretation.
  • Researchy: Fact-checks with live web search and gives sourced, dated answers.
  • Text-cleanup: Turns messy notes into one clean version in your saved tone, without adding facts.

How it helps your business

  • Proposals clients can check are easier to accept.
  • Less unpaid research time before each pitch.

How to set it up

Time to set up

Difficulty

Tools needed

  • Grok BotGrok Bot account needed

Steps

  1. Add the 3 bots in the stack: Research, Researchy and Text-cleanup.
  2. Pick one proposal you are writing now.
  3. Ask the 3 questions it must answer and open every source.
  4. Fact-check the numbers you plan to use.
  5. Clean up the section and compare it with your usual draft.

Tips and pitfalls

  • Quote the source, not the bot.
  • Check the date of each source when the topic moves fast.
  • Keep it small at first: research one section before a whole proposal.
