Who It's For

  • Freelance web designers and small agencies.
  • Anyone who can build and host simple sites for local trades.

What It Does

  • Local Business Website Hunter: Finds businesses with no website, drafts a demo site, a quote and a first message.
  • Lead Scout: Finds businesses that have a site but likely search gaps.
  • Site Research: Writes the evidence brief you attach to the pitch.

How It Makes Money

  • You sell a website build or a monthly care plan to each business that says yes.
  • A demo made from their own public content gives the prospect something concrete to react to.
  • Audit briefs turn a cold message into a specific, useful one.
  • Estimate math: 1 to 3 new site builds or care plans a month at $500 to $1,000 each = about $500 to $3k a month.

How to Set It Up

Time to set up
About 2 to 4 hours (our estimate)
Difficulty
Intermediate

Tools needed

  • Grok BotGrok Bot account needed

Steps

  1. Add the 3 bots in the stack: Local Business Website Hunter, Lead Scout and Site Research.
  2. Write a short brief: who you serve, what you sell and what each bot must never do.
  3. Run each bot once, in the order shown in the flow, and check every output by hand.
  4. Fix instructions where results were off, then run the whole flow end to end.
  5. Schedule the routine parts and keep an approval step before anything reaches a customer.
