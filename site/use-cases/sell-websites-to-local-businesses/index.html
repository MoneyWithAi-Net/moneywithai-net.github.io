Sell Websites to Local Businesses That Have None

Leads and Sales $$$ High E Sell a Service Intermediate 3 Bots

Find businesses with no site or a weak one, show them a demo, and pitch a build with the evidence in hand.

How It Works

Money Potential: $$$ High E

Our estimate. Assumes you charge per website build or a monthly care plan, so income grows with each client who says yes.

Money tiers are our estimates (E) of relative earning potential, each with its assumption. They are not promises, income claims or advice. Results depend on your market, offer and effort.

How It Makes Money

  • You sell a website build or a monthly care plan to each business that says yes.
  • A demo made from their own public content gives the prospect something concrete to react to.
  • Audit briefs turn a cold message into a specific, useful one.

Who It's For

  • Freelance web designers and small agencies.
  • Anyone who can build and host simple sites for local trades.

The Bot Stack

Local Business Website Hunter by @NewLongMoney

Finds businesses with no website, drafts a demo site, a quote and a first message.

Grok Add
Lead Scout by @itsryanlenk

Cost to Run E

A Grok Bot plan, plus an account on each tool the stack connects to: Grok. We show a price only when we have checked it on a public page, with the source and date below.

Steps

  1. Add the three bots and pick one city and one trade to start.
  2. Run Lead Scout and Website Hunter once and review the lists by hand.
  3. Have Site Research brief the best ten prospects.
  4. Edit the drafted messages, check local rules on cold outreach, and send them yourself.
  5. Schedule a weekly run once the first replies come in.

Guardrails

  • Every message and quote stays a draft until you approve it.
  • Do not reuse a business's photos or text in public without permission.
