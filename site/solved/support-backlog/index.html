What Changes for the Business

  • Every ticket arrives with an investigation and a drafted reply.
  • Refunds are prepared with evidence and wait for approval.
  • Help articles stay current as fixes ship.
  • Questions in other languages and after hours get answered, and unsure cases are escalated.

Where It Slips Today

  • Agents spend most of each ticket finding information.
  • The same question is answered from scratch every day.

The Setup

  1. Connect the queue. Your help desk, inbox or WhatsApp line.
  2. Give it your docs. Help articles, policies and refund rules.
  3. Draft, then approve. Agents approve drafts and the refund gate holds money decisions.
  4. Review quality weekly. A coach bot scores a sample and suggests fixes.

Start with the first step, run it for a few weeks, then add the next. Bots draft, you approve.
