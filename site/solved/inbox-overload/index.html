What Changes for the Business

  • Email is labelled and routine replies are drafted for approval.
  • Requests become tasks on your project board.
  • A morning brief lists what needs you today.
  • Notes and actions from meetings are filed automatically.

Where It Slips Today

  • The day goes on email instead of the work clients pay for.
  • Requests buried in threads are forgotten.

The Setup

  1. Label first. Let the triage bot sort mail into a few clear labels for a week before it drafts anything.
  2. Draft routine replies. Approve drafts for the common questions.
  3. Send requests to tasks. Connect your project tool so requests are filed with a due date.
  4. Add the morning brief. One short summary of what needs a decision today.

Start with the first step, run it for a few weeks, then add the next. Bots draft, you approve.
