  • Conversion tracking is checked and fixed.
  • Ad accounts get an audit with a ranked fix list.

Where It Slips Today

  • Ad money goes out with broken tracking.
  • Traffic arrives at a store with checkout problems.
  • Ads say what the owner thinks, not what customers care about.

The Setup

  1. Fix tracking first. Check that sales and leads are recorded correctly.
  2. Audit the accounts. Rank fixes in the ad account.
  3. Check the store. Mystery-shop the store and fix the top issues.
  4. Write from customers. Build ad angles from reviews.
