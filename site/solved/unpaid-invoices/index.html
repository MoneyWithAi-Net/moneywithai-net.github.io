What Changes for the Business

  • Every open invoice is in one list with its due date.
  • Reminders go out on schedule and get firmer step by step.
  • You see your cash position without opening the books.

Where It Slips Today

  • Chasing feels awkward, so it does not happen.
  • Late invoices are noticed only when cash is tight.

The Setup

  1. List open invoices. Export them to one sheet with amount, client and due date.
  2. Write the reminder ladder. A friendly nudge, a firmer note and a final notice in your own words.
  3. Approve each send. Reminders wait for your OK, so nothing goes to a client you have just spoken to.
  4. Mark paid. Update the sheet so reminders stop as soon as money arrives.

Start with the first step, run it for a few weeks, then add the next. Bots draft, you approve.
