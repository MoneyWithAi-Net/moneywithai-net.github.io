What Changes for the Business

  • Each call starts with a brief on attendees and open questions.
  • Deep product questions are answered from your own documents.
  • Recaps go out after each call.
  • Stalled deals are flagged with a drafted next step.

Where It Slips Today

  • Calls end with let me get back to you.
  • Follow-ups go out days late.
  • Deals go quiet and nobody notices.

The Setup

  1. Connect calendar and email. So every meeting gets a brief and a recap.
  2. Load product knowledge. Give the product expert your docs or code.
  3. Flag stalls. Let the deal desk flag deals with no next step.
  4. Review weekly. Check stalled deals and what buyers said.

Start with the first step, run it for a few weeks, then add the next. Bots draft, you approve.
