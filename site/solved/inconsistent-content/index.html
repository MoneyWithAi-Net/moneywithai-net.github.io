What Changes for the Business

  • A weekly content calendar is drafted for your approval.
  • Posts that already worked are refreshed for other networks.
  • Each new blog post becomes a set of social post drafts.
  • Thought-leadership posts are drafted from your own ideas and notes.

Where It Slips Today

  • Posting stops whenever client work gets busy.
  • Good posts are used once and forgotten.
  • Every post starts from a blank page.

The Setup

  1. Collect your best work. Point the bots at past posts and articles that did well.
  2. Draft the calendar. Let the content team draft a week ahead.
  3. Approve in one sitting. Review the week's drafts in one short session.
  4. Repurpose weekly. Bring back proven posts on networks where they have not run.

Start with the first step, run it for a few weeks, then add the next. Bots draft, you approve.
