What Changes for the Business

  • A daily list of public conversations where buyers ask for what you sell.
  • Local businesses that match your offer are listed with evidence.
  • Companies similar to your best customer are found and verified.
  • People unhappy with a competitor are spotted, with a helpful reply drafted.

Where It Slips Today

  • Outreach goes to bought lists that never reply.
  • Buyers ask for help in public and nobody from your business answers.
  • Prospecting stops whenever delivery gets busy.

The Setup

  1. Describe your best customer. Write down who buys, why and where they talk.
  2. Pick two sources. Start with public conversations and lookalikes of a real customer.
  3. Review the list daily. Approve the best prospects and the first message for each.
  4. Keep what works. Drop sources that bring no replies after a month.

Start with the first step, run it for a few weeks, then add the next. Bots draft, you approve.

FAQ

Is this cold outreach?

Partly. The safest sources are people asking for help in public. Keep volumes low and personal.

Does it send messages for me?

It drafts them. You send each one.

What should I measure?

Prospects contacted, replies and calls booked each week.
