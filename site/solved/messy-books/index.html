What Changes for the Business

  • Books are checked against bank and card records every month.
  • Receipts are logged and matched as they come in.
  • Expenses and travel claims are sorted and routed for approval.
  • A short cash summary shows where you stand.

Where It Slips Today

  • Errors found at year end take days to untangle.
  • Receipts get lost between the card and the books.

The Setup

  1. Forward receipts. Send receipts to one address so the scanner logs them.
  2. Run the monthly check. Audit the books against bank and card records and list the fixes.
  3. Route approvals. Team expenses and invoices go to the right person for sign-off.
  4. Hand over. Send your accountant the checked month and the open questions.

Start with the first step, run it for a few weeks, then add the next. Bots draft, you approve.
