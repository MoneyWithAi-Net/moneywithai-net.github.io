Is it a fit?

A good fit if

  • Solo agents and small offices that get leads from portals, forms and social.
  • Agents who lose leads during showings and weekends.
  • Agents who market their own listings and run out of evenings.

We will tell you it is not a fit if

  • You want cold texting or bought contact lists.
  • You want the bot to post or send with no human check.
  • Your leads arrive only in a closed system we cannot connect safely.

How we protect your accounts and data

  • We never ask for passwords. You connect your own email, calendar and listing tools, and can remove access any time.
  • Each connector gets read or draft access where that is enough. Nothing is sent or posted without your check.
  • Qualifying questions are the same for every lead and are written to avoid protected traits under fair housing rules; your broker confirms them.
  • Setups are built by Anthony Kieffer, a cybersecurity and GRC consultant (CISSP, CISM, AIGP). He has 15 years in cybersecurity and risk advisory. See security.

Setups follow the same method as our public security reviews: least-access connectors and your approval before anything leaves.

Does it work for rentals and sales?

Yes. Rentals use the rental lead desk. Buyer and seller leads use the inquiry scorer or the follow-up desk.

Will it text leads on its own?

No. It drafts and you send. We only set up messages to people who asked about a property, and opt-outs are honored at once.
