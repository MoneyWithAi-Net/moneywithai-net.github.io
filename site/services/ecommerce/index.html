What we can set up for you

We start with the one job that costs you the most time. Each setup below uses bots from our directory; you see exactly what they do before anything is connected.

Customer reply drafts

Drafts replies to customer emails and messages from your policies. You edit and send, and drafts leave out refunds or dates you did not give. We configure it draft-only; see its security review.

Built on: Reply Desk (security grade A), Shopify Bot (security grade D) · See the full use case

Order and stock watch

Alerts you to big orders, flags low stock and tracks disputes. It spends no money, and you handle each exception. We configure it draft-only; see its security review.

Built on: Big Order Slack Alert, Store Manager (security grade D) · See the full use case

Win-back emails

Finds lapsed customers who agreed to hear from you and drafts tailored win-back emails. You approve each batch before it sends.

Built on: Churn Win-Back Loop · See the full use case

Tracks rival prices and suggests your own from product costs. You decide every price change.

Is it a fit?

A good fit if

  • Shopify stores where the owner still answers support.
  • Stores with repeat products and a customer list worth winning back.
  • Owners who want one job handled before adding more.

We will tell you it is not a fit if

  • You want a bot to issue refunds or change prices on its own.
  • You want to email people who never agreed to hear from you.
  • Your order volume is still easy to handle by hand.

How we protect your accounts and data

  • We never ask for passwords. You connect your own Shopify and email accounts and can remove access any time.
  • Connectors get the least access the job needs, such as read-only orders for alerts.
  • We set refunds, price changes and emails to wait for your approval and never save a payment card to the bot.
  • Setups are built by Anthony Kieffer, a cybersecurity and GRC consultant (CISSP, CISM, AIGP). See security.

Setups follow the same method as our public security reviews: least-access connectors and your approval before anything leaves.

Questions

Is this only for Shopify?

Most listed store bots connect to Shopify. Customer replies and win-back emails work with any store that has an inbox and a customer list.

Will a bot change my prices?

No. It suggests prices with the data behind them, and you approve each change.

Where does customer data go?

It stays in your store and email accounts. We connect only what the job needs, and you can remove access any time.

What does it cost to run?

You pay each provider directly on your own accounts: a Grok Bot plan, the AI usage (tokens) it consumes and each connected tool, such as Slack. The Shopify bot team use case lists the prices we checked.

What does the setup cost?

$1,500 one-time setup fee. It covers one bot on your accounts, first runs tested together, a one-page runbook and 14 days of email help. AI usage (tokens) and tool subscriptions are not included: they are billed to you directly by each provider.
