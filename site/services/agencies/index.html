Is it a fit?

A good fit if

  • Small agencies where founders still handle the inbox and proposals.
  • Web studios running care plans across many client sites. See web design studios.
  • Automation agencies that want their own back office running first.

We will tell you it is not a fit if

  • You want bots to send client work with no human review.
  • The job needs client account access that the client has not approved.
  • You want a custom software build rather than one bot setup.

How we protect your accounts and data

  • We never ask for passwords. You connect your agency's accounts and can remove access any time.
  • Client accounts stay in the client's name. Bots get the narrowest access that does the job.
  • Every client deliverable and live site change passes an approval gate first.
  • Setups are built by Anthony Kieffer, a cybersecurity and GRC consultant (CISSP, CISM, AIGP). Read the Grok Bot security risks guide.

Setups follow the same method as our public security reviews: least-access connectors and your approval before anything leaves.

Questions

Will clients notice bots in the process?

They notice faster replies. Everything that reaches a client is approved by your team first.

Which job should we start with?

Usually the inbox, because it feeds every other step. We confirm the best first job on the call.

Does this replace our project tool?

No. The bot files tasks into tools you already use, such as Asana or Notion.

What does it cost to run?

You pay each provider directly on your own accounts: a Grok Bot plan, the AI usage (tokens) it consumes and each connected tool. The agency inbox use case lists the prices we checked.
