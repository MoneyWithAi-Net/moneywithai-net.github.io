Is it a fit?

A good fit if

  • Clinics, dental offices and therapy practices with a busy front desk.
  • Practices that miss calls after hours or at peak times.
  • Owners who want one job handled well before adding more.
  • Teams that can name one person to approve the bot's drafts.

We will tell you it is not a fit if

  • You want a bot to give medical advice or triage symptoms.
  • The job needs patient records and your tools are not approved for that data.
  • Nobody on staff can review drafts during the first two weeks.

How we protect your accounts and data

  • We never ask for passwords. You connect your own accounts and can remove access any time.
  • Patient health data stays out of the bot unless your own compliant tools and rules allow it. We check this on the call.
  • Each connector gets the least access the job needs, and every draft waits at an approval gate for staff.
  • Setups are built by Anthony Kieffer, a cybersecurity and GRC consultant (CISSP, CISM, AIGP). Read how we handle security.

Setups follow the same method as our public security reviews: least-access connectors and your approval before anything leaves.

Questions

Can the bot book into our practice software?

It books into a calendar or passes a request to staff. A direct link depends on your software, so we check it on the call.

Will the bot handle patient health information?

Not by default. It stays general unless your own compliant tools and rules allow that data. Confirm your local privacy rules with your own advisor.
