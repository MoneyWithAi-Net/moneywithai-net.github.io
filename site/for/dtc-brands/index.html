What Changes for the Business

  • Product images, short product videos and carousels are produced for each launch.
  • Competitor product drops are flagged the same day.
  • Ad angles come from real reviews and customer messages.
  • Each release gets a planned campaign across your channels.

Where It Slips Today

  • Launch creative is late, so launches go out half ready.
  • A competitor launches and you find out weeks later.
  • Ads guess at what customers care about.

The Setup

  1. Plan the launch. Give the campaign bot the release date and product details.
  2. Produce the creative. Generate product images, videos and carousels for review.
  3. Mine customer language. Feed reviews and messages to the ad angle bot.
  4. Watch competitors. List the rivals and marketplaces to monitor for new drops.

Start with the first step, run it for a few weeks, then add the next. Bots draft, you approve.
