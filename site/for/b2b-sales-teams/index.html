What Changes for the Business

  • Buying signals and lookalike accounts are surfaced with research attached.
  • Openers reference something the buyer actually said.
  • Every call gets a brief before and a logged follow-up after.
  • Old leads get a second look before they are written off.

Where It Slips Today

  • Reps research accounts by hand and run out of selling time.
  • Follow-ups depend on memory.
  • Old leads in the CRM are never worked again.

The Setup

  1. Define your best customer. Start from one great account and let the lookalike bot find similar companies.
  2. Add intent. Watch for buying signals and research those companies first.
  3. Prep and log calls. Briefs before every meeting and notes into the CRM after.
  4. Rework old leads. Clean the old list and reach out to the ones worth a call.

Start with the first step, run it for a few weeks, then add the next. Bots draft, you approve.
