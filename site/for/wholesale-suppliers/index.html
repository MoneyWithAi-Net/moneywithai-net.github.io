What Changes for the Business

  • A clean catalog with current prices is ready to send to buyers.
  • Retail and trade prices are suggested from your real product costs.
  • Supplier bills are captured and routed for approval before payment.
  • Late customer invoices get polite reminders on a schedule.

Where It Slips Today

  • Buyers order from an old price list.
  • Supplier costs rise and your prices do not.
  • Invoices go out and nobody follows up.

The Setup

  1. Keep one cost sheet. List every product with its current cost; the price bot suggests prices from it.
  2. Publish the catalog. Build a catalog with approved prices and send it to buyers.
  3. Control payables. Route supplier invoices for approval before they are paid.
  4. Chase receivables. Turn on polite reminders for overdue customer invoices.

Start with the first step, run it for a few weeks, then add the next. Bots draft, you approve.

FAQ

Does it change prices in my store?

No. It suggests prices and builds the catalog. You approve and update.

Can it negotiate with my suppliers?

It drafts negotiation emails with your numbers. You send them.

What should I measure?

Margin per product, days to get paid and bills paid late.
