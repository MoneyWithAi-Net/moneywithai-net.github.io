What Changes for the Business

  • Photos and notes from site turn into a written issue list the office can act on.
  • Daily notes are logged in one place, so the record is there when a client or inspector asks.
  • Bid and tender documents are ranked and checked against the format rules before you submit.
  • Office mail and calendar requests get routed to the right person instead of piling up.

Where It Slips Today

  • Issues found on site live in someone's camera roll until they become a costly surprise.
  • Tenders are found late or rejected on a format detail after hours of work.
  • The owner is the only one who knows where everything is.

The Setup

  1. Start with the daily log. Have site leads send photos and notes to one channel; the bot writes the daily log and the open issues list.
  2. Sort the office front door. Let an office chief of staff bot read the shared inbox and calendar and route each request to the right person.
  3. Build a bid desk. Feed tender portals and invitations into a bot that ranks fit and checks each submission against its rules.
  4. Keep subcontractor quotes comparable. Collect quotes in one sheet so they are compared line by line before you pick.

Start with the first step, run it for a few weeks, then add the next. Bots draft, you approve.
