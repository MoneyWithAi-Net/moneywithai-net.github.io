What Changes for the Business

  • Posts and emails are drafted and checked against your rules before review.
  • The rules that apply to you are explained, and official mail is sorted so nothing waits.
  • Client meetings produce tidy notes and follow-ups.
  • Staff find approved answers in a company knowledge base.

Where It Slips Today

  • Marketing stalls because every draft waits for a compliance read.
  • A filing deadline is remembered too late.
  • The same questions go to senior staff every week.

The Setup

  1. Write down your rules. List the claims, disclosures and wording your firm must follow.
  2. Draft and check. Marketing drafts pass a rules check before a person approves them.
  3. Sort official mail. Route notices and filing letters to one lane so nothing waits.
  4. Build the knowledge base. Collect approved answers and policies in one searchable place.

Start with the first step, run it for a few weeks, then add the next. Bots draft, you approve.

FAQ

Can a bot sign off marketing?

No. It flags likely issues so your reviewer works faster. The reviewer decides.

Which industries fit?

Firms with written marketing and filing rules, such as advice, insurance, legal and health practices.

What should I measure?

Posts published, review time per draft and deadlines met.
