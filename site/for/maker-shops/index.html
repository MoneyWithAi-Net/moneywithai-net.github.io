What Changes for the Business

  • Customer questions get consistent answers and quotes include your quantity discounts.
  • Quote requests are routed for your approval instead of sitting in the inbox.
  • Artwork and machine jobs are tracked from approval to pickup.

Where It Slips Today

  • Every quote is worked out from scratch.
  • Customers wait for an answer and order elsewhere.
  • Finished jobs wait for payment.

The Setup

  1. Write down your pricing rules. Materials, machine time and discounts in one sheet the quote bot uses.
  2. Route quote requests. Every request becomes a drafted quote you approve.
  3. Track jobs. Coordinate artwork approval and machine work in one place.
  4. Collect. Send approved invoices and reminders.

Start with the first step, run it for a few weeks, then add the next. Bots draft, you approve.
