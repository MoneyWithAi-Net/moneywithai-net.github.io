What Changes for the Business

  • Maintenance requests are triaged, dispatched and closed out with resident texts.
  • Rent deposits are matched to tenants and late or short payments are flagged.
  • Expenses are sorted for tax time and invoices bundled for your accountant.
  • Rental inquiries get fast replies and showings get booked.

Where It Slips Today

  • Maintenance requests arrive by text, email and phone and fall through the gaps.
  • Late rent is noticed weeks late.
  • Tax time means a weekend of receipts.

The Setup

  1. One intake for maintenance. Route every request to the triage bot, with your vendor list and urgency rules.
  2. Match rent weekly. Let the ledger bot read deposits and flag anything late or short.
  3. Answer vacancies. Turn on the rental lead desk for listings and showings.
  4. Hand a tidy year to your accountant. Keep expenses sorted and invoices bundled as you go.

Start with the first step, run it for a few weeks, then add the next. Bots draft, you approve.

Rules to Follow

  • The ledger bot never moves money. It reads and flags.
  • Emergencies such as gas, fire or flooding go straight to a person and the right service.

FAQ

Is this for a few units or a portfolio?

The listed bots were built for small landlords and the same routine works as the portfolio grows.

Can tenants text the bot?

The maintenance bot texts residents with updates. How requests come in is set up with you on the free call.

Does it replace property software?

No. It works alongside the sheets and tools you already use.
