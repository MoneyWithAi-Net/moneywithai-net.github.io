What Changes for the Business

  • Item photos become eBay drafts with a title, sold comps and a suggested price.
  • Photos are cleaned up before listing.
  • Prices are suggested from what each item cost you, so nothing sells at a loss.
  • Competitor price changes on the same products are flagged.

Where It Slips Today

  • Listing one item at a time takes longer than sourcing it.
  • Items are priced by guesswork and sit or sell too cheap.
  • Stock lives on one marketplace when buyers are on several.

The Setup

  1. Photograph and draft. Take photos as usual; the lister drafts the listing with comps for you to review.
  2. Keep a cost sheet. Record what you paid for each item so prices start from real costs.
  3. List in two places. Approve drafts for eBay and Marketplace together.
  4. Watch the market. Get alerts when competitor prices on your products move.

Start with the first step, run it for a few weeks, then add the next. Bots draft, you approve.

Rules to Follow

  • You review every listing and price before it goes live.

FAQ

Does it post listings automatically?

It drafts them. You publish after checking condition, title and price.

Is this only for eBay?

No. One listed setup drafts eBay and Marketplace listings together.

What should I measure?

Items listed per hour, days to sell and margin per item.
