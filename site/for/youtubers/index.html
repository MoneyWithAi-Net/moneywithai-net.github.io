What Changes for the Business

  • Transcripts, chapters, titles and descriptions are drafted after each upload, ready for your approval.
  • Real sponsor emails are separated from scams and answered with a rate based on your public view counts.
  • Each video becomes an article and a set of posts, so it keeps finding new viewers.
  • Invoices for sponsored videos get chased after the video runs, so money owed arrives.

Where It Slips Today

  • Hours after every upload go on chapters, descriptions and thumbnails instead of the next video.
  • Sponsor emails sit unanswered or get a guessed rate that leaves money on the table.
  • Videos stop bringing in viewers a few weeks after they go live.

The Setup

  1. Start with the after-upload work. Connect your channel so each new video gets a transcript, chapters, a title shortlist and a description draft.
  2. Sort the sponsor inbox. Let the sponsor desk flag genuine brand emails and draft a rate reply you approve.
  3. Turn videos into search pages. Publish an article for each video and refresh older pages when they slip.
  4. Collect what you are owed. Track each sponsored video and have a polite invoice reminder drafted after it runs.

Start with the first step, run it for a few weeks, then add the next. Bots draft, you approve.
