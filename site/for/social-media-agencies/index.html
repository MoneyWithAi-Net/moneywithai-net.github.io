What Changes for the Business

  • Weekly calendars are drafted for each client account.
  • Long videos are cut into short clips with captions.
  • Ghostwritten posts match each client's voice and get approved before posting.
  • Relevant trends become post ideas while they are fresh.

Where It Slips Today

  • Every new client adds hours of planning and drafting.
  • Trends are spotted too late to use.
  • Clip editing becomes the bottleneck.

The Setup

  1. Set each client's voice. Collect past posts and brand rules per client.
  2. Plan the calendar. Let the content team draft the week for approval.
  3. Add clips. Feed long videos to the clipping bots.
  4. Watch trends. Get daily trend ideas matched to each client.

Start with the first step, run it for a few weeks, then add the next. Bots draft, you approve.
