What Changes for the Business

  • Calls during the rush get answered and tables booked.
  • Chat orders become structured tickets for your point of sale.
  • Canceled orders and error charges in delivery portals are found and refund requests filed for you to approve.
  • Reviews get drafted replies each week.

Where It Slips Today

  • Nobody can answer the phone at peak service.
  • Delivery app errors are charged to you and never disputed.
  • Reviews pile up unanswered.

The Setup

  1. Cover the booking line. Give the voice agent your hours, table rules and booking sheet.
  2. Take chat orders. Connect the chat channel your guests already use and map the menu.
  3. Recover delivery money. Let the recovery bot read your merchant portals and draft refund requests.
  4. Reply to reviews. Approve the week's review replies in one sitting.

Start with the first step, run it for a few weeks, then add the next. Bots draft, you approve.
