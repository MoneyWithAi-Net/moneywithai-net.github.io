What Changes for the Business

  • Customer messages get drafted replies in your voice for you to approve.
  • Big orders trigger an alert, and fulfilment exceptions reach you instead of hiding in a dashboard.
  • Competitor price changes and launches are flagged so you can respond with real data.
  • Lapsed customers get win-back emails and VIP buyers get a thank-you.

Where It Slips Today

  • Support messages wait overnight and turn into refund requests.
  • Stock and order problems are found by the customer first.
  • One-time buyers never hear from the store again.

The Setup

  1. Cover customer replies. Connect the store inbox or chat so replies are drafted from your policies.
  2. Give each job one bot. Fulfilment, big-order alerts and pricing each get a bot, and you handle the exceptions.
  3. Watch the market. Track rival prices and launches and review the changes weekly.
  4. Win back buyers. Send approved win-back and VIP emails to customers who already bought.

Start with the first step, run it for a few weeks, then add the next. Bots draft, you approve.
