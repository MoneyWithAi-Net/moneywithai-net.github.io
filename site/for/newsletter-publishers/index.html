What Changes for the Business

  • Your saved links and notes become a draft issue each week.
  • Sponsor emails are screened and answered with a rate reply you approve.
  • Lapsed subscribers get a friendly win-back email.
  • Affiliate partners are recruited and checked for missing commissions.

Where It Slips Today

  • Writing the issue from scratch eats the day it is due.
  • Sponsor emails go unanswered because pricing takes research.
  • Readers stop opening and nobody notices until the list shrinks.

The Setup

  1. Feed it your sources. Save links and notes during the week; the roundup bot turns them into a draft issue.
  2. Set up a sponsor desk. Let the inbound scout flag real sponsors and draft rate replies.
  3. Win back quiet readers. Define a lapsed reader and approve a short win-back email.
  4. Grow through partners. Recruit affiliates and audit their commissions monthly.

Start with the first step, run it for a few weeks, then add the next. Bots draft, you approve.
