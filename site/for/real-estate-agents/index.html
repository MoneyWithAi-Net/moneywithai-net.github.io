What Changes for the Business

  • Rental and buyer inquiries get quick replies in your voice and a few qualifying questions.
  • Qualified leads get showings booked on your calendar.
  • Listing records become videos and carousels ready to post.
  • Listing photos are edited, labelled and filed by address.

Where It Slips Today

  • Leads from portals and marketplaces wait while you are at a showing.
  • Unqualified viewers fill the calendar.
  • Listing marketing waits until the weekend.

The Setup

  1. Answer leads first. Connect the inboxes and listing channels where inquiries arrive and set your qualifying questions.
  2. Book showings. Link your calendar so qualified leads can pick a time.
  3. Market each listing. Keep listings in one table so videos and carousels are made from the record.
  4. File the photos. Have listing photos edited and stored by address for every client delivery.

Start with the first step, run it for a few weeks, then add the next. Bots draft, you approve.
