Privacy

What this site collects, why, and how long we keep it. Last updated 24 Oct 2026 (listing form added).

Browsing

  • No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no ad or tracking scripts.
  • Your theme choice (light or dark), and which bots you already copied today, are saved only in your own browser.
  • Our host, Cloudflare, keeps standard technical logs to run and protect the site.

Forms

  • The suggest and request forms store only what you type: the bot name, link, request type and note.
  • The list your bot form stores: the X post or x.ai share link (without any query or fragment), your X handle, the bot name, money category, what it does, the tools it connects to, your one-line summary, your consent tick, your opt-in choice for showing the description or prompt, and the ownership code we show you. It has no email field. The code only proves the request came from your X account; we check it by hand and publish nothing until then. Your handle and the link are public on X already; if the listing is published they appear on it with credit to you.
  • Your email is optional. If you give it, we use it only to reply about that request. No newsletter, no marketing, never shared or sold.
  • Submissions are stored in Cloudflare (Workers KV) and deleted automatically after 180 days, or sooner once handled.
  • We do not store your IP address. To stop spam, submissions are rate-limited per network using a short-lived keyed hash of the network address (a secret key, not a plain hash), which is never saved with your submission.

Copy counts

  • When you press Add or Copy, we add one to that bot's public copy count. No name, account or cookie is involved.
  • To count each visitor once per bot per day, we keep a keyed hash of your network address, the bot and the date (a secret key, so it cannot be turned back into an address) for at most two days, then delete it. We never store your IP address.

Links out

Listings link to X, x.ai and other sites. Those sites have their own privacy rules.

Your requests

To see, fix or delete anything you sent, or to ask about privacy, use the request form. We review requests and act on removals within 7 days.
