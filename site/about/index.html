What This Is

A small, hand-checked list of AI bots and setups that help a business earn, save or recover money. Each listing points to a bot someone built and shared publicly.

Credit First

Every listing credits its creator and links to the original post. Summaries are our own words. A prompt appears here only if its creator opts in.

Public Facts Only

We list a bot's name, creator, tools, category and source link. We do not copy creators' prompts, descriptions or logos.

Honest Numbers

Any number carries a label: V verified by us, E our estimate, A the creator's own claim, with a source. Money tiers are estimates, not promises.

Creators

Want your prompt shown, a detail fixed or your listing removed? Use the request form, or the Report link on any listing. We act on removals within 7 days.
