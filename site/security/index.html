Before you connect your inbox, store or bank to a shared bot, see what it can read, what it can do and whether it asks first. 25 popular setups, graded A to D with one published method.

Biggest shared risk
All Grok Bots on one account share one cloud computer, its files and its logged-in sessions. A bot is not a security boundary.
First thing to do
Give each bot only the connectors its job needs, and require your OK before anything is sent, posted, paid or deleted.

How we score

Each review is a desk review that scores five factors from what the setup can read, do and ask. Reviews marked provisional are scored from our own listing; reading the official share page comes next and can change the grade. The total is out of 100: A 85 to 100, B 70 to 84, C 55 to 69, D below 55. A lower grade does not mean a bot is bad. It means it needs more access, so it needs more care.

FactorPointsWhat we look at
Data access25How much of your data it can read. Full marks for public web or pasted input only; fewer as it reaches inboxes, drives, CRMs, stores or money.
External actions25What it can do outside the chat. Full marks for drafts only; fewer as it can send, post, change records or spend.
Human approval25Whether the setup itself requires your OK before any external or irreversible action.
Shared environment15All Grok Bots on one account share one cloud computer, files and logged-in sessions. Fewer marks as the bot needs more sensitive logins.
Transparency10Whether the public setup states its job, its connectors and its limits clearly.

The method follows common practice for third-party risk and least privilege: limit what a tool can see, limit what it can do, and keep a person in the loop for actions that are hard to undo.

What these reviews are not

  • Not a live test. We read the public setup; we do not run the bot on real accounts.
  • Not a guarantee. Creators can change a shared setup after we review it. Each review shows its date.
  • Some reviews are provisional. 25 of 25 were scored from our own listing because the official share page was not read yet. An approval rule written in the creator's setup can raise the grade; we update each review once its share page is checked.
  • Not an endorsement by the creator or by xAI. Grades are our own view, credited to the method above.
