Description

Reads invoice PDFs with OCR, checks the fields and logs validated invoices to Google Sheets.

What It Does

  • Reads invoice PDFs with OCR.
  • Checks the fields.
  • Logs validated invoices to Google Sheets.
  • Works with Google Sheets and OpenRouter.
  • Leaves decisions with you: it prepares, checks and reports, and you act.

Who It's For

  • Freelancers
  • Small agencies
  • Service businesses

How to Set It Up

Time to Set Up
About 30 to 60 minutes (our estimate)
Difficulty
Beginner
Tools Needed
  • n8n: Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • Google Sheets: Free with a Google account
  • OpenRouter: Pay as you go or paid plan
  1. Read the creator's original page to confirm it fits your business.
  2. Import the template into n8n, either self-hosted or on n8n Cloud.
  3. Connect Google Sheets and OpenRouter when asked, using accounts you control.
  4. Give it your accounting tool, invoice rules and payment terms.
  5. Do a test run on a small sample and check every output by hand.
  6. Once results look right, set a schedule or trigger and keep an approval step for anything customer-facing.

Tips and Pitfalls

  • Check totals and tax before sending.
  • Keep a record of every automated invoice.
  • Templates can break when n8n or a connected app changes; re-test after updates.

Questions

Is the Invoice OCR Checker template free?

The template is free on n8n's gallery. n8n can be self-hosted for free or used on a paid n8n Cloud plan, and connected apps may charge for their own use.

Can it send invoices automatically?

It can draft or send based on your rules. Review the first batches before letting it run unattended.

How long does it take to set up Invoice OCR Checker?

Our estimate is 30 to 60 minutes for a first working run, including connecting Google Sheets and OpenRouter. Tuning it to your business takes a few more runs.
