What it does

  • Reads invoice PDFs, including scans, with OCR.
  • Turns the text into clean fields with an AI model.
  • Splits out each line item with its own key.
  • Updates a Google Sheet with the results.

Who it's for

  • Freelancers who type invoice data into a sheet
  • Small agencies with many supplier PDFs
  • Service firms receiving scanned invoices

Set it up 5 steps

  • n8n – Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • Google Sheets – Free with a Google account
  • OpenRouter – Pay as you go or paid plan
  1. 1Open the original with Get it and copy or download the invoice OCR workflow.
  2. 2Import it into your n8n editor and add your own credentials for Google Sheets and OpenRouter.
  3. 3Swap the sample IDs, addresses and text in the invoice OCR workflow for your own, and read each AI prompt node.
  4. 4Run it on one invoice you know well and compare every field and line item in the sheet with the PDF.
  5. 5Switch the invoice OCR workflow on only after a clean test.
Worked exampleLogging a supplier's invoice

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You select a supplier's PDF invoice in the workflow.
  2. OCR reads the text and the AI step turns it into vendor, date, totals and line items.
  3. Each line item lands in its own row in your Google Sheet with a unique key.
  4. You compare the sheet total with the PDF and find they match.
  5. You run the next invoice the same way.

Questions

Does it work with scanned invoices?

Yes, that is what the OCR step is for. Poor scans read less reliably, so check the numbers.

What ends up in Google Sheets?

The invoice fields and one row per line item, each with a unique key for tracking.

Is Invoice OCR Checker free to use?

The template is free. You pay for n8n if you use its cloud and for AI model usage.

Does it pay invoices?

No. It reads and logs them. Payment stays with you.

How long does it take to set up Invoice OCR Checker?

Our estimate is 30 to 60 minutes, including a test invoice.
