Motivated Seller Finder

Finds likely motivated sellers in your market from property data before others notice them.

Set it up with the steps on this page.

What it does

  • Searches property data in your market.
  • Looks for signals that an owner may sell.
  • Ranks properties with the reason for each.
  • Leaves outreach to you.

Who it's for

  • Listing agents prospecting a farm area
  • Investors looking for off-market homes
  • Small brokerages building seller lists

Set it up 4 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • ZillapiZillapi – Check the vendor's pricing
  1. 1In Grok Bot, create a new bot whose one-line job is to find likely motivated sellers in my market from property data.
  2. 2Write its instructions in your own words, covering the area, the seller signals to look for, how to rank properties, the reason to show for each, and that it never contacts anyone.
  3. 3Set up access to the Zillow data service with your own account.
  4. 4Run it on an area you know and check whether its top picks make sense.
Worked exampleProspecting one neighborhood

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You set the area to one neighborhood and the signals to long ownership and recent price drops.
  2. The bot returns a ranked list of properties with reasons.
  3. You remove two that are already listed with another agent.
  4. You prepare a short letter for the top ten.
  5. You check do-not-mail and do-not-call rules before sending.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • A ranked list of possible sellers with a reason for each.
  • Many owners will not want to sell.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Pick a small area first.
  • Write respectful, low-pressure outreach.
  • Check contact rules before calling.
