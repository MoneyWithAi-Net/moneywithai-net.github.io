How to Set It Up

Time to Set Up

Difficulty

Tools Needed

  1. Read the creator's original page to confirm it fits your business.
  2. Rebuild it in Grok Bot or your own agent by following the creator's description.
  3. Connect Zillapi when asked, using accounts you control.
  4. Give it your farm area, price bands and the kind of seller or buyer you want.
  5. Do a test run on a small sample and check every output by hand.
  6. Once results look right, set a schedule or trigger and keep an approval step for anything customer-facing.

Questions

Is Motivated Seller Finder free to use?

It is free to add from the creator's listing, but you need a Grok Bot account. Connected tools such as Zillapi may need their own plans.

Is it legal to contact property owners this way?

Rules vary by country and state. The bot helps find and sort leads; you are responsible for consent and do-not-call compliance.

How long does it take to set up Motivated Seller Finder?

Our estimate is 30 to 60 minutes for a first working run, including connecting Zillapi. Tuning it to your business takes a few more runs.
