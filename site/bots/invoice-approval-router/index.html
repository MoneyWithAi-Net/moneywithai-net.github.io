What it does

  • Takes PDF invoices from email, Drive or a web form.
  • Extracts invoice data with OpenAI.
  • Emails an approver to approve or reject.
  • Stores approved invoices in Google Sheets.

Who it's for

  • Finance teams that want sign-off on every invoice
  • Small businesses with several approvers
  • Operations managers keeping invoice records

Set it up 5 steps

  • n8n – Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • Gmail – Free with a Google account
  • Google Drive – Free with a Google account (15 GB); more storage is paid
  • Google Sheets – Free with a Google account
  • OpenAI – Pay as you go (API billed per token)
  1. 1Open the original with Get it and copy or download the invoice approval workflow.
  2. 2Import it into your n8n editor and add your own credentials for Gmail, Google Drive, Google Sheets and OpenAI.
  3. 3Swap the sample IDs, addresses and text in the invoice approval workflow for your own, and read each AI prompt node.
  4. 4Send a test invoice to the intake inbox, approve it from your own email and check the row in Sheets.
  5. 5Switch the invoice approval workflow on only after a clean test.
Worked exampleA supplier invoice that needs sign-off

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. A supplier emails a PDF invoice.
  2. The workflow extracts the vendor, number, date and total.
  3. It emails the office manager to approve.
  4. The manager approves after checking the order.
  5. The invoice is logged in Google Sheets as approved.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • Every invoice approved by a person before it is recorded.
  • Approvers need to answer emails promptly.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Name one approver per cost type.
  • Check extracted totals in the first week.
  • Keep rejected invoices in a separate tab.
