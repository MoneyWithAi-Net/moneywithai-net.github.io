What it does

  • Spots X users asking for your kind of service.
  • Researches each one.
  • Writes opening messages and later nudges.
  • Tracks leads in HubSpot or a sheet.

Who it's for

  • Freelancers who find clients on X
  • Small agencies
  • Consultants

Set it up 5 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • HubSpot – Connect only if you use it
  • X – Connect only if you use it
  1. 1Read the bot page to check it fits your work.
  2. 2Add it from the share link.
  3. 3Connect HubSpot and X if you want it to use them.
  4. 4Run it once on a small, real task.
  5. 5Review the result and adjust your instructions.
Worked exampleFinding copywriting clients

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You tell Lead Scout you write landing pages for startups.
  2. It finds founders on X asking for copy help.
  3. It researches each founder's product.
  4. It drafts a first message and a follow-up for each.
  5. It logs them in your sheet, and you send the first messages.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • A tracked list of people asking for your service.
  • Some requests will already be filled.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Reply quickly.
  • Make the first message about them.
  • Keep the tracker current.
