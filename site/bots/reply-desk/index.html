Reply Desk

Drafts replies to customer emails, messages and public reviews for local service businesses. You send them.

What it does

  • Takes a customer email, text message or public review you paste in.
  • Drafts a reply you can edit and send.
  • Leaves out prices, dates and commitments it was not given.
  • Never sends a message or posts a reply itself.

Who it's for

  • Local service owners who answer customers between jobs
  • Restaurants and hotels that reply to public reviews
  • Small shops that want calmer replies to complaints

Set it up 6 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • Email – Any email account you already use
  1. 1Read the original and check it fits.
  2. 2Add it from the share link.
  3. 3Connect Email.
  4. 4Give it your review profiles and the tone you want replies in.
  5. 5Test on a small sample.
  6. 6Schedule only after it looks right; keep approval for customer-facing sends.
Worked exampleAnswering a two-star review

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. A customer leaves a two-star review about a late arrival.
  2. You paste the review into Reply Desk.
  3. It drafts an apology that addresses the delay without inventing a discount.
  4. You add the real reason for the delay and an offer you are comfortable making.
  5. You post the reply yourself from your review account.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • Replies written faster and in a steadier tone, especially for hard messages.
  • Facts such as prices and dates still come from you.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Keep your price list and policies open while you edit.
  • Reply to negative reviews once, publicly, then move it to private contact.
  • Read every draft aloud before you send it.

Questions

Does Reply Desk post or send replies?

No. It only drafts. You review and send every reply.

Will it make up prices or dates?

It is designed not to. Add any prices, dates or offers yourself.

What kinds of messages can it answer?

Emails, DMs and online reviews sent to local service firms.

Is Reply Desk free to use?

It is free to add from the share link, but you need a Grok Bot account.

How long does it take to set up Reply Desk?

Our estimate is 15 to 30 minutes, including a few practice replies in your own tone.
