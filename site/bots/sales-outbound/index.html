Sales Outbound

Description

Researches prospects on your schedule, logs them in Google Sheets and drafts follow-up emails in Gmail for you to send.

What It Does

  • Researches prospects on your schedule.
  • Logs them in Google Sheets.
  • Drafts follow-up emails in Gmail for you to send.
  • Works with Gmail and Google Sheets.
  • Keeps you in charge: review its output before anything reaches a customer or goes public.

Who It's For

  • B2B agencies
  • Consultants and freelancers
  • Founders doing their own outreach

How to Set It Up

Time to Set Up
About 30 to 60 minutes (our estimate)
Difficulty
Beginner
Tools Needed
  • Grok Bot: Grok Bot account needed
  • Gmail: Free with a Google account
  • Google Sheets: Free with a Google account
  1. Read the creator's original post to confirm it fits your business.
  2. Rebuild it in Grok Bot or your own agent by following the creator's description.
  3. Connect Gmail and Google Sheets when asked, using accounts you control.
  4. Give it your ideal customer, your offer and the signals that make a prospect timely.
  5. Do a test run on a small sample and check every output by hand.
  6. Once results look right, set a schedule or trigger and keep an approval step for anything customer-facing.

Tips and Pitfalls

  • Verify emails before sending to protect your domain.
  • Keep volume low and messages specific.
  • Follow anti-spam laws and each platform's rules.

Questions

Is Sales Outbound free to use?

It is free to add from the creator's listing, but you need a Grok Bot account. Connected tools such as Gmail and Google Sheets may need their own plans.

Will it send cold emails for me?

Most setups find and research prospects and draft messages. Keep sending under your control and within anti-spam rules.

How long does it take to set up Sales Outbound?

Our estimate is 30 to 60 minutes for a first working run, including connecting Gmail and Google Sheets. Tuning it to your business takes a few more runs.
