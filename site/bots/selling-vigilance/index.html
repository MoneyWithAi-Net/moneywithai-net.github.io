Worked exampleCataloguing a display case

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You photograph a case of twelve items in the shop.
  2. For each one you say the model, condition and price.
  3. It assigns IDs and makes a photo card per item.
  4. It saves a backup after the first six items.
  5. It produces a priced PDF you print for the counter.
