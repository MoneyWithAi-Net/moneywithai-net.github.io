Original template: Automated Competitor Price Monitoring with Bright Data & n8n by Yaron Been

What it does

  • Checks competitor prices.
  • Logs them in Sheets.
  • Emails on changes.
  • Keeps a price history.

Who it's for

  • DTC brands
  • Ecommerce marketers
  • Product teams

Set it up 6 steps

  • n8n – Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • Gmail – Free with a Google account
  • Google Sheets – Free with a Google account
  1. 1Open the original with Get it and copy or download the price monitor workflow.
  2. 2Import it into your n8n editor and add your own credentials for Bright Data, Google Sheets and Gmail.
  3. 3Swap the sample IDs, addresses and text in the price monitor workflow for your own, and read each AI prompt node.
  4. 4Add the competitor URLs and the sheet the workflow writes to.
  5. 5Run it on two product pages and check the logged prices.
  6. 6Switch the price monitor workflow on only after a clean test.
Worked exampleA rival cuts a price

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You list five competitor product pages.
  2. The workflow checks them every morning.
  3. It sees one rival dropped a blender's price.
  4. It logs the price in the sheet and emails you.
  5. You check the page and run a small offer.
