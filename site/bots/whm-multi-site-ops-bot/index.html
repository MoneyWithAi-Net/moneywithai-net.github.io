Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. The bot checks a server hosting thirty client sites.
  2. It finds the disk is almost full.
  3. It notes two sites with expiring certificates.
  4. It proposes clearing old backups and renewing the certificates.
  5. You approve the renewals and clear the backups yourself.
