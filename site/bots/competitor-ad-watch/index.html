Competitor Ad Watch

Watches competitors' ads through Adlicio and posts new creatives and angles to Slack.

Set it up with the steps on this page.

Price
Free to build in Grok Bot. You need a Grok Bot account and an Adlicio account; check its pricing.
Time
About 15 to 30 minutes (our estimate)
Level
Beginner
Apps
AdlicioAdlicioSlack

By daniel-ddtech · Are you the creator? · Report

What it does

  • Watches competitor ads.
  • Spots new creatives.
  • Notes angles.
  • Posts to Slack.

Who it's for

  • Ecommerce brands
  • Ad agencies
  • Marketing teams

Set it up 4 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • AdlicioAdlicio – Check the vendor's pricing
  • Slack – Free plan available
  1. 1In Grok Bot, create a new bot whose one-line job is to check Adlicio for new ads from the competitors I list and post them to Slack with a short note.
  2. 2Write its instructions in your own words, covering the competitors, how often to check, what to include in each note and that it never shares ads outside the team.
  3. 3Connect Adlicio and a Slack channel.
  4. 4Run it once and check the ads it found.
Worked exampleA rival's new ad angle

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You list three competitor brands.
  2. The bot checks Adlicio for their new ads.
  3. It finds one rival testing a money-back angle.
  4. It posts the ad and a one-line note to Slack.
  5. Your team discusses whether to test a guarantee.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • A steady view of rivals' ads.
  • Seeing an ad does not show its results.

Good to know

  • Watch a few rivals.
  • Note angles, not copy.
