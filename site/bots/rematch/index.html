What it does

  • Goes through your inbox and task list for quiet threads.
  • Finds leads that stopped replying and loops you never closed.
  • Drafts a follow-up for each one you can edit first.
  • Sends nothing until you read the draft and say go.

Who it's for

  • Sales and account owners with leads that stopped replying
  • Small teams that lose track of open loops in email and tasks

Set it up 4 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • Your email and task listYour email and task list – The bot needs access to the threads it checks
  1. 1Read the bot page to confirm it can reach your email and task list.
  2. 2Add it from the share link.
  3. 3Set the age that makes a thread count as quiet, such as seven days.
  4. 4Approve the first five drafts by hand.
Worked exampleWeekly quiet-thread sweep

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You add the bot and set the quiet period.
  2. Rematch lists five threads that went silent.
  3. You edit one draft, then say go on that one only.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

Good to know

  • Read each draft before you say go, especially on pricing.
  • Give the bot only the mailboxes you want it to read.
