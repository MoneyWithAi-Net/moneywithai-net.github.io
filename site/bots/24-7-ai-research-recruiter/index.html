Keeps a hard-to-fill hiring pipeline moving around the clock: it sources AI research candidates, follows up on replies and adds them to Greenhouse.

What it does

  • Sources candidates on LinkedIn Recruiter.
  • Checks for replies and follows up.
  • Adds candidates to Greenhouse.
  • Monitors the hiring pipeline.

Who it's for

  • Recruiters hiring AI researchers
  • Startups without a recruiting team
  • Agencies filling technical roles

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • LinkedIn RecruiterLinkedIn Recruiter – Paid LinkedIn seat
  • GreenhouseGreenhouse – Your applicant tracking system; paid plan

Worked exampleFilling an applied research role

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You give the bot the role and must-have skills.
  2. It finds candidates on LinkedIn Recruiter with relevant papers and roles.
  3. It sends your approved outreach and checks for replies overnight.
  4. Two candidates reply with interest.
  5. It adds them to Greenhouse and you book screening calls.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • A pipeline that keeps moving overnight and on weekends.
  • Top candidates still need a personal touch.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Write outreach in your own voice.
  • Respect LinkedIn's limits.
  • Review candidates before they enter Greenhouse.
