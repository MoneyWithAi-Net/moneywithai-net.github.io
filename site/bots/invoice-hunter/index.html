Invoice Hunter

Finds invoices in your email, pulls out the key details and bundles them by month for your accountant, after you approve.

What it does

  • Searches Gmail for invoice PDFs from the month you choose.
  • Reads vendor, date, amount and invoice number from each one.
  • Packs the month into a folder with a CSV and an index.
  • Waits for your approval before anything goes to your accountant.

Who it's for

  • Freelancers whose accountant asks for invoices every month
  • Small agencies paying many software and contractor bills
  • Service businesses that get most bills as email PDFs

Set it up 5 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  1. 1Read the original and check it fits.
  2. 2Add it from the share link.
  3. 3Give it your accounting tool, invoice rules and payment terms.
  4. 4Test on a small sample.
  5. 5Schedule only after it looks right; keep approval for customer-facing sends.
Worked exampleClosing out a month for the accountant

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. On the first working day of the month, you ask Invoice Hunter for last month's invoices.
  2. It finds the PDFs in Gmail and lists vendor, date, amount and number for each one.
  3. You spot a software invoice that was a personal purchase and remove it.
  4. It packs the rest into the month's folder with the CSV and index.
  5. You approve, and the bundle goes to your accountant.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • A month's invoices gathered in one folder with a CSV, instead of an evening of inbox searching.
  • Invoices that arrive as links or paper still need to be added by hand.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Run it on a month you already closed first and compare totals.
  • Remove personal purchases before you approve the bundle.
  • Agree the CSV columns with your accountant once, then keep them.

Ask a question first

Questions

Does Invoice Hunter send anything without asking?

No. It prepares the monthly bundle and waits for your approval before anything goes to your accountant.

What details does it pull from each invoice?

The vendor, the invoice date, the amount and the invoice number, collected into a CSV with an index.

Is Invoice Hunter free to use?

It is free to add from the share link, but you need a Grok Bot account.

What if an invoice is a scan or a photo?

It works from invoice files in your email. Forward scans as PDF attachments and check the extracted figures, since poor scans read less reliably.

How long does it take to set up Invoice Hunter?

Our estimate is 15 to 30 minutes: connect Gmail, run it on a past month and compare the totals with your records.
