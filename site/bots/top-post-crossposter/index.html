Set it up 5 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • X – Free account; the X API is pay per use
  • Threads – Free plan available
  • LinkedIn – Check the vendor's pricing
  • Bluesky – Free plan available
  • PostBridge – Check the vendor's pricing
  1. 1Read the creator's post with Get it to see how they run it.
  2. 2In Grok Bot, create a new bot whose one-line job is to find my best X posts and refresh them for Threads, LinkedIn and Bluesky through PostBridge.
  3. 3Write its instructions in your own words, covering how to pick top posts, how to adapt each platform's version, how many to post a week and that you review every post.
  4. 4Connect X and PostBridge, and keep posting behind your review.
  5. 5Run it for one post and read all three versions.
Worked exampleBringing a hit post to LinkedIn

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. The bot finds your ten most liked X posts this year.
  2. It picks one about pricing lessons.
  3. It rewrites it with a longer opening for LinkedIn.
  4. It shortens it for Threads and Bluesky.
  5. It queues all three in PostBridge for your review.
