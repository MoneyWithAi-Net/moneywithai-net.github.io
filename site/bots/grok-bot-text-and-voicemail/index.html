Grok Bot Text & Voicemail

A front desk for texts and voicemails sent to a Google Voice number: sorts them by intent and urgency in Gmail and drafts replies for you.

Get it↗

Opens the official Grok Bot page. Press Add to Grok Bot and read the terms before you accept.

Price
Free to add from the share link. You need a Grok Bot account. Google Voice is free for personal use in the US; business plans may charge.
Time

Level
Beginner

What it does

  • Reads texts and voicemails.
  • Sorts by intent.
  • Flags urgent ones.
  • Drafts replies.

Who it's for

  • Small businesses
  • Side hustlers
  • Anyone guarding a personal number

  • Gmail – Connect only if you use it

Worked exampleA missed booking call

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. A customer leaves a voicemail asking to move Friday's appointment.
  2. Google Voice sends the transcript to Gmail.
  3. The bot marks it as a booking change and urgent.
  4. It drafts a text offering two new times.
  5. You pick one time and send it.
