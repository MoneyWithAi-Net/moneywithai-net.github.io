What it does

  • Spots trading local firms that have no site of their own.
  • Builds a demo site from each business's public content.
  • Prepares a quote PDF and an outreach message.
  • Holds everything for your approval.

Who it's for

  • Freelance web designers looking for first clients
  • Small agencies that sell starter websites
  • Marketing consultants serving trades and shops

Set it up 5 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  1. 1Read the original and check it fits.
  2. 2Add it from the share link.
  3. 3Give it the area and trades you want, plus your website offer and price range.
  4. 4Test on a small sample.
  5. 5Schedule only after it looks right; keep approval for customer-facing sends.
Worked examplePitching a mobile mechanic with no site

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You ask for active businesses in your town with no website.
  2. It finds a mobile mechanic with good reviews but only a social page.
  3. It builds a demo from the public reviews and services, a quote PDF and a short message.
  4. You fix the service area, adjust the price and approve the message.
  5. You send it and keep the demo private until the owner replies.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • Pitches that show a business its own site before it pays.
  • Many businesses will say no; the demo makes the yes easier to reach.

Good to know

  • Set your price before the bot builds any quote.
  • Keep demos private until the owner agrees.
  • Check that the business is still trading before you pitch.

Questions

Does it contact businesses automatically?

No. The quote and message wait for your approval before anything goes out.

Can I publish the demo site?

Only with the business owner's permission. Until then, keep it private.

How does it know a business is active?

From public signals such as recent reviews and listings. Check them yourself before pitching.
