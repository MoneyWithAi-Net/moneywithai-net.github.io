What it does

  • Sorts roofing inquiries by urgency and readiness.
  • Drafts SMS and email replies for you to send.
  • Prepares quick-quote hand-offs for the estimator.
  • Keeps public business details consistent and the owner's private details out.

Who it's for

  • Roofing companies with more inquiries than office time
  • Exterior and gutter contractors
  • Family-run trades firms protecting the owner's privacy

Set it up 6 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • SMS – Free plan available
  • Email – Any email account you already use
  1. 1Read the original and check it fits.
  2. 2Add it from the share link.
  3. 3Connect SMS and Email.
  4. 4Give it your services, service area, typical price ranges and hours.
  5. 5Test on a small sample.
  6. 6Schedule only after it looks right; keep approval for customer-facing sends.
Worked exampleThree inquiries after a windy weekend

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. Three web inquiries arrive on Monday morning.
  2. The bot ranks one as urgent because the customer reports a leak.
  3. It drafts a text and an email for each, using your business phone number only.
  4. It prepares a quick-quote handoff for the urgent job with the address and photos.
  5. You edit one draft, send all three, and pass the handoff to your estimator.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • Faster, more consistent replies to roofing inquiries and cleaner hand-offs to the estimator.
  • Other trades will need to rewrite the qualifying questions.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Load your public business name, address and phone exactly as listed online.
  • Keep personal numbers out of its instructions.
  • Send urgent leak calls to a person by phone.

Questions

Who made Legend Lead Ops?

A roofing company, Legend Roofing, built it for its own lead intake and shared it as a template.

Does it send messages for me?

It drafts SMS and email replies. You review and send them.

What are the privacy gates?

Rules that keep the business's public details consistent and keep the owner's personal contact details out of customer messages.

Is Legend Lead Ops free to use?

It is free to add from the share link, but you need a Grok Bot account.

Does it only work for roofers?

It was built for roofing, so other trades should adjust the qualifying questions and quote handoff first.
