  • Reads your tools.

Who it's for

  • Marketing leads

Set it up 4 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • Gmail – Free with a Google account
  • Slack – Free plan available
  • Notion – Free plan available
  • Google Analytics – Free
  • HubSpot – Free CRM available; paid tiers
  • Google Ads – Paid plan
  1. 1In Grok Bot, create a new bot whose one-line job is to collect current marketing context from my tools and write a short brief before planning decisions.
  2. 2Write its instructions in your own words, covering which sources to read, how far back to look, the sections of the brief and which numbers to quote exactly.
  3. 3Connect Gmail, Slack, Notion, Google Analytics, HubSpot and Google Ads, using read access where possible.
  4. 4Ask for one brief and compare each figure with the source dashboards.
Worked exampleBefore a monthly planning meeting

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You ask for a fresh marketing brief on Monday morning.
  2. It reads last month's traffic, new HubSpot deals and ad spend.
  3. It adds key Slack threads about a product delay.
  4. The brief says paid search drove most new deals while social fell.
  5. You use it to set next month's budget split.
