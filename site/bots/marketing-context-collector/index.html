Questions

Is Marketing Context Collector free to use?

It is free to add from the creator's listing, but you need a Grok Bot account. Connected tools such as Gmail, Slack and Notion may need their own plans.

How often does it report?

Most setups run on a schedule you choose, such as daily or weekly.

How long does it take to set up Marketing Context Collector?

Our estimate is 1 to 2 hours for a first working run, including connecting Gmail, Slack and Notion. Tuning it to your business takes a few more runs.
