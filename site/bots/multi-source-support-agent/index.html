Description

A support agent that answers from several knowledge bases and escalates to a human when unsure.

What It Does

  • A support agent that answers from several knowledge bases.
  • Escalates to a human when unsure.
  • Works with Gmail, Ollama, Slack, Supabase and Wikipedia.
  • Keeps you in charge: review its output before anything reaches a customer or goes public.

Who It's For

Tips and Pitfalls

  • Require code review for every fix.
  • Start with small, well-defined bugs.
  • Templates can break when n8n or a connected app changes; re-test after updates.

Questions

Is the Multi Source Support Agent template free?

The template is free on n8n's gallery. n8n can be self-hosted for free or used on a paid n8n Cloud plan, and connected apps may charge for their own use.

Will it change my code?

It can propose fixes for review. Never merge without a developer checking the change.

How long does it take to set up Multi Source Support Agent?

Our estimate is half a day or more for a first working run, including connecting Gmail, Ollama and Slack. Tuning it to your business takes a few more runs.
