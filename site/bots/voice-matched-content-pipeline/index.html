What it does

  • Reads public X history.
  • Builds a voice guide.
  • Plans content in Notion.
  • Supports ghostwriting.

Who it's for

  • Agencies
  • Ghostwriters
  • Consultants

Set it up 5 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • X – Free account; the X API is pay per use
  • Claude – Free plan available; API is pay as you go
  • Notion – Free plan available
  • Whop – Check the vendor's pricing
  1. 1Read the creator's post with Get it to see how they run it.
  2. 2In Grok Bot, create a new bot whose one-line job is to read a client's public X history, write a voice guide and plan posts in Notion.
  3. 3Write its instructions in your own words, covering what to note about voice, how many posts to read, the Notion page layout and that it only drafts.
  4. 4Connect Notion, and allow it to read public X posts.
  5. 5Run it on your own X account and see if the guide sounds like you.
Worked exampleOnboarding a new founder client

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You give it the founder's X handle.
  2. It reads their recent public posts and replies.
  3. It writes a voice guide: short sentences, plain words, strong views on hiring.
  4. It plans twenty post ideas in Notion.
  5. The founder approves the guide, and you start drafting.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • Drafts that sound like the client from day one.
  • Clients still need to approve.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Get client consent.
  • Share the voice guide first.
  • Have the client sign off on posts.
