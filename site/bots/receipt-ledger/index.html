Receipt Ledger

Keeps a running spending ledger for write-offs: it reads receipts from your email into a Google Sheet every day and sends a Monday summary.

What it does

  • Scans your email for receipts each day.
  • Logs each one into a spending ledger in Google Sheets.
  • Sends a short summary every Monday.

Who it's for

  • Small business owners tracking write-offs
  • Freelancers who get receipts by email
  • Anyone who wants a weekly spend summary without manual entry

Set it up 5 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • Gmail – Where your receipts arrive
  • Google Sheets – Free; holds the ledger
  1. 1Read the bot page to confirm it fits your receipts.
  2. 2Add it from the share link.
  3. 3Connect the mailbox where receipts arrive and a free Google Sheets account.
  4. 4Check the first week of rows against your real receipts.
  5. 5Share the sheet with your bookkeeper if you have one.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • A ledger that fills itself and a Monday summary. Accuracy depends on how clear your receipt emails are.
  • Expect to fix a few categories in the first weeks.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Use one mailbox for business receipts if you can.
  • Spot-check amounts weekly at first.
  • It is a log, not tax advice; ask your accountant what counts as a write-off.

Questions

Is Receipt Ledger free to use?

It is free to add from the creator's share link, but you need a Grok Bot account.

Does Receipt Ledger replace my accountant?

No. It keeps a spending log and a weekly summary. Your accountant still decides what is deductible.

How long does it take to set up Receipt Ledger?

Our estimate is 15 to 25 minutes, including connecting email and a sheet.
