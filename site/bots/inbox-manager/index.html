Inbox Manager

What it does

  • Scans new Gmail.
  • Labels messages.
  • Sets priority.
  • Drafts replies.

Who it's for

  • Founders
  • Managers
  • Busy professionals

Set it up 5 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • Gmail – Free with a Google account
  1. 1Read the creator's post with Get it to see how they run it.
  2. 2In Grok Bot, create a new bot whose one-line job is to scan my new Gmail, label and prioritize it and draft replies for the messages that need me.
  3. 3Write its instructions in your own words, covering your label names, what counts as urgent, who always gets priority and how you like replies to sound.
  4. 4Connect Gmail and do not allow it to send.
  5. 5Run it on today's inbox and check the labels before turning on drafts.
Worked exampleA morning inbox

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. Forty emails arrive overnight.
  2. The bot labels newsletters, receipts and updates.
  3. It marks three messages as needing you today.
  4. It drafts replies for two of them.
  5. You send both and archive the rest.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • An inbox sorted by priority.

Good to know

  • Start with labels.
  • Review drafts.
  • Consider a separate inbox.
