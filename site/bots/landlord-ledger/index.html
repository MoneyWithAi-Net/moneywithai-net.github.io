What it does

  • Ties every rent payment to the tenant who made it.
  • Flags late or short rent.
  • Groups costs under the Schedule E headings.
  • Drafts reminders you approve; never moves money.

Who it's for

  • Owners of a few rental units
  • Short-term rental hosts tracking expenses
  • Small property managers

Set it up 5 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  1. 1Read the original and check it fits.
  2. 2Add it from the share link.
  3. 3Give it your accounts, properties and how you categorise income and costs.
  4. 4Test on a small sample.
  5. 5Schedule only after it looks right; keep approval for customer-facing sends.
Worked exampleFirst of the month for a three-unit building

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You give Landlord Ledger this month's bank statement.
  2. It matches two deposits to units A and B.
  3. It flags unit C as short by the amount of last month's late fee.
  4. It sorts a plumber's bill and an insurance payment into expense categories.
  5. You approve a polite reminder to unit C.

Good to know

  • List each tenant's rent and due date first.
  • Approve reminders before they go out.
  • Keep receipts for every expense.

Ask a question first

Questions

Does Landlord Ledger collect rent?

No. It matches deposits, flags late or short rent and drafts reminders. It never moves money.

What is Schedule E?

The US tax form where rental income and expenses are reported. Check category choices with an accountant.

Does it message tenants?

It drafts reminders that you approve and send.

Is Landlord Ledger free to use?

It is free to add from the share link, but you need a Grok Bot account.

How long does it take to set up Landlord Ledger?

Our estimate is 15 to 30 minutes, mostly listing your tenants and rents.
