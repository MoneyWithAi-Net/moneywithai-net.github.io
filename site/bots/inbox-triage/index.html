Inbox Triage

Sorts new email and social DMs, closes what needs no reply and turns the rest into Trello tasks.

Get it↗
Price
Free to build in Grok Bot. You need a Grok Bot account. Trello and each social account need their own logins.

Based on this post on X by @rchase · Are you the creator? · Report

What it does

  • Scans email and DMs.
  • Closes what needs nothing.
  • Creates Trello cards.
  • Links to the original.

Who it's for

  • Founders
  • Solo operators
  • Small business owners

Set it up 5 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • Facebook – Free plan available
  • X – Free account; the X API is pay per use
  • LinkedIn – Check the vendor's pricing
  • Trello – Free plan available
  1. 1Read the creator's post with Get it to see how they run it.
  2. 2In Grok Bot, create a new bot whose one-line job is to review my email and social messages, close what needs no reply from me and make a Trello card for anything that does.
  3. 3Write its instructions in your own words, covering what counts as needing your reply, which senders always matter, how to word Trello cards and a rule never to delete anything.
  4. 4Sign in to your email, Trello and each social account in the bot's browser, starting with email only.
  5. 5Run it on one morning's messages and check every closed item before letting it run daily.
Worked exampleA busy morning of messages

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. The bot checks your email and three social inboxes.
  2. It closes a sales pitch and two automated notices.
  3. It finds a partner question on LinkedIn that needs you.
  4. It adds a Trello card with a link to that message.
  5. You work through the Trello list after lunch.
