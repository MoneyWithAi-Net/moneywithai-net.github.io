Gig Books Closer

Closes the books after live events by reconciling Soundcheck gigs with QuickBooks.

Set it up with the steps on this page.

Price
Free to build in Grok Bot. You need a Grok Bot account; check Soundcheck and QuickBooks for their pricing.

By steventt07 · Are you the creator? · Report

What it does

  • Pulls gigs from Soundcheck.
  • Matches them with payments in QuickBooks.
  • Flags unpaid balances and mismatches.
  • Posts entries only with your approval.

Who it's for

  • Musicians and bands
  • Managers handling several acts
  • Small event crews

Set it up 4 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • SoundcheckSoundcheck – Check the vendor's pricing
  • QuickBooks – Paid plan
  1. 1In Grok Bot, create a new bot whose one-line job is to reconcile gigs in Soundcheck with payments and expenses in QuickBooks after each event.
  2. 2Write its instructions in your own words, covering how to match gigs to payments, what counts as a mismatch, how to report unpaid balances and that it posts nothing without approval.
  3. 3Connect Soundcheck and QuickBooks with your own accounts, as their documentation describes.
  4. 4Run it on a past gig you already reconciled by hand.
Worked exampleClosing a weekend of shows

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You played three gigs over the weekend.
  2. The bot pulls the three gigs from Soundcheck.
  3. It matches two deposits and one balance in QuickBooks.
  4. It flags one gig whose balance has not arrived.
  5. You send the venue a reminder and approve the matched entries.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • Each gig reconciled, with unpaid balances found quickly.
  • Cash payments need recording by hand.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Close the books weekly.
  • Record cash on the night.
  • Approve entries before posting.
