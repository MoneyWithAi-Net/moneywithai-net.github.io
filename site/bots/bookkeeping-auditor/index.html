Bookkeeping Auditor

Audits your books against bank, card and check records and lists the errors to fix.

What it does

  • Compares QuickBooks entries with bank, card and check records.
  • Flags missing, duplicate and wrong-amount entries.
  • Spots items posted to the wrong account.
  • Lists errors to fix and leaves the changes to you.

Who it's for

  • Freelancers who keep their own QuickBooks
  • Small businesses before a lender or tax review
  • Bookkeepers checking client files at month end

Set it up 5 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • QuickBooks – Paid plan
  1. 1Read the creator's post with Get it to see how they run it.
  2. 2In Grok Bot, create a new bot whose one-line job is to check my books against bank, card and check records and list errors.
  3. 3Write its instructions in your own words, covering which accounts to check, the month to review, what counts as an error (missing, duplicate, wrong amount, wrong account), and that it reports and never edits.
  4. 4Connect QuickBooks with an account you control, and decide how statements reach it: an upload each month or a read-only bank connection.
  5. 5Run it on a month you already closed and compare its list with what you know was wrong.
Worked exampleA month-end check for a small studio

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You export last month's bank and card statements.
  2. The auditor compares them with the month in QuickBooks.
  3. It lists a duplicated software charge and a deposit recorded in the wrong month.
  4. Your bookkeeper confirms both and makes the corrections.
  5. You keep the list with the month's records.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • A clear list of mismatches between your books and your bank, card and check records.
  • Some flagged items will be timing differences, not errors.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Use statements for exactly the same dates as the books you check.
  • Fix errors in QuickBooks yourself and note why.
  • Keep each month's error list with your records.

Questions

Does Bookkeeping Auditor change my books?

No. It lists the errors it finds. You or your bookkeeper make the changes.

Which accounting software does it work with?

This setup is built around QuickBooks, checked against your bank, card and check records.

Is Bookkeeping Auditor free to use?

Setting it up in Grok Bot costs nothing extra beyond your Grok Bot account and your QuickBooks plan.

How long does it take to set up Bookkeeping Auditor?

Our estimate is 30 to 60 minutes, including a first check on a month you have already closed.
