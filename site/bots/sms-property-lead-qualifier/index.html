What it does

  • Texts new website form leads right away via Twilio.
  • Asks about budget, area and timeline with an AI agent.
  • Stores the chat by phone number in Supabase Postgres.
  • Logs answers in Sheets and sends a summary to the agent.

Who it's for

  • Buyer's agents who get web form leads
  • Small brokerages without an inside sales team
  • Investors who answer buyer inquiries

Set it up 6 steps

  • n8n – Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • Google Sheets – Free with a Google account
  • OpenAI – Pay as you go (API billed per token)
  • Postgres – Free plan available
  • Twilio – Paid plan
  1. 1Open the original with Get it and copy or download the SMS lead qualifier workflow.
  2. 2Import it into your n8n editor and add your own credentials for Twilio, OpenAI, Supabase and Google Sheets.
  3. 3Swap the sample IDs, addresses and text in the SMS lead qualifier workflow for your own, and read each AI prompt node.
  4. 4Point the form trigger at your website form, and edit the qualifying questions and the hand-off summary to match how you work.
  5. 5Submit your own form with your own phone number and finish a whole conversation.
  6. 6Switch the SMS lead qualifier workflow on only after a clean test.
Worked exampleA buyer inquiry on a Saturday

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. A buyer submits your website form on Saturday evening.
  2. Within a minute they get a text asking what area and price range they have in mind.
  3. They answer and say they want to move in about three months.
  4. The workflow logs the answers in your sheet and sends you a short summary.
  5. You call them on Monday with listings that match.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • Every form lead gets a reply in minutes and arrives pre-qualified.
  • Some buyers will not reply to texts; keep email and calls as backups.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Collect texting consent on your form.
  • Keep the opening text short and say who you are.
  • Read the conversation log before calling the lead.

Ask a question first

Questions

When does it text a lead?

As soon as a buyer fills in the inquiry form on your property site.

What does the AI ask?

Pre-qualifying questions such as budget, location and timeline. You can edit them.

Where is the conversation stored?

In a Postgres database on Supabase, tied to the buyer's phone number, with a summary in Google Sheets.

Is it legal to text leads automatically?

Rules vary. Only text people who gave consent, identify yourself, and check local rules. This is not legal advice.

How long does it take to set up SMS Property Lead Qualifier?

Our estimate is 2 to 4 hours, including Twilio, Supabase and a full test from your own phone.
