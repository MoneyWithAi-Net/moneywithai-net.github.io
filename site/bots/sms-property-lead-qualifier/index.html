Description

Qualifies real estate leads over SMS with Twilio and records the answers in Google Sheets.

What It Does

  • Qualifies real estate leads over SMS with Twilio.
  • Records the answers in Google Sheets.
  • Works with Google Sheets, OpenAI, Postgres and Twilio.
  • Keeps you in charge: review its output before anything reaches a customer or goes public.

Who It's For

  • Real estate agents
  • Small brokerages
  • Property investors

How to Set It Up

Time to Set Up
About 2 to 4 hours (our estimate)
Difficulty
Advanced
Tools Needed
  • n8n: Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • Google Sheets: Free with a Google account
  • OpenAI: Pay as you go (API billed per token)
  • Postgres: Free plan available
  • Twilio: Paid plan
  1. Read the creator's original page to confirm it fits your business.
  2. Import the template into n8n, either self-hosted or on n8n Cloud.
  3. Connect Google Sheets, OpenAI, Postgres and Twilio when asked, using accounts you control.
  4. Give it your farm area, price bands and the kind of seller or buyer you want.
  5. Do a test run on a small sample and check every output by hand.
  6. Once results look right, set a schedule or trigger and keep an approval step for anything customer-facing.

Tips and Pitfalls

  • Check do-not-call and data rules before you contact owners.
  • Skip-traced data can be wrong; verify before outreach.
  • Templates can break when n8n or a connected app changes; re-test after updates.

Questions

Is the SMS Property Lead Qualifier template free?

The template is free on n8n's gallery. n8n can be self-hosted for free or used on a paid n8n Cloud plan, and connected apps may charge for their own use.

Is it legal to contact property owners this way?

Rules vary by country and state. The bot helps find and sort leads; you are responsible for consent and do-not-call compliance.

How long does it take to set up SMS Property Lead Qualifier?

Our estimate is 2 to 4 hours for a first working run, including connecting Google Sheets, OpenAI and Postgres. Tuning it to your business takes a few more runs.
