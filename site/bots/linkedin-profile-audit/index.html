LinkedIn Profile Audit

Compares your LinkedIn profile with your current work and drafts updates for your approval.

What it does

  • Reads your current work from Notion and Slack.
  • Compares it with your LinkedIn profile.
  • Drafts headline, about and experience updates.
  • Waits for your approval.

Who it's for

  • Consultants whose focus changes often
  • Founders who sell through their profile
  • Employees moving into new roles

Set it up 5 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • LinkedIn – Check the vendor's pricing
  • Notion – Free plan available
  • Slack – Free plan available
  1. 1Read the creator's post with Get it to see how they run it.
  2. 2In Grok Bot, create a new bot whose one-line job is to compare my LinkedIn profile with my current work and draft updates for my approval.
  3. 3Write its instructions in your own words, covering which notes count as current work, which profile sections to review, your tone and that it never posts or edits.
  4. 4Connect Notion and Slack with access only to the pages and channels you choose.
  5. 5Run it once and check every suggested line is true and public-safe.
Worked exampleAfter a quarter of new work

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You have spent three months leading a new partnerships project.
  2. The bot reads your Notion project notes.
  3. It finds that your profile still describes your old role.
  4. It drafts a new headline and an experience entry.
  5. You trim the wording and update LinkedIn yourself.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • A profile that reflects your current work.
  • You will still want to tune the wording.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Limit its access to work you can share.
  • Run it each quarter.
  • Keep claims factual and modest.
