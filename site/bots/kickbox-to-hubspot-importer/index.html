Kickbox to HubSpot Importer

Verifies contact emails with Kickbox and writes the deliverable results back into existing HubSpot records.

What it does

  • Starts from a HubSpot view or a contacts spreadsheet.
  • Checks each email address with Kickbox.
  • Imports deliverable results back as updates to existing contacts.
  • Never creates new HubSpot records.

Who it's for

  • Sales teams about to email a large HubSpot list
  • Agencies cleaning a client's CRM
  • Founders who imported old contact lists

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • Kickbox – Check the vendor's pricing
  • HubSpot – Free CRM available; paid tiers
  • Google Sheets – Free with a Google account

Worked exampleCleaning a list before a campaign

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You pick a HubSpot view of 200 contacts you plan to email next week.
  2. The importer sends each address to Kickbox for a check.
  3. It reports which addresses came back deliverable.
  4. It updates those existing HubSpot contacts and leaves the rest untouched.
  5. You exclude the unverified contacts from the campaign.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • Fewer bounces and a cleaner HubSpot, with only verified addresses updated.
  • Unverified contacts stay as they were; decide separately what to do with them.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Run a small batch first to see Kickbox costs.
  • Back up the HubSpot view before the first import.
  • Exclude unverified contacts from campaigns.

Ask a question first

Questions

Will it create new HubSpot contacts?

No. It is an update-only import for contacts that already exist.

What is Kickbox?

An email verification service that checks whether an address is likely to receive mail.

Is Kickbox to HubSpot Importer free to use?

The bot is free to add, but Kickbox charges for verifications and you need a Grok Bot account.

Can I start from a spreadsheet?

Yes. Point it at a spreadsheet of contacts, or at a HubSpot view.

How long does it take to set up Kickbox to HubSpot Importer?

Our estimate is 30 to 60 minutes, mostly connecting HubSpot and Kickbox and testing a small batch.
