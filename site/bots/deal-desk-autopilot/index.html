Description

Reviews every open deal, spots stalled steps and drafts the next follow-up.

What It Does

  • Reviews every open deal.
  • Spots stalled steps.
  • Drafts the next follow-up.
  • Works with Gmail and Google Calendar.
  • Keeps you in charge: review its output before anything reaches a customer or goes public.

Who It's For

  • B2B sales teams
  • Founders who run their own sales calls
  • Agencies that pitch often

How to Set It Up

Time to Set Up
About 15 to 30 minutes (our estimate)
Difficulty
Beginner
Tools Needed
  • Grok Bot: Grok Bot account needed
  • Gmail: Free with a Google account
  • Google Calendar: Free with a Google account
  1. Read the creator's original page to confirm it fits your business.
  2. Rebuild it in Grok Bot or your own agent by following the creator's description.
  3. Connect Gmail and Google Calendar when asked, using accounts you control.
  4. Give it your product facts, pricing rules and the CRM or notes you keep.
  5. Do a test run on a small sample and check every output by hand.
  6. Once results look right, set a schedule or trigger and keep an approval step for anything customer-facing.

Tips and Pitfalls

  • Feed it accurate product facts; it repeats what it is given.
  • Get consent before recording any call.
  • Review follow-ups before they go to a prospect.

Questions

Is Deal Desk Autopilot free to use?

It is free to add from the creator's listing, but you need a Grok Bot account. Connected tools such as Gmail and Google Calendar may need their own plans.

Does it join my calls?

Depending on the setup it prepares notes, answers questions or drafts follow-ups. Check the creator's source for exactly which parts it covers.

How long does it take to set up Deal Desk Autopilot?

Our estimate is 15 to 30 minutes for a first working run, including connecting Gmail and Google Calendar. Tuning it to your business takes a few more runs.
