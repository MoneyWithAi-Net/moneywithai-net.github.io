Campaign Autopilot

Reviews your Google Analytics, Meta Ads and LinkedIn results on a set schedule and posts campaign fixes to Slack for your approval.

Set it up with the steps on this page.

Price
Free to build in Grok Bot. You need a Grok Bot account, plus your ad and analytics accounts; ad spend is billed by each platform.

What it does

  • Pulls campaign results.
  • Spots slipping campaigns.
  • Suggests fixes.
  • Posts to Slack for approval.

Who it's for

  • Small marketing teams
  • Agencies
  • Founders running ads

Set it up 4 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • Google Analytics – Free
  • Meta Ads – Paid plan
  • LinkedIn – Check the vendor's pricing
  • Slack – Free plan available
  1. 1In Grok Bot, create a new bot whose one-line job is to review my Google Analytics, Meta Ads and LinkedIn results each week and post suggested fixes to Slack.
  2. 2Write its instructions in your own words, covering your goals, the numbers that matter, the kinds of fixes it may suggest and that it never changes campaigns itself.
  3. 3Connect Google Analytics, Meta Ads and LinkedIn with read access, and Slack with one channel.
  4. 4Run one review and check its suggestions against the dashboards.
Worked exampleA Thursday campaign review

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. The bot pulls the week's numbers from all three sources.
  2. It sees a Meta ad whose cost per lead doubled.
  3. It suggests pausing it and testing a new image.
  4. It posts the suggestion in your Slack channel.
  5. You approve the pause and plan the new image.
