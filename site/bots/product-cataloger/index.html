What it does

  • Organises products.
  • Adds SKUs and labels.
  • Groups categories.
  • Exports a PDF.

Who it's for

  • Small product brands
  • Makers
  • Wholesalers

Set it up 6 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  1. 1Read the original and check it fits.
  2. 2Add it from the share link.
  3. 3Gather your product photos or an existing price list in one place.
  4. 4Tell it whether you already use SKUs or want it to suggest a simple system.
  5. 5Do a test run on one category and check every price and SKU by hand.
  6. 6Once results look right, run the full catalog and keep a final review before you send it to anyone.
Worked exampleA wholesale catalog for candles

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You upload photos and a list of 24 candles with SKUs.
  2. The bot groups them into seasonal, classic and gift sets.
  3. It adds a label and thumbnail for each candle.
  4. It produces a PDF catalog ready to send to shops.
  5. You check prices on each page before sending.
