Keyword Conversation Finder

Finds LinkedIn posts and comments that mention the problems you solve, giving you warm conversations to join.

Set it up with the steps on this page.

Price
You can build it free in Grok Bot. Keyword monitoring runs through Signado, which you pay for separately.
Time
About 30 to 60 minutes (our estimate)
Level
Beginner
Apps
SignadoSignado

What it does

  • Watches LinkedIn for your keywords via Signado.
  • Finds posts and comments about your problem.
  • Returns conversations worth joining.
  • Leaves commenting to you.

Who it's for

  • Consultants who sell through LinkedIn
  • Agencies looking for warm openings
  • Founders who answer questions in their niche

Set it up 4 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • SignadoSignado – Check the vendor's pricing
  1. 1In Grok Bot, create a new bot whose one-line job is to find LinkedIn posts and comments that mention the problems I solve so I can join helpful conversations.
  2. 2Write its instructions in your own words, covering your keywords, the problems you solve, how recent posts must be, how many to return and that it never comments or messages.
  3. 3Connect Signado with your own account, as its documentation describes.
  4. 4Run it with three keywords and check whether the results are real conversations.
Worked exampleFinding conversations about churn

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You set keywords such as customer churn and losing clients.
  2. The bot finds a founder's post asking how to keep clients past month three.
  3. It finds a comment thread on the same problem in another post.
  4. You leave a practical tip on each.
  5. The founder replies and asks to chat.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • A steady list of live conversations about your problem.
  • Not every conversation leads to a sale.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Choose keywords buyers actually use.
  • Give a real answer, not a pitch.
  • Reply quickly while posts are fresh.
