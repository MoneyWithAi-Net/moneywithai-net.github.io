What it does

  • Listens on Telegram.
  • Lists open orders.
  • Shows order details.
  • Says when none are open.

Who it's for

  • Store owners
  • Small e-commerce teams
  • Fulfilment staff

Set it up 6 steps

  • n8n – Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • Shopify – Paid plan
  • Telegram – Free plan available
  1. 1Open the original with Get it and copy or download the Telegram Shopify Order Check workflow.
  2. 2Import it into your n8n editor and add your own credentials for Telegram and Shopify.
  3. 3Swap the sample IDs, addresses and text in the Telegram Shopify Order Check workflow for your own, and read each AI prompt node.
  4. 4Create your Telegram bot with BotFather and a Shopify app that can only read orders.
  5. 5Send /orders from your own Telegram account and check that each order button shows the right items.
  6. 6Switch the Telegram Shopify Order Check workflow on only after a clean test.
Worked exampleChecking orders on the go

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. While out, you type /orders in your private Telegram chat.
  2. It replies with three open orders as buttons.
  3. You tap the newest order.
  4. It shows the items and quantities for that order.
  5. You message your packer to prioritise it.
