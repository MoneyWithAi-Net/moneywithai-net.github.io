Description

Lets you check and monitor Shopify orders by sending simple commands to a Telegram bot.

What It Does

  • Lets you check and monitor Shopify orders by sending simple commands to a Telegram bot.
  • Works with Shopify and Telegram.
  • Leaves decisions with you: it prepares, checks and reports, and you act.

Who It's For

  • Shopify and online store owners
  • Small DTC brands
  • Ecommerce operators

How to Set It Up

Time to Set Up
About 1 to 2 hours (our estimate)
Difficulty
Intermediate
Tools Needed
  • n8n: Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • Shopify: Paid plan
  • Telegram: Free plan available
  1. Read the creator's original page to confirm it fits your business.
  2. Import the template into n8n, either self-hosted or on n8n Cloud.
  3. Connect Shopify and Telegram when asked, using accounts you control.
  4. Give it your store platform, order and stock rules and who handles exceptions.
  5. Do a test run on a small sample and check every output by hand.
  6. Once results look right, set a schedule or trigger and keep an approval step for anything customer-facing.

Tips and Pitfalls

  • Test on a few orders before running it on all of them.
  • Keep refunds and price changes behind approval.
  • Templates can break when n8n or a connected app changes; re-test after updates.

Questions

Is the Telegram Shopify Order Check template free?

The template is free on n8n's gallery. n8n can be self-hosted for free or used on a paid n8n Cloud plan, and connected apps may charge for their own use.

Will it change my store settings?

Only what you connect and allow. Start with read-only reports, then grant more access step by step.

How long does it take to set up Telegram Shopify Order Check?

Our estimate is 1 to 2 hours for a first working run, including connecting Shopify and Telegram. Tuning it to your business takes a few more runs.
