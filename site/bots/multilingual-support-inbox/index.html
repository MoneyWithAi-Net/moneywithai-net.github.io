What it does

  • Receives WhatsApp and email.
  • Translates and summarizes.
  • Drafts replies.
  • Logs chats in Sheets.

Who it's for

  • Ecommerce brands
  • Travel and hospitality
  • International support teams

Set it up 6 steps

  • n8n – Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • Google Sheets – Free with a Google account
  • Google Translate – Check the vendor's pricing
  • WhatsApp – Pay per message on the Business Platform; service replies are free
  1. 1Open the original with Get it and copy or download the support inbox workflow.
  2. 2Import it into your n8n editor and add your own credentials for WhatsApp Business, your email, Google Translate and Google Sheets.
  3. 3Swap the sample IDs, addresses and text in the support inbox workflow for your own, and read each AI prompt node.
  4. 4Create the log sheet and set your WhatsApp Business number.
  5. 5Send a test message in another language and check the draft.
  6. 6Switch the support inbox workflow on only after a clean test.
Worked exampleA Spanish message on WhatsApp

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. A customer writes in Spanish about a late order.
  2. The workflow translates it and writes a summary.
  3. It drafts a reply in Spanish.
  4. It logs the chat in the shared sheet.
  5. You check the draft and send it.

Good to know

  • Check sensitive replies.
  • Keep the log tidy.
  • Test each language.
