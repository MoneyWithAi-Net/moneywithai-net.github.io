X Posting Advisor

Advises when to post, quote or wait on X using public ranking signals and your own account metrics.

What it does

  • Reads your metrics.
  • Advises timing.
  • Suggests formats.
  • Guides engagement.

Who it's for

  • Founders building in public
  • Creators
  • Small brands

Set it up 5 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • X – Free account; the X API is pay per use
  • X API – Pay per use
  1. 1Read the creator's post with Get it to see how they run it.
  2. 2In Grok Bot, create a new bot whose one-line job is to advise me whether to post, quote or wait on X, based on how X ranks posts and my own account numbers.
  3. 3Write its instructions in your own words, covering your goals on X, your audience, what kinds of posts you make and how to explain each suggestion briefly.
  4. 4Connect X so it can read your account metrics.
  5. 5Ask for advice on one draft and compare it with how your past posts did.
Worked exampleTiming a launch post

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You paste your launch post and ask when to share it.
  2. The advisor checks when your followers are usually active.
  3. It suggests Tuesday morning instead of Friday night.
  4. It recommends putting the link in a reply.
  5. You post on Tuesday and note the result in your log.
