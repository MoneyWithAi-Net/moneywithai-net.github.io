Worked exampleSetting up a bakery account

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. On the first run you set the topic as sourdough baking.
  2. You set the audience as home bakers and the voice as warm and practical.
  3. The desk drafts five posts on starters, flour and timing.
  4. You approve three and post them yourself.
  5. Next week's drafts follow the same topic and voice.
