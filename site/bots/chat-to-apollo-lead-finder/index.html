What it does

  • Takes requests on Telegram.
  • Turns them into Apollo searches.
  • Enriches leads.
  • Saves them to a database.

Who it's for

  • Agencies using Apollo
  • Sales teams
  • Founders building lead lists

Set it up 6 steps

  • n8n – Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • OpenAI – Pay as you go (API billed per token)
  • Postgres – Free plan available
  • Supabase – Free plan available
  • Telegram – Free plan available
  1. 1Open the original with Get it and copy or download the Apollo lead finder workflow.
  2. 2Import it into your n8n editor and add your own credentials for Telegram, OpenAI, Apollo and your database.
  3. 3Swap the sample IDs, addresses and text in the Apollo lead finder workflow for your own, and read each AI prompt node.
  4. 4Create the database table the workflow writes to, and set your Telegram bot.
  5. 5Send one narrow test request and check the saved rows.
  6. 6Switch the Apollo lead finder workflow on only after a clean test.
Worked exampleFinding leads from a chat message

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You message the Telegram bot: HR managers at hotels in Lisbon.
  2. The workflow turns the message into Apollo search terms.
  3. It pulls thirty matching leads.
  4. It enriches them and saves them to your database.
  5. You export the list for review.
