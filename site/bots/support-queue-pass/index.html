What it does

  • Passes over the Zendesk queue hourly.
  • Drafts replies on the tickets it is confident about.
  • Works with Zendesk and Slack.
  • Keeps you in charge: review its output before anything reaches a customer or goes public.

Who it's for

  • SaaS support teams
  • Ecommerce customer service
  • Agencies handling client requests

Questions

Is Support Queue Pass free to use?

It is free to add from the creator's listing, but you need a Grok Bot account. Connected tools such as Zendesk and Slack may need their own plans.

Does it answer tickets or just sort them?

Depending on the setup it sorts, tags, drafts replies or both. Check the source for the exact scope.
