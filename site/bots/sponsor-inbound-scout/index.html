Questions

Is Sponsor Inbound Scout free to use?

It is free to add from the creator's listing, but you need a Grok Bot account. Connected tools such as Gmail may need their own plans.

How does it find partners or sponsors?

It looks at public signals such as who advertises in your niche or who runs affiliate programs, then drafts a short pitch for you to review.

How long does it take to set up Sponsor Inbound Scout?

Our estimate is 15 to 30 minutes for a first working run, including connecting Gmail. Tuning it to your business takes a few more runs.
