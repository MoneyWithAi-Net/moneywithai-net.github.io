Sponsor Inbound Scout

Watches Gmail for inbound sponsor emails and flags the legitimate ones with a quick brief.

What it does

  • Watches Gmail for advertising inquiries.
  • Checks that the sender looks genuine.
  • Writes a short brief with a suggested offer.
  • Leaves the reply to you.

Who it's for

  • Newsletter owners who sell ad slots
  • Podcasters with sponsor inquiries
  • Creators who miss emails

Set it up 5 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • Gmail – Free with a Google account
  1. 1Read the creator's post with Get it to see how they run it.
  2. 2In Grok Bot, create a new bot whose one-line job is to watch Gmail for inbound sponsor emails and flag genuine ones with a short brief.
  3. 3Write its instructions in your own words, covering what a sponsor email looks like, how to check the sender, what the brief includes (who, what they asked, a fitting offer), and that it never replies.
  4. 4Connect Gmail with your own account.
  5. 5Forward yourself a past sponsor email and check that it is flagged and briefed correctly.
Worked exampleA local business asks about ads

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. A local cafe owner emails asking about newsletter ad rates.
  2. The scout flags it and checks the cafe is real.
  3. It writes a brief: who they are, what they asked, which ad slot fits.
  4. You reply the same day with your rate sheet.
  5. You note the lead in your sponsor list.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • Sponsor inquiries spotted and briefed while the sponsor is still interested.
  • Some inquiries will be scams; check before sharing rates.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Keep a current rate sheet ready.
  • Reply to good leads the same day.
  • Check unfamiliar senders.

Ask a question first

Questions

What does it look for?

Inbound emails from sponsors or businesses interested in advertising with you.

Does it reply to sponsors?

No. It flags and briefs. You reply.

How does it check an email is genuine?

It looks at the sender and company details. Always check before sharing rates or contracts.
