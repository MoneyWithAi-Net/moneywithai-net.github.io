Sends a business owner a weekday brief of what shipped, what is stuck, what needs them, open promises and the day's calendar.

What it does

  • Tracks what shipped.
  • Flags stuck work.
  • Lists open commitments.
  • Shows your calendar.

Who it's for

  • Founders
  • Small business owners
  • Managers

Set it up 5 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • LinkedIn – Connect only if you use it

  2. 2Add it from the share link.
  3. 3Connect LinkedIn if you want it to use them.

  5. 5Review the result and adjust your instructions.
Worked exampleA Tuesday brief

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. At 7:30 am you get the brief.
  2. It says the new checkout shipped yesterday.
  3. It says a supplier contract is stuck waiting on legal.
  4. It lists one decision on hiring that needs you.
  5. It shows three meetings on today's calendar.
