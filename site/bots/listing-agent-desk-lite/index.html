What it does

  • Turns facts and photos into listings.

  • Runs a quick price check.
  • Drafts replies to buyers.

Who it's for

  • Listing agents
  • Agents with overseas buyers

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You share the apartment's facts and twelve photos.
  2. It drafts a listing in English and Spanish.
  3. It runs a quick comparable check and suggests a price range.
  4. A buyer asks about parking, and it drafts a reply.
  5. You check the facts and publish.
