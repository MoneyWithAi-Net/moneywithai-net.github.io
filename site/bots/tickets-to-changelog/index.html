Tickets To Changelog

Turns the week's Zendesk tickets into changelog entries in Notion.

Based on this post on X by @ericzakariasson · Are you the creator? · Report

What it does

  • Reads Zendesk tickets.
  • Groups related issues.
  • Writes changelog entries.
  • Saves them to Notion.

Who it's for

  • Support leads
  • Product managers
  • Small software companies

Set it up 5 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • Zendesk – Paid plan
  • Notion – Free plan available
  1. 1Read the creator's post with Get it to see how they run it.
  2. 2In Grok Bot, create a new bot whose one-line job is to read this week's Zendesk tickets and write changelog entries in Notion for the changes customers asked about.
  3. 3Write its instructions in your own words, covering which tickets to include, how to group them, the entry style and that it never includes customer details.
  4. 4Connect Zendesk with read access and Notion with access to your changelog page.
  5. 5Run it on last week's tickets and check the entries.
Worked exampleA Friday changelog

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. On Friday the bot reads the week's solved tickets.
  2. It groups twelve tickets about a fixed export bug.
  3. It writes one entry: exports now include all columns.
  4. It saves the entry to your Notion changelog.
  5. The product team checks it and publishes.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • A changelog that reflects what customers asked for.
  • Entries need checking.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Remove private details.
  • Check with the product team.
  • Publish weekly.
