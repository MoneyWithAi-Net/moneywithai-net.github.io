Image Gen Bot

Makes brand stills and short clips: gathers references, writes the image prompt, generates, removes backgrounds and iterates until you lock a result.

What it does

  • Gathers your brand details and reference images.
  • Writes the image or video prompt.
  • Generates the still or clip.
  • Removes the background on FAL when you need a cut-out.
  • Iterates until you lock the result.

Who it's for

  • Small brands that need stills and short clips
  • Marketers who make social visuals
  • Shops that want product visuals without a studio shoot

Set it up 5 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • FALFAL – Needed for background removal; check FAL pricing
  1. 1Read the bot page to check it fits your work.
  2. 2Add it from the share link.
  3. 3Connect your FAL account for background removal.
  4. 4Give it your brand details and two or three references.
  5. 5Review each output and lock only the ones you would publish.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • Stills or short clips built from your brief and references.
  • A clean cut-out where you need one.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Use only images and brand assets you own or have the rights to.
  • Label AI-made visuals where your platform or client requires it.
  • Lock one result at a time so the review stays simple.
