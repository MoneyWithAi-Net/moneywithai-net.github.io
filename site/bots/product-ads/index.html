What it does

  • Builds campaign drafts from your product photos and details.
  • Keeps spend inside the daily budget you set.
  • Pauses ads that underperform under the rules you agreed.
  • Scales ads that convert under the same rules.

Who it's for

  • Online sellers who run their own product ads
  • Small shops with a fixed ad budget

Worked exampleA daily ad budget

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You set a daily budget and the pause and scale rules.
  2. Product Ads drafts an ad from one product's photo and details.
  3. You approve the draft and it runs inside the budget.
  4. Losing ads are paused and winning ads get more budget under your rules.

Good to know

  • Set the daily budget before anything runs.
  • Write the pause and scale rules before launch, not after.
  • Ad platforms charge by their own terms, so check the bill.
