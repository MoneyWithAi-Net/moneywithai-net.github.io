What it does

  • Keeps books, customers and sign-ups in one database.
  • Records double-entry transactions from plain words.
  • Keeps full history of every change.
  • Starts from a ready-made SQLite file.

Who it's for

  • Freelancers who want books, customers and sign-ups in one place
  • Small businesses without accounting software
  • Bookkeepers who like a database underneath

Ask a question first

Questions

What is the database?

An open-source business schema that holds books, customers and sign-ups and keeps full history.

Do I need SQL skills?

No. You describe what happened and it records it, though knowing the basics helps.

Does it follow double-entry bookkeeping?

Yes. Each transaction is recorded so the books balance.

Is Business free to use?

It is free to add from the share link, but you need a Grok Bot account.
