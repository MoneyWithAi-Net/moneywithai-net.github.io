What it does

  • Reads item photos.
  • Writes eBay listings.
  • Writes Marketplace posts.
  • Posts after approval.

Who it's for

  • People clearing out a home
  • Resellers
  • Small secondhand shops

Set it up 5 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  1. 1Read the original and check it fits.
  2. 2Add it from the share link.
  3. 3Give it photos of your items, condition notes and your pricing rules.
  4. 4Test on a small sample.
  5. 5Schedule only after it looks right; keep approval for customer-facing sends.
Worked exampleSelling a used bike

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You send three photos of a used road bike.
  2. Sam writes an eBay listing with shipping details.
  3. It writes a shorter Marketplace post for local pickup.
  4. You correct the frame size.
  5. You approve, and Sam posts both.
