What it does

  • Applies your written refund and dispute policy.
  • Drafts responses to disputes using delivery evidence.
  • Prepares refunds as drafts for your approval.
  • Writes a weekly summary of refund and dispute rates.

Who it's for

  • Online and retail sellers who handle refunds
  • Shops that answer payment disputes

Set it up 5 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  1. 1Read the bot page to check it fits your work.
  2. 2Add it from the share link.
  3. 3Write your refund and dispute policy in plain words.
  4. 4Run it on a few past cases and compare with what you decided.
  5. 5Approve each draft before anything reaches a buyer.
Worked exampleA disputed delivery

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. A buyer disputes a charge for an order.
  2. Customer Care checks the policy and the delivery record.
  3. It drafts a response with the evidence.
  4. You approve or edit the draft, and then send it.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • Draft refund and dispute replies, one per case, for your approval.
  • A weekly summary of refund and dispute rates.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Keep the policy short and specific so drafts follow it.
  • Keep delivery records, since disputes depend on them.
  • Nothing reaches a buyer until you approve it.
