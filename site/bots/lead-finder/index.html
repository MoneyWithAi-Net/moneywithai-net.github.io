What it does

  • Searches public posts for people asking for what you sell.
  • Logs each match in a Google Sheet.
  • Drafts a short, helpful first message for the strongest matches.
  • Leaves sending to you.

Who it's for

  • Salespeople prospecting for new clients
  • Owners of small side businesses
  • Agencies looking for people who need their service

Set it up 5 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • Google Sheets – Where the leads are logged
  1. 1Read the bot page and confirm it fits your offer.
  2. 2Add it from the share link.
  3. 3Describe what you sell and who you want to reach.
  4. 4Run it on one platform first and check the matches by hand.
  5. 5Send only the messages you have reviewed.
Worked exampleFinding people who ask for a service

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You describe the service you sell and the places to look.
  2. Lead Finder finds public posts asking for that service.
  3. It logs each match in a Google Sheet.
  4. It drafts a first message for the strongest matches.
  5. You read each one, edit it and send it yourself.

Good to know
