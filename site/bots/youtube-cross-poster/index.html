YouTube Cross Poster

Reposts each new YouTube video to Facebook and Instagram with captions written for each platform.

Original template: Auto-Publish YouTube Videos to Facebook & Instagram with AI-Generated Captions by Yaron Been

What it does

  • Watches your channel.
  • Writes captions per platform.
  • Posts to Facebook.
  • Posts to Instagram.

Who it's for

  • YouTube creators
  • Small brands
  • Social media managers

Set it up 6 steps

  • n8n – Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • OpenAI – Pay as you go (API billed per token)
  1. 1Open the original with Get it and copy or download the cross poster workflow.
  2. 2Import it into your n8n editor and add your own credentials for YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and OpenAI.
  3. 3Swap the sample IDs, addresses and text in the cross poster workflow for your own, and read each AI prompt node.
  4. 4Connect your Facebook page and Instagram business account through Meta.
  5. 5Publish an unlisted test video and check what it posts.
  6. 6Switch the cross poster workflow on only after a clean test.
Worked exampleA new cooking video

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You publish a pasta recipe on YouTube.
  2. The workflow spots the new upload.
  3. It writes a Facebook caption and a shorter Instagram caption.
  4. It posts to both.
  5. You check the posts and reply to comments.
