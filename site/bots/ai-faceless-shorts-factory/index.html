Set it up 4 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • AITuberAITuber – Check the vendor's pricing
  • YouTube – Check the vendor's pricing
  • TikTok – Free plan available
  1. 1In Grok Bot, create a new bot whose one-line job is to produce shorts for my faceless YouTube or TikTok channel through AITuber.
  2. 2Write its instructions in your own words, covering your niche, script length, voice style, posting schedule, that scripts are original and platform labelling rules.
  3. 3Connect AITuber and keep posting manual at first.
  4. 4Make one short and watch it before posting.
Worked exampleA history shorts channel

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You choose a niche: strange facts from history.
  2. The bot writes three original scripts.
  3. AITuber makes the shorts with voice and visuals.
  4. You review each one.
  5. You post one a day to YouTube and TikTok.
