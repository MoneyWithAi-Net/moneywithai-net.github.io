What it does

  • Answers calls with Vapi.
  • Checks your calendar.
  • Books appointments.
  • Logs callers in Airtable.

Who it's for

  • Small businesses
  • Agencies
  • Solo professionals

Set it up 6 steps

  • n8n – Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • Airtable – Free plan available
  • Google Calendar – Free with a Google account
  1. 1Open the original with Get it and copy or download the receptionist workflow.
  2. 2Import it into your n8n editor and add your own credentials for Vapi, Twilio, Google Calendar and Airtable.
  3. 3Swap the sample IDs, addresses and text in the receptionist workflow for your own, and read each AI prompt node.
  4. 4Set your opening hours and services in the Vapi assistant.
  5. 5Call the number yourself and book a test slot.
  6. 6Switch the receptionist workflow on only after a clean test.
Worked exampleA call to book a massage

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. A caller asks for a massage on Saturday.
  2. The receptionist checks your Google Calendar.
  3. It offers 10 am or 2 pm, and the caller picks 2 pm.
  4. It books the slot and saves the caller in Airtable.
  5. You see the new booking in your calendar.
