What it does

  • Pulls in tickets.
  • Sorts spam.
  • Triages requests and bugs.
  • Drafts resolutions.

Who it's for

  • Solo founders
  • Indie app makers
  • Small support teams

Set it up 5 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • Claude – Free plan available; API is pay as you go
  1. 1Read the creator's post with Get it to see how they run it.
  2. 2In Grok Bot, create a new bot whose one-line job is to pull in my support tickets, sort spam, triage bugs and requests and draft replies from my support notes.
  3. 3Write its instructions in your own words, covering what counts as spam, how to label bugs and requests, where the support notes file lives and that you send every reply.
  4. 4Connect your support inbox and give read-only access to the tools it needs.
  5. 5Run it on yesterday's tickets and compare its drafts with what you sent.
Worked exampleA morning of tickets

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You open the bot on your phone with twelve new tickets.
  2. It files five cold pitches and guest post offers as spam.
  3. It groups two feature requests for the same export option.
  4. It drafts a fix note for a login bug using past ticket notes.
  5. You add your own line to each reply and send.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • Support you can do from anywhere.
  • A person still sends replies.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Start read-only.
  • Keep support notes current.
  • Review spam sorting.
