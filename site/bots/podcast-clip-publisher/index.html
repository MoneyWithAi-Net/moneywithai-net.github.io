Podcast Clip Publisher

Transcribes podcast episodes and turns the highlights into X posts.

What it does

  • Transcribes episodes.
  • Finds highlights.
  • Drafts X posts.
  • Adds timestamps.

Who it's for

  • Podcasters
  • Small media teams
  • Companies with a show

Set it up 4 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • X – Free account; the X API is pay per use
  1. 1In Grok Bot, create a new bot whose one-line job is to transcribe each new podcast episode, summarise the highlights and draft X posts from them.
  2. 2Write its instructions in your own words, covering where episodes come from, how long the summary should be, how many posts to draft and whether it must wait for your approval before posting.
  3. 3Connect X if you want it to post, and add a transcription tool if your episodes are long.
  4. 4Run it on one past episode and compare its quotes with the audio.
Worked examplePosts from a 60-minute interview

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You share the link to this week's episode.
  2. The bot transcribes it and writes a one-page summary.
  3. It picks four highlights with timestamps.
  4. It drafts three X posts, each with one quote and a link.
  5. You check the quotes against the audio and schedule two.
