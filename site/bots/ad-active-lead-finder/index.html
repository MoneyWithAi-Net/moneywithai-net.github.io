Finds B2B companies currently running ads in your niche via public ad libraries and Maps, then pushes them to your CRM.

What It Does

  • Finds B2B companies currently running ads in your niche via public ad libraries and Maps.
  • Pushes them to your CRM.
  • Works with Meta Ad Library, Google Ads Transparency, Google Maps, GoHighLevel and HubSpot.
  • Keeps you in charge: review its output before anything reaches a customer or goes public.

Who It's For

  • B2B agencies
  • Consultants and freelancers
  • Founders doing their own outreach

Time to Set Up

Tools Needed

  1. Read the creator's original page to confirm it fits your business.
  2. Add it to Grok Bot from the official share link.
  3. Connect Meta Ad Library, Google Ads Transparency, Google Maps, GoHighLevel and HubSpot when asked, using accounts you control.
  4. Give it your ideal customer, your offer and the signals that make a prospect timely.
  5. Do a test run on a small sample and check every output by hand.
  6. Once results look right, set a schedule or trigger and keep an approval step for anything customer-facing.

Questions

Is Ad-Active Lead Finder free to use?

It is free to add from the creator's listing, but you need a Grok Bot account. Connected tools such as Meta Ad Library, Google Ads Transparency and Google Maps may need their own plans.

Will it send cold emails for me?

Most setups find and research prospects and draft messages. Keep sending under your control and within anti-spam rules.

How long does it take to set up Ad-Active Lead Finder?

Our estimate is 2 to 4 hours for a first working run, including connecting Meta Ad Library, Google Ads Transparency and Google Maps. Tuning it to your business takes a few more runs.
