Ask a question first

Questions

Which machines is Z1 CAM Shop for?

The Makera Z1 and Z1 Pro desktop CNC machines.

Does it control the machine?

No. It plans and writes setup sheets; you run the machine.

Are its feeds and speeds safe to use as-is?

Treat them as starting points and check them against your tool maker's guidance.

Is Z1 CAM Shop free to use?

It is free to add from the share link, but you need a Grok Bot account.

How long does it take to set up Z1 CAM Shop?

Our estimate is 30 to 60 minutes.
