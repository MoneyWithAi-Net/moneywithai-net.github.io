How to Set It Up

Time to Set Up
About 15 to 30 minutes (our estimate)
Difficulty
Beginner
Tools Needed
  • Grok Bot: Grok Bot account needed
  • Google Ads: Paid plan
  1. Read the creator's original post to confirm it fits your business.
  2. Add it to Grok Bot from the official share link.
  3. Connect Google Ads when asked, using accounts you control.
  4. Give it your services, patient or client types and any advertising rules you follow.
  5. Do a test run on a small sample and check every output by hand.
  6. Once results look right, set a schedule or trigger and keep an approval step for anything customer-facing.

It is free to add from the creator's listing, but you need a Grok Bot account. Connected tools such as Google Ads may need their own plans.

Is it suitable for regulated professions?

It can draft campaigns, but you must check them against your profession's rules before publishing.
