What it does

  • Takes chat orders.
  • Builds order tickets.
  • Logs in Sheets.
  • Updates stock.

Who it's for

  • Restaurants
  • Takeaways
  • Cafes

Set it up 6 steps

  • n8n – Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • Google Sheets – Free with a Google account
  • OpenAI – Pay as you go (API billed per token)
  1. 1Open the original with Get it and copy or download the ordering workflow.
  2. 2Import it into your n8n editor and add your own credentials for Google Sheets and OpenAI.
  3. 3Swap the sample IDs, addresses and text in the ordering workflow for your own, and read each AI prompt node.
  4. 4Add your menu and prices to the sheet the workflow reads.
  5. 5Place three test orders, including one with a change, and check the tickets.
  6. 6Switch the ordering workflow on only after a clean test.
Worked exampleA takeaway order

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. A customer types: two margheritas, one without basil, and a cola.
  2. The chatbot confirms the items and the change.
  3. It creates an order ticket with each item.
  4. It logs the order and updates stock in the sheet.
  5. The kitchen sees the ticket in the system.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • Clean orders from chat.
  • Unusual orders need a check.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Use clear menu names.
  • Test tricky orders.
  • Confirm allergies.
