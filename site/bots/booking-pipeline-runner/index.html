Questions

Is Booking Pipeline Runner free to use?

It is free to add from the creator's listing, but you need a Grok Bot account. Connected tools such as Soundcheck and HubSpot may need their own plans.

Does it message clients automatically?

It can send or draft confirmations and reminders based on your rules. Keep consent and opt-out in place.

How long does it take to set up Booking Pipeline Runner?

Our estimate is 15 to 30 minutes for a first working run, including connecting Soundcheck and HubSpot. Tuning it to your business takes a few more runs.
