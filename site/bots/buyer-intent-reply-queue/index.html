Buyer Intent Reply Queue

Queues social posts that show buyer intent from HotMention, with draft replies sent to Slack.

Set it up with the steps on this page.

By alexandr-belogubov · Are you the creator? · Report

What it does

  • Watches for buying-intent posts via HotMention.
  • Drafts a reply for each.
  • Sends drafts to a Slack queue.
  • Leaves posting to your team.

Who it's for

  • B2B agencies watching for buyers
  • Consultants who reply to requests
  • Founders who want timely replies

Set it up 4 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • HotMention – Check the vendor's pricing
  • Slack – Free plan available
  1. 1In Grok Bot, create a new bot whose one-line job is to find social posts showing buyer intent through HotMention and queue draft replies in Slack.
  2. 2Write its instructions in your own words, covering your product and keywords, what counts as buying intent, the reply tone and that it never posts replies.
  3. 3Connect HotMention and Slack with your own accounts, and pick the Slack channel for the queue.
  4. 4Run it for a day and check the queue for real buyer posts.
Worked exampleA buyer asks for tool recommendations

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. Someone posts asking for a good invoicing tool for agencies.
  2. HotMention flags it as buying intent.
  3. The bot drafts a helpful reply and sends it to your Slack queue.
  4. You edit it to mention one useful tip.
  5. You post the reply from your own account.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • Fast, helpful replies to buyers who are asking.
  • Some flagged posts will not be real buyers.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Reply within the day.
  • Edit every draft.
  • Skip posts where a reply would feel pushy.
