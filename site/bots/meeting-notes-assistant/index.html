Meeting Notes Assistant

Finds a meeting's transcript and writes notes, decisions and action items to Google Docs.

Based on this post on X by @mvanhorn · Are you the creator? · Report

What it does

  • Finds meeting transcripts.
  • Writes notes.
  • Lists decisions and actions.
  • Saves to Google Docs.

Who it's for

  • Consultants
  • Managers
  • People with many meetings

Set it up 5 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • Google Calendar – Free with a Google account
  • Google Docs – Free plan available
  1. 1Read the creator's post with Get it to see how they run it.
  2. 2In Grok Bot, create a new bot whose one-line job is to find each meeting's transcript from my Google Calendar and write notes, decisions and action items to a Google Doc.
  3. 3Write its instructions in your own words, covering which meetings to cover, the note layout, how to name the doc and that it never emails the notes.
  4. 4Connect Google Calendar and Google Docs, and the place your transcripts are saved.
  5. 5Run it on one past meeting and compare with your memory.
Worked exampleNotes from a project check-in

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. Your check-in ends at 11 am.
  2. The bot finds the event and its transcript.
  3. It writes a summary, two decisions and five action items.
  4. It saves them to a Google Doc linked from the event.
  5. You fix one owner's name and share the doc.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • A clean record of every meeting.
  • Action items need a check.

Good to know

  • Tell people about recording.
  • Check owners and dates.
  • Share notes quickly.
