What it does

  • Receives WhatsApp messages.
  • Searches your documents.
  • Writes replies.
  • Sends answers back.

Who it's for

  • Small shops
  • Service businesses
  • Support teams

Set it up 6 steps

  • n8n – Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • Google Drive – Free with a Google account (15 GB); more storage is paid
  • OpenAI – Pay as you go (API billed per token)
  • Qdrant – Free plan available
  • WhatsApp – Pay per message on the Business Platform; service replies are free
  1. 1Open the original with Get it and copy or download the WhatsApp Docs Answer Bot workflow.
  2. 2Import it into your n8n editor and add your own credentials for WhatsApp, Google Drive, OpenAI and Qdrant.
  3. 3Swap the sample IDs, addresses and text in the WhatsApp Docs Answer Bot workflow for your own, and read each AI prompt node.
  4. 4Create the Qdrant collection, then load your Google Drive documents into it and rewrite the system message for your business.
  5. 5Message the number from your own phone with five real customer questions and check each answer against your documents.
  6. 6Switch the WhatsApp Docs Answer Bot workflow on only after a clean test.
Worked exampleAnswering a returns question

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. A customer asks on WhatsApp how long they have to return headphones.
  2. The workflow searches your returns policy document in Qdrant.
  3. OpenAI writes a short reply quoting the 30-day window from your policy.
  4. The reply goes back to the customer on WhatsApp.
  5. You review the conversation log at the end of the day.
