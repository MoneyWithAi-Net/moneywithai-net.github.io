What it does

  • Takes a photo.
  • Removes background.
  • Makes a video.
  • Emails the link.

Who it's for

  • Online store owners
  • Shopify sellers
  • Small ecommerce teams

Set it up 6 steps

  • n8n – Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • Gmail – Free with a Google account
  • Google Drive – Free with a Google account (15 GB); more storage is paid
  • Google Sheets – Free with a Google account
  1. 1Open the original with Get it and copy or download the Photo to 3D Product Video workflow.
  2. 2Import it into your n8n editor and add your own credentials for Google Drive, Google Sheets, Gmail, remove.bg and Fal.ai.
  3. 3Swap the sample IDs, addresses and text in the Photo to 3D Product Video workflow for your own, and read each AI prompt node.
  4. 4Create the Drive folder and the Google Sheet it logs to, and add your remove.bg and Fal.ai API keys.
  5. 5Submit one product photo through the form and check the Drive folder, the sheet row and the email link.
  6. 6Switch the Photo to 3D Product Video workflow on only after a clean test.
Worked exampleA video for a water bottle

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You submit a photo of a steel water bottle through the form.
  2. The workflow makes a Drive folder and logs the product in the sheet.
  3. remove.bg cuts out the background.
  4. Fal.ai builds a short rotating video from the clean image.
  5. You get an email with the link and add the video to your listing.
