Resume Ranking Sheet

Counts one download per person per bot per day. Fewer than 10 shows New.

Description

Scores resumes from Google Drive against a job description and logs ranked candidates in Google Sheets.

What It Does

  • Scores resumes from Google Drive against a job description.
  • Logs ranked candidates in Google Sheets.
  • Works with Google Drive, Gemini and Google Sheets.
  • Leaves decisions with you: it prepares, checks and reports, and you act.

Who It's For

  • Small businesses hiring
  • Recruiting agencies
  • Startups growing their team

How to Set It Up

Time to Set Up
About 2 to 4 hours (our estimate)
Difficulty
Advanced
Tools Needed
  • n8n: Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • Google Drive: Free with a Google account (15 GB); more storage is paid
  • Gemini: Pay as you go or paid plan
  • Google Sheets: Free with a Google account
  1. Read the creator's original page to confirm it fits your business.
  2. Import the template into n8n, either self-hosted or on n8n Cloud.
  3. Connect Google Drive, Gemini and Google Sheets when asked, using accounts you control.
  4. Give it the role, must-have skills and your hiring process.
  5. Do a test run on a small sample and check every output by hand.
  6. Once results look right, set a schedule or trigger and keep an approval step for anything customer-facing.

Tips and Pitfalls

  • Use clear, fair criteria and review every decision.
  • Follow local hiring and data laws.
  • Templates can break when n8n or a connected app changes; re-test after updates.

Questions

Is the Resume Ranking Sheet template free?

The template is free on n8n's gallery. n8n can be self-hosted for free or used on a paid n8n Cloud plan, and connected apps may charge for their own use.

Does it decide who gets hired?

No. It helps screen and organise. People make the hiring decisions.

How long does it take to set up Resume Ranking Sheet?

Our estimate is 2 to 4 hours for a first working run, including connecting Google Drive, Gemini and Google Sheets. Tuning it to your business takes a few more runs.
