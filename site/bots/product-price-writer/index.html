Product Price Writer

Description

Reads product costs from Google Sheets and suggests retail prices for each Shopify product and variant.

What It Does

  • Reads product costs from Google Sheets.
  • Suggests retail prices for each Shopify product and variant.
  • Works with Google Sheets and Shopify.
  • Leaves decisions with you: it prepares, checks and reports, and you act.

Who It's For

  • Shopify and online store owners
  • Small DTC brands
  • Ecommerce operators

How to Set It Up

Time to Set Up
About 30 to 60 minutes (our estimate)
Difficulty
Beginner
Tools Needed
  • Grok Bot: Grok Bot account needed
  • Google Sheets: Free with a Google account
  • Shopify: Paid plan
  1. Read the creator's original post to confirm it fits your business.
  2. Rebuild it in Grok Bot or your own agent by following the creator's description.
  3. Connect Google Sheets and Shopify when asked, using accounts you control.
  4. Give it your store platform, order and stock rules and who handles exceptions.
  5. Do a test run on a small sample and check every output by hand.
  6. Once results look right, set a schedule or trigger and keep an approval step for anything customer-facing.

Tips and Pitfalls

  • Test on a few orders before running it on all of them.
  • Keep refunds and price changes behind approval.
  • Log every automated change.

Questions

Is Product Price Writer free to use?

It is free to add from the creator's listing, but you need a Grok Bot account. Connected tools such as Google Sheets and Shopify may need their own plans.

Will it change my store settings?

Only what you connect and allow. Start with read-only reports, then grant more access step by step.

How long does it take to set up Product Price Writer?

Our estimate is 30 to 60 minutes for a first working run, including connecting Google Sheets and Shopify. Tuning it to your business takes a few more runs.
