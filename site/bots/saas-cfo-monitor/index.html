SaaS CFO Monitor

Weekly check of SaaS spend and revenue across billing, accounting and email, with a monthly finance summary.

What it does

  • Reads revenue in Stripe each week.
  • Reads spending in Xero or QuickBooks.
  • Finds billing emails that explain changes.
  • Writes a monthly finance summary.

Who it's for

  • SaaS founders without a finance team
  • Small agencies with subscription revenue
  • Finance leads at early-stage companies

Set it up 5 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • Stripe – Pay as you go or paid plan
  • Xero – Paid plan
  • QuickBooks – Paid plan
  • Gmail – Free with a Google account
  1. 1Read the creator's post with Get it to see how they run it.
  2. 2In Grok Bot, create a new bot whose one-line job is to check SaaS revenue and spending weekly and send a monthly finance summary.
  3. 3Write its instructions in your own words, covering which metrics to watch, what counts as a surprise, the weekly and monthly report format, and that it reads only and never changes anything.
  4. 4Connect Stripe, your accounting tool and Gmail with read-only access where possible.
  5. 5Run it on last month and compare its numbers with your own reports.
Worked exampleA weekly check that catches a cost

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. On Monday the monitor reads the past week in Stripe and your accounting tool.
  2. It notes revenue is flat but a hosting bill doubled.
  3. It finds the billing email that explains a plan change.
  4. You check with your developer and downgrade the plan.
  5. At month end the summary includes the change.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • A weekly early warning on revenue and costs, plus a monthly summary.
  • It reports; decisions stay with you.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Use read-only access where you can.
  • Tell it what counts as a surprise for you.
  • Compare its monthly summary with your books.
