What it does

  • Reads the mix of models and usage you describe.
  • Flags where a cheaper tier may do the same job.
  • Links each suggestion to the provider's official pricing page.
  • Runs a plain-English summary each Monday.

Who it's for

  • Businesses that pay for AI models through their apps or tools
  • Teams that want a regular check on AI costs

Set it up 4 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  1. 1Read the bot page and check it fits your setup.
  2. 2Add it from the official share link.
  3. 3List your models and rough usage.
  4. 4Check each suggestion on the provider's pricing page before you switch.
