What it does

  • Collects business details from BBB listings with BrowserAct.
  • Cleans the data with Google Gemini.
  • Skips businesses already in your sheet.
  • Can send outreach through Gmail if you enable it.

Who it's for

  • Lead-gen consultants building niche lists
  • Local SEO freelancers
  • Agencies targeting established local firms

Set it up 6 steps

  • n8n – Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • Gemini – Pay as you go or paid plan
  1. 1Open the original with Get it and copy or download the BBB lead workflow.
  2. 2Import it into your n8n editor and add your own credentials for BrowserAct, Google Gemini, Google Sheets and Gmail.
  3. 3Swap the sample IDs, addresses and text in the BBB lead workflow for your own, and read each AI prompt node.
  4. 4Disable the Gmail outreach node for your first runs so nothing is sent while you review the data.
  5. 5Run it on one small category with the Gmail step turned off and check every row.
  6. 6Switch the BBB lead workflow on only after a clean test.
Worked exampleCollecting roofers in one state

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You point the workflow at BBB listings for roofers in your state.
  2. BrowserAct collects each listing and Gemini cleans the fields.
  3. The workflow skips businesses already in your sheet.
  4. You review the new rows and remove two with no website.
  5. You draft your own emails to the rest instead of using the automatic send.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • A de-duplicated sheet of businesses from BBB listings.
  • Contact details may be missing for some listings.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Turn off automatic emails until you trust the data.
  • Respect the source site's terms.
  • Check each business before contacting it.

From Better Business Bureau listings, collected with BrowserAct.
