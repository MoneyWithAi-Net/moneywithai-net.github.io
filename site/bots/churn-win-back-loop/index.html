Churn Win-Back Loop

Runs a win-back campaign for lapsed customers from your records, drafting tailored emails for approval.

What it does

  • Finds customers who left.
  • Drafts win-back emails.
  • Collects reasons.
  • Suggests improvements.

Who it's for

  • Product teams
  • Ecommerce brands
  • Founders of paid communities

Set it up 5 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  1. 1Read the creator's post with Get it to see how they run it.
  2. 2In Grok Bot, create a new bot whose one-line job is to find customers who left in the last six months, draft a personal win-back email to each and sort the reasons they give.
  3. 3Write its instructions in your own words, covering the time window, who to leave out, your tone, the offer if any and that it only drafts emails.
  4. 4Connect your customer records and email, with sending left to you.
  5. 5Run it on five past customers and check the drafts.
Worked exampleWinning back members of a paid community

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You give it the list of members who left in six months.
  2. It drafts a short, personal email to each.
  3. You approve the drafts and send them.
  4. It collects the replies and sorts the reasons.
  5. It suggests three changes, and you pick one to try.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • Some returning customers and clear reasons why others left.
  • Many will not reply.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Respect unsubscribes.
  • Keep emails short and honest.
  • Act on the main reason.
