Phone Receptionist

Answers a business phone line and books meetings on Google Calendar. The share card lists a paid phone plan.

What it does

  • Answers calls on a phone number connected to Grok Bot.
  • Answers common questions from the business details you give it.
  • Books meetings straight into your Google Calendar.
  • Offers a dedicated-number version or an outbound-only Bland version.

Who it's for

  • Salons and clinics that miss calls during appointments
  • Trades businesses whose owner is on a roof or under a sink
  • Shops that want after-hours bookings

Set it up 6 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • Google Calendar – Free with a Google account
  • Bland – Pay as you go or paid plan
  1. 1Read the original and check it fits.
  2. 2Add it from the share link.
  3. 3Connect Google Calendar and Bland.
  4. 4Give it your hours, services, booking rules and when to transfer to a person.
  5. 5Test on a small sample.
  6. 6Schedule only after it looks right; keep approval for customer-facing sends.
Worked exampleA salon that misses calls during appointments

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You add the dedicated-number version and connect the salon's Google Calendar.
  2. You give it your services, opening hours and the rule that it never quotes color prices.
  3. A client calls during a busy afternoon and asks for a cut on Friday.
  4. The receptionist offers two open times and books the one the client picks.
  5. You check the calendar entry and call back anyone it flagged as needing a person.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • Fewer missed calls turning into missed bookings. Callers who need a person still need a callback.
  • Expect to tune its answers after the first week of real calls.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Write a short list of things it must never say, such as prices you quote case by case.
  • Tell callers they are speaking with an AI assistant.
  • Test with friends before giving the number to customers.

Ask a question first

Questions

Can Phone Receptionist take inbound calls?

Yes, on the dedicated-number version. The Bland version can only make outbound calls.

Does it cost anything?

The bot is free to add. The dedicated phone number is a paid plan listed on the creator's share page, and you need a Grok Bot account.

Where do bookings go?

Into your Google Calendar, so set the hours and buffers you want before it goes live.

Should callers know they are talking to an AI?

Yes. Tell callers at the start of the call, and follow local rules on recording and automated calls.

How long does it take to set up Phone Receptionist?

Our estimate is 30 to 60 minutes, including a few test calls from your own phone.
