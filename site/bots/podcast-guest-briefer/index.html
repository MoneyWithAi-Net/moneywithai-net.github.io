Podcast Guest Briefer

Description

Researches each podcast guest and writes a brief in Google Docs before recording.

What It Does

  • Researches each podcast guest.
  • Writes a brief in Google Docs before recording.
  • Works with Gmail and Google Docs.
  • Keeps you in charge: review its output before anything reaches a customer or goes public.

Who It's For

  • Podcasters
  • Small media teams
  • Companies with a show

How to Set It Up

Time to Set Up
About 15 to 30 minutes (our estimate)
Difficulty
Beginner
Tools Needed
  • Grok Bot: Grok Bot account needed
  • Gmail: Free with a Google account
  • Google Docs: Free plan available
  1. Read the creator's original post to confirm it fits your business.
  2. Rebuild it in Grok Bot or your own agent by following the creator's description.
  3. Connect Gmail and Google Docs when asked, using accounts you control.
  4. Give it your show, episode files or feeds and where you publish.
  5. Do a test run on a small sample and check every output by hand.
  6. Once results look right, set a schedule or trigger and keep an approval step for anything customer-facing.

Questions

Is Podcast Guest Briefer free to use?

It is free to add from the creator's listing, but you need a Grok Bot account. Connected tools such as Gmail and Google Docs may need their own plans.

What does it do with my episodes?

Typically transcripts, show notes, clips or promotion drafts. Check the source for the exact parts it covers.

How long does it take to set up Podcast Guest Briefer?

Our estimate is 15 to 30 minutes for a first working run, including connecting Gmail and Google Docs. Tuning it to your business takes a few more runs.
