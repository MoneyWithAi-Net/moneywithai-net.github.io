How to Set It Up

Time to Set Up
About 15 to 30 minutes (our estimate)
Difficulty
Beginner
Tools Needed
  • Grok Bot: Grok Bot account needed
  • X: Free account; the X API is pay per use
  1. Read the creator's original post to confirm it fits your business.
  2. Rebuild it in Grok Bot or your own agent by following the creator's description.
  3. Connect X when asked, using accounts you control.
  4. Give it your voice, topics you cover and examples of posts you like.
  5. Do a test run on a small sample and check every output by hand.
  6. Once results look right, set a schedule or trigger and keep an approval step for anything customer-facing.

  • Edit drafts so they sound like you.
  • Do not post on a schedule just to post; quality beats volume.
  • Check facts and links before publishing.

Questions

Is Account Growth Planner free to use?

It is free to add from the creator's listing, but you need a Grok Bot account. Connected tools such as X may need their own plans.

Can it post to X automatically?

Some setups can schedule posts once you approve them. Keep an approval step so nothing goes out you have not read.

How long does it take to set up Account Growth Planner?

Our estimate is 15 to 30 minutes for a first working run, including connecting X. Tuning it to your business takes a few more runs.
