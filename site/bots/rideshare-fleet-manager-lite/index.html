What it does

  • Reads the weekly Uber and Bolt exports you upload.
  • Builds one earnings summary per driver across both platforms.
  • Keeps a watchlist for insurance and licence expiry dates.
  • Upload-only: no portal logins and no payments or messages sent.

Who it's for

  • Operators who run a small fleet of rideshare cars
  • Fleet managers who track drivers on Uber and Bolt
