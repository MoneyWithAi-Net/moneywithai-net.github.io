What it does

  • Watches deals that change stage.
  • Checks them against your qualification rules.
  • Quotes evidence from transcripts.
  • Suggests CRM updates for approval.

Who it's for

  • Sales managers
  • Revenue operations teams
  • Founders who forecast

  4. 4Review the result and adjust your instructions.
Worked exampleA deal jumps to proposal

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. A rep moves a deal to proposal stage.
  2. Deal Inspector reads the last two call transcripts.
  3. It finds clear evidence of budget but no named decision maker.
  4. It quotes the budget line and flags the missing decision maker.
  5. The manager asks the rep to confirm who signs.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • Stage changes backed by real evidence.
  • Transcripts miss what was said off-call.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Write your qualification rules clearly.
  • Discuss flags with the rep.
  • Review weekly before forecasting.
