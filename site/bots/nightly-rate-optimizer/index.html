How to Set It Up

Time to Set Up
About 15 to 30 minutes (our estimate)
Difficulty
Beginner
Tools Needed
  • Grok Bot: Grok Bot account needed
  • StayingAPI: Check the vendor's pricing
  1. Read the creator's original page to confirm it fits your business.
  2. Rebuild it in Grok Bot or your own agent by following the creator's description.
  3. Connect StayingAPI when asked, using accounts you control.
  4. Give it your target areas, budget and the numbers you use to judge a deal.
  5. Do a test run on a small sample and check every output by hand.
  6. Once results look right, set a schedule or trigger and keep an approval step for anything customer-facing.

Questions

Is Nightly Rate Optimizer free to use?

It is free to add from the creator's listing, but you need a Grok Bot account. Connected tools such as StayingAPI may need their own plans.

Can it tell me if a property is a good deal?

It runs the numbers you set from public data. The decision and risk are yours.

How long does it take to set up Nightly Rate Optimizer?

Our estimate is 15 to 30 minutes for a first working run, including connecting StayingAPI. Tuning it to your business takes a few more runs.
