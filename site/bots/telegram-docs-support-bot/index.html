How to Set It Up

Time to Set Up
About 1 to 2 hours (our estimate)
Difficulty
Intermediate
Tools Needed
  • n8n: Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • OpenAI: Pay as you go (API billed per token)
  • Telegram: Free plan available
  1. Read the creator's original page to confirm it fits your business.
  2. Import the template into n8n, either self-hosted or on n8n Cloud.
  3. Connect OpenAI and Telegram when asked, using accounts you control.
  4. Give it your FAQs, documents and when to hand over to a person.
  5. Do a test run on a small sample and check every output by hand.
  6. Once results look right, set a schedule or trigger and keep an approval step for anything customer-facing.

Tips and Pitfalls

  • Keep answers grounded in your own documents.
  • Offer a human handover.
  • Templates can break when n8n or a connected app changes; re-test after updates.

Questions

Is the Telegram Docs Support Bot template free?

The template is free on n8n's gallery. n8n can be self-hosted for free or used on a paid n8n Cloud plan, and connected apps may charge for their own use.

Can it answer from my own documents?

Yes, most setups answer from the documents or sheets you connect. Keep them current.

How long does it take to set up Telegram Docs Support Bot?

Our estimate is 1 to 2 hours for a first working run, including connecting OpenAI and Telegram. Tuning it to your business takes a few more runs.
