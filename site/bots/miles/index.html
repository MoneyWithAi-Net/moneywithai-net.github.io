What it does

  • Scans four platforms twice a weekday.
  • Looks for buying signals.
  • Ranks people by intent.
  • Focuses on CRM and AI receptionist services.

Who it's for

  • GoHighLevel agencies
  • CRM consultants
  • AI voice agent sellers

Worked exampleA morning scan

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. At 9 am Miles scans the four platforms.
  2. It finds a dentist on Reddit asking for an AI receptionist.
  3. It finds a coach on LinkedIn unhappy with their CRM.
  4. It ranks the dentist first by intent.
  5. You reply to both with a helpful answer.
