Property Market Tracker

Tracks property listings and market signals with Bright Data and summarizes changes into Google Sheets.

What it does

  • Collects listing data through Bright Data.
  • Writes prices and locations to Google Sheets.
  • Summarizes changes with OpenAI.
  • Runs on the schedule you set.

Who it's for

  • Property investors tracking a market
  • Analysts who report on prices
  • Agents who want a weekly market note

Set it up 6 steps

  • n8n – Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • Google Sheets – Free with a Google account
  • OpenAI – Pay as you go (API billed per token)
  1. 1Open the original with Get it and copy or download the market tracker workflow.
  2. 2Import it into your n8n editor and add your own credentials for Bright Data, OpenAI and Google Sheets.
  3. 3Swap the sample IDs, addresses and text in the market tracker workflow for your own, and read each AI prompt node.
  4. 4Set the listing source, area and fields to collect in the Bright Data step.
  5. 5Run it once for a small area and check the rows and the summary.
  6. 6Switch the market tracker workflow on only after a clean test.
Worked exampleWatching one city's condos

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You set the workflow to track two-bedroom condos in one city.
  2. Bright Data collects the current listings each week.
  3. The workflow writes prices and locations to your sheet.
  4. OpenAI summarizes the week: more listings, a few price drops.
  5. You spot one building with several price cuts and look closer.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • A growing record of listings and changes in your market.
  • Scraped data can have gaps.

Good to know

  • Start with one area and property type.
  • Follow site terms and data rules.
  • Check unusual figures by hand.
