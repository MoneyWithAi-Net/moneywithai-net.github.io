Startup Finances Bot

Pulls startup finance data from Nuvio each morning and summarizes cash and what changed.

What it does

  • Reads your Nuvio data each morning.
  • Reports what moved and anything to act on.
  • Records income or costs you describe.
  • Never moves money.

Who it's for

  • Founders using Nuvio for their finances
  • Freelancers who want a morning money check
  • Small businesses logging expenses by chat

Set it up 5 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • NuvioNuvio – Check the vendor's pricing
  1. 1Read the creator's post with Get it to see how they run it.
  2. 2In Grok Bot, create a new bot whose one-line job is to brief me each morning on what changed in my finances and log income or expenses when I ask.
  3. 3Write its instructions in your own words, covering the time of the briefing, what counts as a change worth mentioning, how to log a transaction you describe, and that it never moves money.
  4. 4Connect Nuvio with your own account as its documentation describes.
  5. 5Ask for today's briefing and check it against Nuvio.
Worked exampleA founder's morning

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. At 8 am the bot reads yesterday's changes in Nuvio.
  2. It reports a customer payment arrived and a card charge is unexplained.
  3. You tell it the charge was a conference ticket.
  4. It logs the expense in the right category.
  5. You move on with your day.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • A short daily note on what moved and what to act on.
  • It is only as current as your Nuvio data.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Choose a fixed briefing time.
  • Describe unexplained charges as soon as you see them.
  • Review its logged entries weekly.
