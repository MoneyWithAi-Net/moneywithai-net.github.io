Questions

Is Speed-to-Lead Closer free to use?

It is free to add from the creator's listing, but you need a Grok Bot account. Connected tools such as Stripe may need their own plans.

Can it send quotes on its own?

It drafts quotes and first replies from your price rules. Keep a human approval step for anything unusual or high value.

How long does it take to set up Speed-to-Lead Closer?

Our estimate is 15 to 30 minutes for a first working run, including connecting Stripe. Tuning it to your business takes a few more runs.
