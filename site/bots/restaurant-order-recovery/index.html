What it does

  • Checks your delivery merchant portals daily.
  • Finds canceled orders and error charges.
  • Submits reimbursement requests where allowed.
  • Sends a digest only when there is news.

Who it's for

  • Restaurants selling through delivery apps
  • Multi-location owners checking many portals
  • Managers short on time for disputes

Set it up 5 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  1. 1Read the original and check it fits.
  2. 2Add it from the share link.
  3. 3Give it the accounts or orders to check and your rules for chasing money owed.
  4. 4Test on a small sample.
  5. 5Schedule only after it looks right; keep approval for customer-facing sends.
Worked exampleA week of delivery errors

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You connect the bot to your delivery merchant portals.
  2. It finds an order canceled after the food was made and a wrong error charge.
  3. It files a reimbursement request for each.
  4. Your daily digest lists both requests.
  5. You check the portal later in the week to see which were approved.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • Canceled orders and wrong charges claimed instead of written off.
  • Each platform decides which claims it pays.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Check each platform's rules before automating claims.
  • Read the daily digest each morning.
  • Keep order records in case a claim is questioned.

Yes, where the platform allows. Every request appears in the daily digest.
