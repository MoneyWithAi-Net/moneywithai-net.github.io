Original template: AI-Generated LinkedIn Posts with OpenAI, Google Sheets & Email Approval Workflow by Aryan Shinde

What it does

  • Reads topics from a sheet.
  • Writes posts with OpenAI.
  • Emails drafts for approval.
  • Posts after approval.

Who it's for

  • Founders
  • Consultants
  • Small brands on LinkedIn

Set it up 6 steps

  • n8n – Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • Gmail – Free with a Google account
  • Google Sheets – Free with a Google account
  • LinkedIn – Check the vendor's pricing
  • OpenAI – Pay as you go (API billed per token)
  1. 1Open the original with Get it and copy or download the LinkedIn post workflow.
  2. 2Import it into your n8n editor and add your own credentials for Google Sheets, Gmail, LinkedIn and OpenAI.
  3. 3Swap the sample IDs, addresses and text in the LinkedIn post workflow for your own, and read each AI prompt node.
  4. 4Set up the sheet with the topic and status columns the workflow reads.
  5. 5Run it once and reject the first draft to check nothing posts.
  6. 6Switch the LinkedIn post workflow on only after a clean test.
Worked exampleA weekly post for a consultant

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You add five topics to the sheet.
  2. On Tuesday the workflow picks the next one.
  3. It writes a post about pricing lessons.
  4. It emails you the draft with an approve option.
  5. You approve it, and it posts and marks the row done.
