Original template: AI Customer Support Assistant · WhatsApp Ready · Works for Any Business by Matt F.

What it does

  • Answers WhatsApp messages.
  • Reads your website.
  • Remembers each chat.
  • Writes replies with OpenAI.

Who it's for

  • Small businesses on WhatsApp
  • Shops with common questions
  • Service businesses

Set it up 6 steps

  • n8n – Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • OpenAI – Pay as you go (API billed per token)
  • WhatsApp – Pay per message on the Business Platform; service replies are free
  1. 1Open the original with Get it and copy or download the help desk workflow.
  2. 2Import it into your n8n editor and add your own credentials for WhatsApp Business and OpenAI.
  3. 3Swap the sample IDs, addresses and text in the help desk workflow for your own, and read each AI prompt node.
  4. 4Set your website address as the source it reads.
  5. 5Message the number with a common question and check the answer.
  6. 6Switch the help desk workflow on only after a clean test.
Worked exampleA question about delivery

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. A customer asks on WhatsApp whether you deliver on Sundays.
  2. The workflow reads your delivery policy page.
  3. It replies that you deliver Monday to Saturday.
  4. The customer asks about a specific product.
  5. It answers using the product page and remembers the chat.

Good to know

  • Keep your site current.
  • Offer a human option.
  • Review chats weekly.
