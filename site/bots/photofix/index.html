Edits real estate listing photos, labels them and files them by address in Drive or Dropbox for client delivery.

What it does

  • Takes in a batch of listing photos.
  • Applies honest edits.
  • Labels each photo.
  • Files them by address in Drive or Dropbox.

Who it's for

  • Real estate photographers
  • Agents who shoot their own listings
  • Rental hosts refreshing their photos

Set it up 6 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • Google Drive – Free with a Google account (15 GB); more storage is paid
  • Dropbox – Free plan available
  1. 1Read the original and check it fits.
  2. 2Add it from the share link.
  3. 3Connect Google Drive and Dropbox.
  4. 4Give it the deliverable, the client's brief and your quality checklist.
  5. 5Test on a small sample.
  6. 6Schedule only after it looks right; keep approval for customer-facing sends.
Worked exampleA shoot at a three-bedroom house

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You upload forty photos from the morning's shoot.
  2. PhotoFix brightens the interiors and straightens the vertical lines.
  3. It labels each photo by room.
  4. It files them in a folder named by address in Dropbox.
  5. You share the folder link with the agent that afternoon.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • Edited, labeled photos delivered by address the same day.
  • Some shots will still need manual edits.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Shoot well; edits cannot fix everything.
  • Keep the original files.
  • Check edited photos are true to the property.
