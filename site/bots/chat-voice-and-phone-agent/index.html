Description

A chat, voice and phone agent built on Voiceflow that answers from your documents and books on Google Calendar.

What It Does

  • A chat, voice and phone agent built on Voiceflow that answers from your documents.
  • Books on Google Calendar.
  • Works with Google Calendar, Google Drive, OpenAI and Qdrant.
  • Keeps you in charge: review its output before anything reaches a customer or goes public.

Who It's For

  • Clinics and salons
  • Trades and home services
  • Restaurants and local shops

How to Set It Up

Time to Set Up
About 2 to 4 hours (our estimate)
Difficulty
Advanced
Tools Needed
  • n8n: Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • Google Calendar: Free with a Google account
  • Google Drive: Free with a Google account (15 GB); more storage is paid
  • OpenAI: Pay as you go (API billed per token)
  • Qdrant: Free plan available
  1. Read the creator's original page to confirm it fits your business.
  2. Import the template into n8n, either self-hosted or on n8n Cloud.
  3. Connect Google Calendar, Google Drive, OpenAI and Qdrant when asked, using accounts you control.
  4. Give it your hours, services, booking rules and when to transfer to a person.
  5. Do a test run on a small sample and check every output by hand.
  6. Once results look right, set a schedule or trigger and keep an approval step for anything customer-facing.

Tips and Pitfalls

  • Tell callers they are speaking with an AI where required.
  • Test with real call scenarios first.
  • Templates can break when n8n or a connected app changes; re-test after updates.

Questions

Is the Chat Voice and Phone Agent template free?

The template is free on n8n's gallery. n8n can be self-hosted for free or used on a paid n8n Cloud plan, and connected apps may charge for their own use.

Will callers know it is an AI?

You should tell them where the law requires it, and it is good practice anyway.

How long does it take to set up Chat Voice and Phone Agent?

Our estimate is 2 to 4 hours for a first working run, including connecting Google Calendar, Google Drive and OpenAI. Tuning it to your business takes a few more runs.
