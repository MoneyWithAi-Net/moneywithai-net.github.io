Finds live funding programmes for a company or client project and prepares the pack a person files.

What It Does

  • Finds live funding programmes for a company or client project.
  • Prepares the pack a person files.
  • Keeps you in charge: review its output before anything reaches a customer or goes public.

Who It's For

  • Grant writers
  • Nonprofit consultants
  • Small charities and social enterprises

How to Set It Up

Time to Set Up
About 30 to 60 minutes (our estimate)
Difficulty
Beginner
Tools Needed
  • Grok Bot: Grok Bot account needed
  1. Read the creator's original post to confirm it fits your business.
  2. Add it to Grok Bot from the official share link.
  3. Give it the funder's guidance, your organisation's facts and past applications.
  4. Do a test run on a small sample and check every output by hand.
  5. Once results look right, set a schedule or trigger and keep an approval step for anything customer-facing.

Does it write the whole grant application?

It drafts sections from your facts and the funder's guidance. A person must check and submit it.
