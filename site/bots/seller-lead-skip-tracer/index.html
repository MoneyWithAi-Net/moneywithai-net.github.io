What it does

  • Searches properties through BatchData with your filters.
  • Scores leads on absentee ownership, years owned, equity and tax status.
  • Skip traces owner contact details.
  • Pushes qualified leads into HubSpot.

Who it's for

  • Real estate investors looking for off-market deals
  • Wholesalers building seller lists
  • Agents prospecting for listings

Set it up 5 steps

  • n8n – Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • HubSpot – Free CRM available; paid tiers
  1. 1Open the original with Get it and copy or download the BatchData skip tracing workflow.
  2. 2Import it into your n8n editor and add your own credentials for BatchData and HubSpot.
  3. 3Swap the sample IDs, addresses and text in the BatchData skip tracing workflow for your own, and read each AI prompt node.
  4. 4Run one narrow search with a small limit and check the scores and contacts before adding more.
  5. 5Switch the BatchData skip tracing workflow on only after a clean test.
Worked exampleAbsentee owners in one zip code

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You set the search to single-family homes in one zip code with high equity.
  2. The workflow scores each property and keeps those with absentee owners.
  3. It looks up owner contact details for the top-scoring leads.
  4. It adds them to HubSpot with the property details.
  5. You check the do-not-call list before anyone is called.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • Scored seller leads with contact details in HubSpot.
  • Many owners will not want to sell; scoring helps you prioritize.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Check do-not-call lists before calling.
  • Start with a small search to control data costs.
  • Write a respectful first message.

Ask a question first

Questions

What is skip tracing?

Looking up contact details, such as phone, email and mailing address, for a property owner.

Where does the property data come from?

From BatchData's property search, using the filters you set.

Is Seller Lead Skip Tracer free to use?

The template is free. BatchData is a paid service and HubSpot and n8n have their own plans.

Can I call these owners right away?

Check consent rules and do-not-call lists first. This is not legal advice.

How long does it take to set up Seller Lead Skip Tracer?

Our estimate is 1 to 2 hours, including BatchData and HubSpot setup.
