What it does

  • Reads a 2D drawing such as a floor plan, site plan or part profile.
  • Builds a 3D model you can open, rotate and use.
  • Makes a preview image you can share or post.
  • Gives approximate quantities to help early estimates.

Who it's for

  • Home builders who sell from plans
  • Remodelers and architects showing clients a layout
  • Makers working from part drawings

Set it up 5 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • A clear 2D drawingA clear 2D drawing – PDF or image of the plan

Worked exampleShowing a buyer a plan they cannot picture

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. A buyer is unsure about an open-plan layout from your standard home range.
  2. You upload the clean PDF floor plan for that home.
  3. Plan to 3D returns a model and a preview image of the ground floor.
  4. You compare room sizes with the drawing and correct one wall the bot misread.
  5. You send the buyer the preview, labelled as an illustration, not a construction document.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • Clients and buyers can see a layout in 3D instead of reading a flat plan. Quality depends on how clear the drawing is.
  • Quantities are rough and not a substitute for a takeoff.

Good to know

  • Use quantities only as a starting point; measure before you order.
  • Label the model as a preview, not a construction document.
  • Share plans only when you have the rights to them.

Ask a question first

Questions

Can I build from the Plan to 3D model?

No. Treat it as a preview. Build only from your approved, stamped drawings.

Is Plan to 3D free to use?

It is free to add from the share link, but you need a Grok Bot account.

Which drawings work best?

Clear, labelled 2D drawings such as floor plans, site plans and part profiles. Blurry photos or hand sketches give weaker models.

How accurate are the quantities?

They are approximate and meant for early estimates. Measure and do a proper takeoff before you order anything.

How long does it take to set up Plan to 3D?

Our estimate is about 30 minutes for a first plan, including checking the model against the drawing.
