X Account Crew

Runs an X account as a crew of five agents covering ideas, drafting, editing, posting and review.

What it does

  • Finds ideas.
  • Drafts and edits posts.
  • Queues approved posts.
  • Reviews results.

Who it's for

  • Founders
  • Creators on X
  • Small brands

Set it up 5 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • X – Free account; the X API is pay per use
  • Hacker News – Free plan available
  • GitHub – Free plan available
  • Reddit – Free plan available
  1. 1Read the creator's post with Get it to see how they run it.
  2. 2In Grok Bot, create a new bot whose one-line job is to run my X account as five roles: ideas, drafting, editing, posting and review, with my approval before posting.
  3. 3Write its instructions in your own words, covering your topics, your voice, the role of each agent, how many posts per day and that nothing posts without your OK.
  4. 4Connect your X account, and allow it to read Hacker News, GitHub and Reddit.
  5. 5Run one day of drafts and read them all.
Worked exampleA day for a developer account

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. The idea agent finds a popular GitHub project.
  2. The drafting agent writes a post about it.
  3. The editing agent tightens the hook.
  4. You approve it, and the posting agent queues it.
  5. The review agent reports how it did that evening.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • A steady, reviewed posting routine.
  • Growth is never guaranteed.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Approve every post.
  • Follow X's rules.
  • Keep your voice.
