What It Does

  • Turns a customer call into follow-up slides covering what you heard and next steps, in Figma or Google Slides.
  • Fits into sales-call support work for b2b sales teams.
  • Keeps you in charge: review its output before anything reaches a customer or goes public.

Who It's For

  • B2B sales teams
  • Founders who run their own sales calls
  • Agencies that pitch often

How to Set It Up

Time to Set Up
About 15 to 30 minutes (our estimate)
Difficulty
Beginner
Tools Needed
  • Grok Bot: Grok Bot account needed
  1. Read the creator's original post to confirm it fits your business.
  2. Add it to Grok Bot from the official share link.
  3. Give it your product facts, pricing rules and the CRM or notes you keep.
  4. Do a test run on a small sample and check every output by hand.
  5. Once results look right, set a schedule or trigger and keep an approval step for anything customer-facing.

Tips and Pitfalls

  • Feed it accurate product facts; it repeats what it is given.
  • Get consent before recording any call.
  • Review follow-ups before they go to a prospect.

Questions

Is Echo free to use?

It is free to add from the creator's listing, but you need a Grok Bot account.

Does it join my calls?

Depending on the setup it prepares notes, answers questions or drafts follow-ups. Check the creator's source for exactly which parts it covers.

How long does it take to set up Echo?

Our estimate is 15 to 30 minutes for a first working run. Tuning it to your business takes a few more runs.
