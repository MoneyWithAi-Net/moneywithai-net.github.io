Quote Follow Up Sequence

Sends quote follow-up emails on day 3, 7 and 14 from a Google Sheet until the customer replies.

What it does

  • Reads open quotes from a Google Sheet every morning.
  • Works out days since each quote was sent.
  • Sends the day 3, 7 or 14 follow-up from Gmail.
  • Writes the sent date back so no step repeats.

Who it's for

  • Roofers, painters and other contractors who send many quotes
  • Local service firms without a sales person
  • Owners who forget follow-ups in busy weeks

Set it up 6 steps

  • n8n – Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • Gmail – Free with a Google account
  • Google Sheets – Free with a Google account
  1. 1Open the original with Get it and copy or download the quote follow-up workflow.
  2. 2Import it into your n8n editor and add your own credentials for Gmail and Google Sheets.
  3. 3Swap the sample IDs, addresses and text in the quote follow-up workflow for your own, and read each AI prompt node.
  4. 4Set up your sheet with the columns the workflow reads, such as customer email, sent date and status, and write your three follow-up emails.
  5. 5Add one test quote with your own email and a sent date three days ago, run the workflow by hand and check the email and the sheet.
  6. 6Switch the quote follow-up workflow on only after a clean test.
Worked exampleA roofer's open quotes

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You list each quote in the sheet with the customer email, sent date and status open.
  2. On day 3 the workflow sends a short check-in from your Gmail.
  3. A customer replies on day 5 and you change their status to won.
  4. Another customer gets the day 7 and day 14 emails, then the sequence ends.
  5. You review the sheet weekly to see which quotes are still open.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • Every open quote gets three timely follow-ups without you tracking dates.
  • Replies still need you; the workflow stops at sending.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Keep each email short and end with one clear question.
  • Update the status column the moment a quote is won or lost.
  • Send the first test to yourself.

Questions

How does it know when to stop?

It only emails quotes whose status is open, and it records each sent date so it never repeats a step.

Can I change the 3, 7 and 14 day timing?

Yes. The day offsets and the email text are set in the workflow's configuration.

Do I need to self-host n8n?

No. It runs on n8n Cloud or on a self-hosted n8n.

Is Quote Follow Up Sequence free to use?

The template is free. Your cost depends on how you run n8n.

How long does it take to set up Quote Follow Up Sequence?

Our estimate is 30 to 60 minutes, including a test with your own email address.
