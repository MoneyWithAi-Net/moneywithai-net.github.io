What it does

  • Reads leads from Google Sheets.
  • Checks each lead's time zone.
  • Calls them with a Retell AI voice agent.
  • Writes the outcome back to the sheet.

Who it's for

  • Agencies calling many new leads
  • Teams following up on event sign-ups
  • Businesses sending payment reminder calls

Set it up 6 steps

  • n8n – Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • Google Sheets – Free with a Google account
  1. 1Open the original with Get it and copy or download the voice caller workflow.
  2. 2Import it into your n8n editor and add your own credentials for Google Sheets and Retell AI.
  3. 3Swap the sample IDs, addresses and text in the voice caller workflow for your own, and read each AI prompt node.
  4. 4Set up your Retell AI agent and script, and add the sheet columns the workflow expects.
  5. 5Put only your own phone number in the sheet and take the test call.
  6. 6Switch the voice caller workflow on only after a clean test.
Worked exampleFollowing up on webinar sign-ups

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. Forty webinar sign-ups who agreed to a call are in your sheet.
  2. The workflow checks each lead's local time.
  3. The voice agent calls those in business hours and asks about booking a demo.
  4. It records booked, not interested or no answer in the sheet.
  5. You follow up personally with the booked leads.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • Every lead called at a sensible hour, with outcomes logged.
  • Many calls will go unanswered.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Only call people who agreed to it.
  • Disclose that the caller is an AI.
  • Test the script on yourself first.
