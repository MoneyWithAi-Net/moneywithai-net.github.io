Set it up 4 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • ConverlyConverly – Check the vendor's pricing
  1. 1In Grok Bot, create a new bot whose one-line job is to guide me step by step through setting up Converly conversion tracking on my website and checking that leads reach my ad accounts.
  2. 2Write its instructions in your own words, covering which lead types you have, which ad and analytics tools you use, the order of steps and a test at the end of each step.
  3. 3Have your Converly login and your Google Ads, Meta or analytics access ready, since you make the changes yourself.
  4. 4Submit one test lead and confirm it appears in each connected ad account before adding more flows.
Worked exampleTracking a contact form

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You tell the bot you run Google Ads and use a website contact form.
  2. It explains where to paste the Converly snippet on your site.
  3. It helps you build a flow that fires when the form is submitted.
  4. You send a test entry and it tells you where to look in Google Ads.
  5. Once the test shows up, you add a second flow for bookings.
