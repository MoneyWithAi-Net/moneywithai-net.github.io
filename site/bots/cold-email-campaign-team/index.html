What it does

  • Takes a campaign brief.
  • Uses a director model to plan and assign work.
  • Runs specialist models for research, writing and sequencing.
  • Returns a campaign ready for your edits.

Who it's for

  • Agencies planning client outbound
  • Consultants launching a new offer
  • Founders writing their first campaign

Set it up 6 steps

  • n8n – Free to self-host; n8n Cloud is paid
  • OpenAI – Pay as you go (API billed per token)
  1. 1Open the original with Get it and copy or download the cold email campaign workflow.
  2. 2Import it into your n8n editor and add your own credentials for OpenAI.
  3. 3Swap the sample IDs, addresses and text in the cold email campaign workflow for your own, and read each AI prompt node.
  4. 4Write the brief input with your offer, audience and goal, and check which model each role uses.
  5. 5Run it with a brief for an offer you know well and read every output.
  6. 6Switch the cold email campaign workflow on only after a clean test.
Worked examplePlanning a campaign for a design studio

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. You brief the workflow: brand design for funded startups.
  2. The director sets the angle and assigns the specialists.
  3. The research and writing roles draft a four-email sequence.
  4. The deliverability role adds sending tips.
  5. You edit the emails and load them into your email tool.

Ask a question first

Questions

How does the multi-agent setup work?

A director model plans the work and assigns parts to specialist models, then combines their results.

Does Cold Email Campaign Team send emails?

No. It produces the campaign; you send from your own tool.

What do I give it?

A brief describing your offer, audience and goal.

Is Cold Email Campaign Team free?

The workflow itself is free. Running several models per campaign uses OpenAI credit, and n8n Cloud is a paid option.

How long does it take to set up Cold Email Campaign Team?

Plan on 1 to 2 hours by our estimate, including checking which model each role uses.
