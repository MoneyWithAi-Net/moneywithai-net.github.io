Evergreen Content Flywheel

Finds strong evergreen posts in FeedHive each week and queues them for reuse.

Set it up with the steps on this page.

What it does

  • Reviews past posts.
  • Picks evergreen ones.
  • Queues reposts.
  • Reports in Slack.

Who it's for

  • Small brands
  • Social media freelancers
  • Agencies

Set it up 4 steps

  • Grok Bot – Grok Bot account needed
  • FeedHive – Check the vendor's pricing
  • Notion – Free plan available
  • Slack – Free plan available
  1. 1In Grok Bot, create a new bot whose one-line job is to find my strongest evergreen posts in FeedHive each week and queue them to be shared again.
  2. 2Write its instructions in your own words, covering how to judge a post as evergreen, what to skip, how long to wait before a repeat and how many to queue each week.
  3. 3Connect FeedHive, Notion for the reuse log and Slack for the weekly note.
  4. 4Run it once and review its picks before anything is queued.
Worked exampleRecycling a how-to post

Example only: an illustration of how a session could go, not a real client, case or result.

  1. The bot scans posts from the last year in FeedHive.
  2. It finds a how-to post that did well last spring.
  3. It checks Notion and sees it has not been reused yet.
  4. It queues the post with a fresh opening line.
  5. Slack shows you the three posts it queued this week.

What to expect Our rough estimate of value to the business, not a promise or income claim

  • Older posts working again.
  • Outdated posts must be skipped.

Our estimate, not a promise or a typical result.

Good to know

  • Skip dated posts.
  • Refresh openings.
  • Space out repeats.
